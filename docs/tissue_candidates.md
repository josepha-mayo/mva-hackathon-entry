# Preserve tissue function through a measured deficit

Public-source research nominations, 23 October 2026. These are independent
experiments, not a combination or evidence that the child has the modeled
deficits. No candidate has demonstrated exact-genotype drug rescue here.

## Fasudil and belumosudil: qualify ciliary function separately

Prior MVA work establishes a reason to investigate cilia, but its genetic and
inhibitor interventions are not ROCK-drug rescue. In a separate 2025 study,
fasudil increased ciliation in some other genetic models, with no rescue in
the tested TMEM67-deficient model. Hydroxyfasudil, an active metabolite,
increased a Hedgehog reporter; the deficient RPGRIP1L model already had
elevated agonist-induced signaling. A further increase is not necessarily
normalization. Belumosudil's principal organoid/cyst results are distinct
structural endpoints and cannot inherit the hydroxyfasudil signaling result.
[MVA ciliary mechanism](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2015.01.003),
[ROCK study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s43856-025-00847-1)

The useful experiment asks whether a demonstrated BUB1B-associated defect
responds with regulated stimulus-dependent signaling and successful return
to the cell cycle. Cilia count alone is insufficient. Track cytokinesis,
division and daughter outcomes in cycling cells, and measure each compound's
exposure. Higher-dose fasudil mitotic toxicity in other models motivates
looking for a usable window; it does not prove low-exposure MVA toxicity or
the same risk for every ROCK inhibitor.
[Fasudil checkpoint study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3958508/)

Belumosudil has a US oral cGVHD product; fasudil has a Japanese intravenous
post-subarachnoid-hemorrhage product. These establish different medicine
nominations and formulation histories, not chronic pediatric MVA suitability.
Hydroxyfasudil is retained as a metabolite comparator, not a separately verified
marketed medicine. [REZUROCK label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=102e4ef4-7f84-4e34-8df1-479c24d1575d),
[Eril label](https://www.pmda.go.jp/PmdaSearch/iyakuDetail/100898_2190414A1033_1_07)

## Cilostazol: a barrier-function question with a specific missing bridge

Low-BubR1 mice showed a barrier-permeability defect at five months; no significant difference was detected at one
month. A separate rat endothelial study found cilostazol-associated increases
in resistance and decreases in tracer permeability, including addition during
reoxygenation after oxygen/glucose deprivation. Neither experiment establishes
cilostazol rescue of a BUB1B-deficient barrier.
[BubR1 barrier study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26883501/),
[cilostazol barrier study](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10571-012-9896-1)

First establish a reproducible deficit in a qualified human barrier model.
Test permeability and resistance together with viable coverage, geometry and
cell composition. Measure parent/metabolite exposure and cAMP response;
separate actual barrier restoration from assay interference or a changed
number of cells. Useful recovery from a measured deficit can be considered
without claiming that past chromosome errors have been reversed.

The luminal endothelial target is accessible from blood, so direct brain
penetration is not a necessary premise for this specific hypothesis. It would
be required for a separate direct neural/glial claim. Current labeling records
high binding, active metabolites, a heart-failure contraindication and
unestablished pediatric safety/effectiveness. These are candidate boundaries,
not assertions about this child. [Cilostazol label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=db0278e9-4ece-4aaf-9216-6bcbec58206a)

## How to choose the experiment

Select the module using an established model phenotype and available material,
not an invented cross-study efficacy score. The [finite pilot](finite_pilot.md)
keeps one qualified bypass module independently eligible alongside the
ataluren feasibility arm. NAC remains a redox comparator. A result in a
different gene, age or injury state motivates a test; it does not establish a
medicine response in MVA or justify treatment.
