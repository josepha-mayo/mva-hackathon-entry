# Preserve tissue function through a measured deficit

Public-source adversarial review, 23 October 2026. **Cilostazol and fasudil
remain conditional experiments; neither is an established treatment lead.**
Belumosudil moves to a reserve confirmation role. No exact-genotype drug
rescue, child-specific tissue deficit or comparative clinical efficacy is
established. The new evidence changes the experiments, not just the wording.

## Cilostazol: test the parent–metabolite response without hiding cAMP support

Low-BubR1 mice had increased barrier permeability at five months, with no
significant difference at one month. A separate rat endothelial experiment
found functional benefit with nominal 1 micromolar cilostazol after 96 hours,
and during three hours of reoxygenation after six hours of oxygen/glucose
deprivation. Those are different schedules and neither is a BUB1B drug-rescue
experiment. The medium included plasma-derived serum and hydrocortisone.
[Disease-axis study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26883501/),
[Horai 2013](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10571-012-9896-1)

The human endothelial positive is materially different: passaged cells,
nominal 30 micromolar cilostazol, and **50 micromolar forskolin added to every
preparation**. Electrical resistance was measured at 12 hours and tracer
permeability after three days. This supports an incremental PDE-inhibition
effect under stimulated cAMP production, not unconditioned replication of
the rat result at 1 micromolar. A PDE inhibitor may need sufficient cAMP
production; neither deficient production nor excess PDE3 activity has been
demonstrated in this child's cells. [Liu 2012](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3504123/)

Clinical counterevidence is retained. COMCID analyzed 159 older adults with
mild cognitive impairment and found no MMSE benefit over 96 weeks. DREAM
randomized 120 adults with small-vessel disease and found no benefit on its
two-year white-matter-hyperintensity volume endpoint or secondary functional
outcomes. Its abstract says 109 analyzed (55/54), while Results/Table 1 give
107 (53/54); outcome-specific available counts are smaller. We preserve this
discrepancy. These diseases are not MVA, and the negatives do not exclude a
demonstrable BUB1B barrier response. LACI-2's exploratory dependence signal
also remains a positive, not confirmatory efficacy or independent MVA evidence.
[COMCID](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2023.44938),
[DREAM](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9793825/),
[LACI-2](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamaneurol.2023.1526)

The 2026 six-month LACI-2 analysis also reported a nominal cognitive positive;
it is the same cohort, not independent replication. CSPS-2 supports secondary
stroke prevention in Japanese adults, a separate clinical benefit that does
not establish BBB repair or developmental recovery in MVA.
[Six-month analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42535538/),
[CSPS-2](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20833591/)

**Five-condition proposal:** deficient vehicle; deficient parent alone;
deficient parent plus its two active metabolites at the same parent exposure;
corrected vehicle; corrected mixture. This tests metabolite contribution and
generic barrier enhancement without adding conditions. It replaces the old
two-cilostazol/one-ataluren barrier layout. One exposure mixture cannot
establish a dose-response curve or rule out the medicine globally.

Use a qualified barrier model and record serum, hydrocortisone and other
conditioning factors. Omit added forskolin during the inference phase if
barrier competence permits. If removing it destroys the assay, report a
conditioned result and hold the claim of standalone rescue. Measure cAMP,
tracer permeability, resistance and viable coverage. A larger cAMP signal is
target response, not restored barrier function. The corrected-mixture arm
can reveal generic enhancement, but a ceiling can hide that response.

Measure free parent/metabolites in the actual medium. Adult total peaks are
not child-specific exposure limits; individual metabolite and parent peaks
need not occur together. Use a matched-time profile or label an unmatched
mixture as an exposure sensitivity experiment. Parent plus its metabolites
models one medicine's circulating analytes; it is not a proposed clinical
drug combination. The label records high binding, active metabolites, a
heart-failure contraindication and unestablished pediatric safety/effectiveness.
Limited pediatric clinical use elsewhere does not establish MVA benefit.
The blood-facing endothelial target does not require a direct neuronal
penetration premise. [Cilostazol label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=db0278e9-4ece-4aaf-9216-6bcbec58206a)

## Fasudil: separate parent from metabolite and resolve function at exposure

MVA-model cilia work supplies a biological rationale, not ROCK-drug rescue.
Smith 2025 found ciliation improvements in some other genetic models, with
TMEM67 nonresponse. Crucially, its Figure 3g plot and Supplemental Data 4
say **fasudil**, while the caption and luciferase Methods say
**hydroxyfasudil**, at 10 micromolar. Compound identity for that Gli result
is unresolved; our earlier unqualified metabolite attribution is withdrawn.
RPGRIP1L cells already had elevated agonist-induced Gli signaling, so a
further increase does not necessarily normalize it. Belumosudil's structural
and organoid/cyst findings cannot inherit that reporter result.
[Miyamoto 2015](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2015.01.003),
[Smith 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s43856-025-00847-1)

Separately identified 3-micromolar hydroxyfasudil ciliation positives support
including the metabolite even without resolving the Gli identity conflict.
The study's 5-micromolar metabolite DNA-content result did not show a significant
cell-cycle redistribution; that does not establish cytokinesis or re-entry safety.

Human exposure is a separate hurdle. In the 14-adult SAFE-ROCK study,
approximate total parent peaks were 0.0048 micromolar orally and 0.345
micromolar intravenously; hydroxyfasudil peaks were about 0.36 micromolar.
These are study-specific total-plasma values, not unbound culture, brain or
pediatric exposures. ROCK-ALS measured CSF hydroxyfasudil around 0.019–0.031
micromolar in its groups, with parent almost undetectable. Its primary safety
result and exploratory transient MUNIX signal do not establish clinical
neurologic efficacy; major clinical outcomes did not significantly improve.
The trial was not powered for efficacy and those analyses lacked multiplicity
correction. CSF samples are not established peaks or free brain-cell exposure.
The 1 micromolar parent ciliation examples and disputed 10 micromolar Gli
experiment therefore cannot be declared clinically exposure-matched.
[SAFE-ROCK](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40263-024-01070-7),
[ROCK-ALS](https://doi.org/10.1016/S1474-4422(24)00373-9)

A separate 160-person acute ischemic-stroke trial reported short-term
neurological and one-month outcome positives. This preserves a human
functional signal without assigning it to a ciliary mechanism or congenital MVA.
[Primary stroke trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16005902/)

**Six-condition proposal:** deficient vehicle, two fasudil conditions, two
hydroxyfasudil conditions and corrected vehicle, each with stimulus-present
and stimulus-absent siblings. Replace the two first-round belumosudil slots;
do not enlarge the experiment. Require endogenous, regulated signaling
toward the corrective range, with basal activity shown separately, followed
by competent re-entry/cytokinesis and daughter outcomes in cycling cells.
Record medium binding and actual exposure in both quiescent and cycling
states. No direct clinical concentration or dose is prescribed.

Belumosudil remains a possible orthogonal confirmation after a hit, conditional
on its own exposure bridge. A human hepatic-impairment study derived a normal-
hepatic-group geometric-mean unbound peak of 0.444 ng/mL (about 1 nM; n=8,
single adult dose) using measured individual binding fractions, with mean
unbound fraction 0.0295%. This was not a directly sampled free peak. It does not define
steady-state cGVHD, brain or pediatric exposure, but prevents equating a total
plasma peak with the 0.5–5 micromolar low-serum cilia experiments.
[Measured unbound study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12227017/)

Fasudil's Japanese IV formulation and belumosudil's US oral cGVHD product
establish different medicine histories, not chronic pediatric MVA suitability.
Hydroxyfasudil is an active-metabolite reference, not a separately verified
marketed medicine. High-exposure cytokinesis findings in other deficient
models justify checking the window, not assuming toxicity at every exposure.
[Eril label](https://www.pmda.go.jp/PmdaSearch/iyakuDetail/100898_2190414A1033_1_07),
[REZUROCK label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=102e4ef4-7f84-4e34-8df1-479c24d1575d),
[BRCA2 counterstudy](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.80254)

## Choose a relevant function before declaring a winner

Select one module through available appropriate material and an informative
phenotype, as specified in the [finite pilot](finite_pilot.md). A concurrent
controlled pilot may establish phenotype and response together. Tissue and
developmental state matter: a separate cortical BubR1-loss model showed no
obvious embryonic primary-cilium morphology defect, while later ependymal
development was abnormal. Gross morphology is not a signaling test, but it
prevents universalizing the fibroblast cilia story to every neural state.
[Brain-model study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6522074/)

Selumetinib is [deferred](alternative_challenge.md) after direct-drug
counterevidence. This does not improve the evidence for ataluren or NAC.
Ataluren remains a limited feasibility arm; NAC remains a protective
comparator. No cross-study numerical score or cure claim is justified.
