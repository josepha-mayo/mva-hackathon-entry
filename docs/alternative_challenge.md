# What the broader drug search changes

Public-source adversarial review, 23 October 2026. No additional approved
medicine with demonstrated functional rescue of the proposed inherited BUB1B
genotype was identified in this bounded search. That is not proof of absence
or evidence that ataluren is best. The main [decision](drug_decision.md) and
[finite pilot](finite_pilot.md) retain independent functional hypotheses.

## Selumetinib: a conditional extension, with an untested drug substitution

In RAS/MEK-transformed ARPE19 cells depleted of BUBR1, research MEK inhibitor
U0126 improved chromosome alignment from below 50% to above 90%. The experiment
used 10 micromolar U0126 and MG132 prevented mitotic exit. Untransformed
BUBR1-depleted cells already aligned above 90%. Separate reduced-lagging and
micronucleus experiments retained endogenous BUBR1. Thus the source supports
an excessive-MAPK/attachment hypothesis, not completed faithful divisions in
inherited BUB1B deficiency. [Primary PNAS study](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2208255119)

Selumetinib is a marketed MEK inhibitor, but it did not produce that published
rescue. A small conditional comparison can ask whether an approved drug
reproduces the useful effect in a model with excessive mitotic MAPK/Hec1
activity. U0126 remains a research reference; its concentration cannot be
assigned to selumetinib. Require unblocked segregation, completed cytokinesis
and useful descendants, not alignment only during arrest. No such abnormal
MAPK state is assumed in the child. The product label's high-exposure mouse
aneugenicity result also requires a fidelity assessment; it is not proof of
harm at clinical exposure. [KOSELUGO label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=7d042c61-f28f-4ab5-ab10-d7558c0d49ff)

## Direct-model positives cannot be transferred by pathway name

Colivelin improved behavioral and injury-related endpoints in neonatal
hypoxia–ischemia mice with BUBR1 knockdown. It was an experimental peptide
given before injury, not an inherited-MVA rescue or delayed repair. Absence
of the corresponding non-knockdown injury-plus-drug arm leaves generic injury
protection possible. Shared STAT3 signaling does not establish filgrastim
as an interchangeable medicine. [Chang 2025](https://doi.org/10.1002/cbin.70033)

G-CSF has a neonatal injury tissue-protection positive, but later independent
experiments found no long-term behavioral/injury benefit under their tested
schedules. A G-CSF/GSK3-beta mechanistic paper was retracted in 2026 and is
excluded as affirmative support. These findings do not invalidate all G-CSF
research; they prevent a falsely complete bridge to BUBR1 disease.
[Positive rat study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1888563/),
[negative mouse study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21145889/),
[2019 negative study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6436372/),
[retraction](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.expneurol.2026.115708)

## Two attractive shortcuts fail the source check

The MCP1 aging study used BubR1-hypomorphic mice for a baseline comparison,
but treated Ercc1-deficient mice with dasatinib/quercetin and old wild-type
mice with rapamycin. Those arms are not pharmacological BubR1 rescue, and
lower MCP1 is not itself recovered tissue function.
[Primary study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5847863/)

Low-nanomolar Taxol combined with partial BUBR1 depletion increased severe
segregation errors and reduced viability in cancer-cell models. The 1–10 nM
series must not be collapsed into the separate MPS1-arm 2–3 nM examples.
These are not MVA cells, but the deficient-protein interaction opposes a
generic microtubule-stabilization nomination.
[Janssen 2009](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2776415/)

The next commitment follows a reproducible, relevant functional signal.
Tissue support may matter without correcting prior aneuploidy; survival or
proliferation alone does not demonstrate that benefit. No clinical dose,
untested combination, comparative efficacy score or cure claim is proposed.
