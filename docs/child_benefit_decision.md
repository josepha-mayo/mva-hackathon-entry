# Make benefit for the child the first decision

Public-source research decision · 23 October 2026. **The unresolved question is whether a proposed medicine improves a function that matters in BUB1B disease at achievable exposure.** Neither winning an entry nor passing software tests establishes that. No biological drug response or benefit for this child has been demonstrated by this work.

## Choose the problem before promoting a medicine

The treating/research team should select a measurable present goal from its existing clinical knowledge: an ability to improve, a further loss to prevent, or an identified complication to address. Stable developmental difficulty, new loss of skills and an acute organ problem are different questions. This public-source extension has not verified which is the child's current priority; that is not evidence that the clinical team lacks that information. Identifying a relevant deficit can occur alongside the first controlled pilot, without completing every molecular mechanism first.

NALL's A–T trial supports a clinical analogy; it does not establish ataxia or a shared reversible constraint in this child. A useful symptomatic gain could matter without repairing every chromosome, but a marker change cannot stand in for it. [NALL evidence](levacetylleucine_challenger.md)

## The gene-specific biology changes the experiment

Adult BubR1-low hippocampal models had reduced progenitor output and neuronal maturation **without a detected survival difference** in the studied pulse-chase. An anti-apoptosis marker may therefore miss their limiting lesion. Young-neuron BUBR1 perturbation produced a different direction of dendrite growth from adult experiments; more dendrites or firing cannot be a universal rescue rule. The young-neuron paper's electrical findings concerned HDAC11 manipulation, not demonstrated BUB1B electrical rescue. [Yang 2017](https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.12594), [Watanabe 2014](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2014.03.022)

Near-complete cortical loss instead depleted progenitors and late-born neurons; a hypomorphic model did not reproduce the same cortical phenotype. A neuron-only assay can also omit impaired oligodendrocyte production/myelination. The function, lineage and stage must therefore be qualified rather than inferred from a drug's favorable paper. [Cortical primary](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6522074/), [myelination primary](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5191862/)

In a suitable existing neural system, a small **late BUBR1-restoration benchmark** could help distinguish an ongoing reversible constraint in surviving cells from effects of earlier lineage history. Compare stage-appropriate restoration after cell-cycle exit and a documented deficit with a matched procedural control, on the same useful evoked function. This is a proposed research control requiring feasibility review; it is not a treatment or an executed rescue. A negative cannot establish irreversible injury unless restoration and assay competence are interpretable. It can run alongside a phenotype-led drug pilot; it is not a universal entrance gate. Its controls require additional resources beyond the eight assigned drug/genotype conditions.

## Separate a resource lead from a runnable experiment

An official **Coriell GM21284** MVA lymphoblast line has an institutional release route. Stock/release has not been confirmed, it has a different allele pair, and EBV transformation changes its context. It is a lead for a cycling-cell question, **not a neural model or an exact-child ataluren test**. Published MVA cells have correctable checkpoint/alignment phenotypes, but that does not establish useful daughter output or neural recovery. [Official catalog](https://catalog.coriell.org/0/Sections/Search/Sample_Detail.aspx?Product=CC&Ref=GM21284), [primary complementation](https://doi.org/10.1158/0008-5472.CAN-09-4319)

No publicly listed BUB1B neural/corrective-reference pair with demonstrated rescue of the proposed evoked-function endpoint was verified in the bounded search. This does not mean no laboratory has one. The immediate practical commitment is to establish the selected lab's material access and an informative endpoint. A convenient blood-cell assay cannot rank three neural medicines.

There is also an existing research programme to build around: the MVA Society publicly reports a worm drug-repurposing study, patient single-cell profiling, supplement studies and growth of family-derived skin fibroblasts. These are organizational reports of research activity, **not published efficacy results, a data-release receipt or access to this child's cells**. The submission can identify which proposed functional question fits an already available platform, subject to the research team's confirmation. Follow-up belongs within the authorized hackathon/research process; no individual or family contact was made. [Official programme description](https://mvasociety.org/our-research-projects/)

| Observed result | Decision that could move toward benefit |
|---|---|
| A clinically relevant model deficit and informative reference behavior exist | Run the bounded drug-specific exposure comparison on that function; keep survival, starting-cell accounting and relevant precursor reserve. |
| A drug improves useful output after a deficit exists | Confirm independently, retain already-normal functions and investigate tissue delivery; BUBR1 restoration is not required for a functional bypass. |
| Only stress markers, cell counts or spontaneous activity improve | Do not call it useful rescue; examine the actual function and selection/loss explanations. |
| The model lacks the proposed deficit, appropriate lineage or interpretable exposure | Redirect that experiment; an uninterpretable result cannot eliminate every candidate or tissue. |

## Human care evidence does not support a blanket claim

The human literature includes complication-directed benefit and serious treatment harm/failure. One molecularly confirmed BUB1B Wilms-tumor report described subsequent remission follow-up and quality of life after surgery/dialysis; another reported graft rejection and fatal infection after initial transplant engraftment. Neither selects a therapy for this child or establishes whole-body disease correction. No direct human BUB1B neurological-rescue or disease-modifying medicine result was found in the bounded review. That is an evidence gap, not proof that nothing can help. [Complication-directed outcome](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31081598/), [transplant report](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13023-019-1073-x)

The useful research deliverable is a specific, feasible test of a patient-relevant function, with explicit results that change the next decision. The current [three-medicine comparison](neural_protection_comparison.md) remains conditional on that qualification. Existing care and treatment of an identified complication remain with the child's clinical team; the hackathon nomination supplies no reason to delay them. No patient contact, controlled-data access, material acquisition or wet experiment occurred in this review.
