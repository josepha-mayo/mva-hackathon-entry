# A first experiment that fits the actual resource

24 October 2026. This follow-up changes experimental eligibility and retains a newly found direct-model positive. **No medicine has earned clinical priority or demonstrated benefit for the child.** The aim is a result that can change a real research decision.

## Do not spend the native-stop experiment on a frameshift model

The listed Coriell GM21284 MVA B-cell line has S738fsX753/R814H. Reading through its frameshift-generated stop would not repair the preceding reading frame or reproduce the proposed L737Ter/N1002K target. Exclude this resource from the native-stop-restoration ataluren arm. That is a sequence-mechanism inference, not a measured failure of every ataluren effect. Stock, project release and a suitable functional assay remain unconfirmed. [Official catalog](https://catalog.coriell.org/0/Sections/Search/Sample_Detail.aspx?Product=CC&Ref=GM21284)

The older checkpoint/alignment observations with similar allele labels were in transformed fibroblasts; they do not qualify the currently listed lymphoblast product. Suspension-cell tracking also cannot inherit an adherent-cell capacity example without validation. The original eight-condition matrix remains conditional on its specified model; its size is not a reason to build a new platform. [Primary MVA study](https://doi.org/10.1158/0008-5472.CAN-09-4319)

## Start smaller when a qualified functional system already exists

If a laboratory already has an accessible MVA cycling preparation and an interpretable useful-output assay, a four-condition PBA feasibility comparison is an option: MVA vehicle/PBA and available reference vehicle/PBA, with one predeclared measured exposure profile. Label an unrelated reference honestly; it does not isolate BUB1B causality. PBA's human aneuploid-neuron stress result motivates investigation but proves neither BUBR1 stabilization nor useful cycling output. A pre-existing, evidenced oxidative-amplification question could instead use the NAC redox-fork comparison. This is resource-based question selection, not a drug-efficacy ranking. [PBA primary](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-020-70362-x), [NAC comparison](redox_fork.md)

Keep starting-cell denominators, completed divisions, errors, death and live nondivision visible. If tracking cannot support the proposed all-founder endpoint, a narrower checkpoint/viable-output assay must retain that narrower claim. A usable phenotype and initial drug response can be established together; complete mechanism or a newly built isogenic panel is not an entrance requirement. Independently confirm an interpretable hit before expanding. No such experiment has been performed here.

The PBA/elamipretide/NALL neural comparison still requires an appropriate neural system. Generic cells can qualify exposure, assay interference or a stated generic function; they cannot select a BUB1B neural treatment.

## A direct-model chemical positive, with an opposing cellular result

An ASCPT 2013 conference abstract reports AZD1152 treatment in BubR1-hypomorphic mice: median survival 136 versus 118 days in 70 treated/70 vehicle animals, with a reported p=0.04. Preliminary cardiac observations favored treatment; other aging traits and overt quality of life did not improve. Treatment began early in life. This is limited conference evidence, without a verified full follow-up, chromosome/daughter outcome or exact-child model. It is not a marketed-drug nomination. [Official abstract LBII-2, printed p92](https://www.ascpt.org/Portals/28/docs/Annual%20Meetings/Annual%20Meeting%20Archive/2013%20Final%20Program.pdf?ver=2018-01-19-101927-487)

A separate source-data reanalysis finds that AZD1152-HQPA increased mean misalignment in each of five WT/transgenic-expression MEF groups under an imposed challenge. Genetic protection against that challenge is not drug rescue. These endpoints and models differ from mouse survival. The sharper hypothesis is **state-dependent restoration of attachment/error-correction balance**, not maximal Aurora inhibition. [Actual source-data audit](aurora_source_data.md)

A separate patient-cell result was also resolved: FH535 and IWR-1 suppressed the measured Wnt activity but did not restore cilia in PCS1 fibroblasts. Genetic DVL2/3 suppression had a partial ciliary positive. These are different perturbations; pathway-reporting normalization was insufficient in those cells. This does not decide every Wnt-related neural question. [Primary paper, p2061/S2D–E](https://doi.org/10.1093/hmg/ddr090)

## An approved target hit does not close the drug bridge

Fostamatinib/R406 was examined as an approved-drug bridge to Aurora B. The primary profile gives R406 an isolated-enzyme AURKB IC50 of 30 nM and binding Kd of 180 nM; those are different assays. The PMDA reports a cellular histone-H3 endpoint EC50 of 4.33 µM in nocodazole-arrested A549 cells. Its protein fraction and intracellular exposure were not recovered. The approximately 144-fold cross-assay difference is not a patient exposure margin. No direct R406-BUB1B functional rescue was verified. [Primary profile and Table S3](https://doi.org/10.1002/prp2.175), [PMDA review, section 3.2.5](https://www.pmda.go.jp/drugs/2023/P20230117001/230034000_30400AMX00459_A100_1.pdf)

The current label explicitly does not recommend use below 18 because of growing-bone findings; hypertension, liver injury, diarrhea and neutropenia also matter. Its SYK/B-cell effects could confound an EBV-transformed model; such sensitivity has not been measured in GM21284. **Keep this as a reserve nonclinical hypothesis, outside the fixed first pilot.** A smaller, state-specific useful response remains untested; neither enzyme binding nor a clean protein marker establishes it. No dose or use in the child is proposed. [Current prescribing information](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=21149cc3-049b-43e2-b141-c9499160556c)

## Existing programmes need their actual model and result table

The Society describes worm, supplement and fibroblast programmes but does not publicly specify an MVA compound ranking on that page. Its linked published UNC80 worm screen is a different genotype and endpoint; its hits cannot be relabeled as MVA rescue. Likewise, older SAN-1 synthetic-lethal genetic screens identify vulnerabilities, not restorative medicines. A useful follow-up would identify the actual existing model, a reproducible relevant function and compound-response/negative results before human BUB1B confirmation. No platform access, contact, materials request or experimental result is implied. [Official programmes](https://mvasociety.org/our-research-projects/), [UNC80 primary screen](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.92491.4)
