## A plausible nomination is different from an established treatment lead

The primary BUBR1 work supports distinguishing deficient protein abundance
from persistent dysfunction. Raising certain other mutant constructs to
WT-comparable abundance restored a spindle-drug checkpoint readout in an
engineered system. It did not test the proposed missense allele, an eligible
medicine or the complete faithful-division outcome. WT cDNA complementation
of a patient cell is also distinct from rescue of unchanged mutant protein.
[Suijkerbuijk et al., 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20516114/)

Arimoclomol has cross-target cellular activity. For example, a VCP-fibroblast
study used nominal 10 micromolar exposure and reported an improved MTT
signal. That metabolic assay is not a direct count of faithful divisions;
the target and disease differ. The current US label describes NPC use with
miglustat and states that its clinical mechanism is unknown. These facts do
not establish a BUBR1 rescue mechanism.
[VCP study](https://doi.org/10.3390/biomedicines10102443),
[US label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=5feffc0e-453d-47fa-91dd-38d4952309bc)

Phenylbutyrate's other-target functional positives and negatives remain in
the [medicine evidence card](drug_decision.md). Its own formulation, active
analyte, exposure profile and useful BUBR1 response are unqualified. A named
comparison is not a measured ranking.

## Mechanism comparison alongside a qualified phenotypic pilot

In qualified renewable material, compare WT and unchanged missense sequence
over assigned, overlapping physiological full-length abundance ranges, with
the same verified background and remaining-allele context. Preserve the
native correction/carrier reference and qualify RNA/product identity, tags,
localization, stage and expression dynamics. Retain all assigned cells;
post-treatment selection of cells with convenient protein amounts cannot
manufacture a causal abundance match.

Require checkpoint hold, attachment-error correction, release, completed
unchallenged divisions and daughter outcomes. A longer arrest, correct
localization or a brighter protein band cannot substitute for that outcome.

| Result in a qualified comparison | What changes |
|---|---|
| Unchanged missense protein supports useful function at appropriate supply | A supply-directed mechanism remains plausible; distinguish input, early loss and mature loss for attribution while a qualified phenotype pilot can proceed |
| Amount is appropriate but function remains abnormal | Amount alone is insufficient under that regimen; this does not reject every conformational corrector or mechanism-independent phenotypic hit |
| WT and correction references fail, RNA/product identity is unresolved, or the comparison is selected/confounded | The assay is unqualified; do not call a drug or mechanism ineffective |
| A reproducible exact-genotype basal phenotype and a separable functional assay exist | A resource-limited marketed-medicine screen can test unknown mechanisms; each hit still needs its own exposure-qualified confirmation |

This comparison informs a mechanism branch. It is not a universal
prerequisite for a small qualified renewable-cell medicine pilot. Each
exploratory compound must earn further priority through its own measured relevant
cellular response, exposure/time profile, useful function and independent
confirmation. A functional bypass also does not prove the original molecular
lesion. Tissue-level relevance remains a separate requirement.

## Correction must not be confused with selection or reversibility

The old protocol already anticipates mock/process controls, genomic QC and
a true-null comparison where a stop product persists. Retain those controls
in the current compressed design:

- A matched carrier needs the same genetic background, not just the same
  allele labels. An unrelated carrier is a transportability reference.
- Track pre-edit origins, recovery and expansion. Founder enrollment before
  drug exposure misses cells lost during editing or cloning. Reciprocal edits
  cannot restore an eliminated karyotype or cell state.
- Carry matched process controls through the same bottleneck and prespecify
  genomic/passage/state QC. Better-growing recovered clones alone do not
  demonstrate genetic rescue. Do not select cleaner clones as the result.
  If selection remains unresolved, withhold a causal rescue interpretation.
- Measure native stop-allele RNA and any truncated product before using that
  allele as a null/dose-only control or assigning missense interference.
- Correction before expansion or differentiation can prevent a phenotype.
  A claim of recovery after a deficit needs a timed intervention in a
  comparable, established pretreatment state. A pre-emptively corrected line
  does not set a validated treatment ceiling for damaged cells.

These are identification risks to control, not findings that an editing
artifact actually occurred. Start with renewable material; no new use of
participant material is authorized by this design.

## A historical concentration rule cannot settle clinical feasibility

The inherited arimoclomol 2-micromolar nominal culture cap is a historical
screen rule. It is not a validated unbound tissue or intracellular limit.
No replacement numerical ceiling is assigned here.

A negative below the cap means no useful response under the tested condition.
Without measured relevant pharmacodynamics, it cannot distinguish failure
to engage a process from an engaged process failing to help. A positive below
the cap does not establish achievable tissue exposure. A positive above it
cannot pass the frozen screen, but is neither automatically clinically
feasible nor automatically impossible. A different exposure hypothesis needs
an independently justified, prespecified protocol; do not move an old threshold
after seeing an outcome. Another compound cannot inherit this cap.

The [current label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=5feffc0e-453d-47fa-91dd-38d4952309bc)
reports approximately 10% plasma binding, a roughly four-hour half-life,
dose-dependent CSF concentrations and separate pediatric NPC serum estimates.
Its adult PK table uses twice the maximum recommended dosage. None supplies
the child's effect-relevant free cellular exposure or a BUBR1 response.

## What would support a treatment claim

A reproducible laboratory hit would still need relevant tissue function,
candidate-specific safety/translation and clinical evidence. The FRZB
[tissue hypothesis](tissue_function.md) remains a proposed state-dependent
experiment; developmental genetics do not establish post-deficit drug repair.
No literature search, software pass or completed submission can guarantee
that a medicine will heal this child.
