# Ataluren must earn its place

Public-source research update, 23 October 2026. No experiment in this entry
has demonstrated a medicine response in the proposed genotype or benefit to
the child. Ataluren is one bounded feasibility hypothesis, not an established
treatment lead or an exclusive first step.

## Why retain the experiment?

A premature stop supplies a specific question: could pharmacological
readthrough produce enough useful BUBR1 to improve future cell function?
Other-gene positives justify testing that possibility. In SMC1A patient cells,
ataluren increased protein/partner association and a separate chromosome
panel showed fewer structural gaps or breaks. The selected-metaphase endpoint
did not establish faithful division; its dose and independent-replicate
details were not specified. [SMC1A study](https://doi.org/10.1002/epi.70150)

Primary FANCC nonsense-mutant fibroblasts also showed fewer micronuclei after
drug treatment in a 2025 study. The eight fields were not independent donors;
all-founder division/selection tracking was absent.
[Fanconi study, Figure 7](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41420-025-02571-0)

An uncontrolled three-person Shwachman-Diamond series reported protein,
hematological and pancreatic-marker improvements. It supplies a human
non-CNS signal, with no concurrent control. Repeated samples do not enlarge
the patient count, and limited clonal follow-up does not establish cancer
protection. [SDS study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-63137-3)

Native SMAD9 cells with a UGAA stop context and little mutant transcript
showed improved BMP-dependent microRNA processing; endothelial responses
remained below controls. Thus stop context or predicted RNA decay alone
cannot exclude a response. That result does not establish a BUB1B response.
[BMPR2/SMAD9 study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3824059/)

## Why refuse a presumption of success?

Ataluren failed the confirmatory ACT CF trial even after excluding chronic
inhaled aminoglycosides. A small randomized pediatric Dravet/CDKL5 crossover
trial also found no measured seizure or developmental benefit; it did not
measure CNS engagement. These are different disorders, with their own study
limits, but they directly challenge a generic nonsense-variant-to-benefit
inference. [ACT CF](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcf.2020.01.007),
[pediatric CNS trial](https://doi.org/10.1002/acn3.51306)

In AIPL1 retinal organoids, increased protein immunoreactivity did not correct
the pathological cGMP endpoint. In FANCA models, metabolic effects also
occurred without a nonsense allele, while proliferation decreased in mutant
and control cells. A positive band or cleaner surviving population therefore
cannot establish useful readthrough. [AIPL1 study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.stemcr.2022.08.005),
[FANCA study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41420-026-02983-6)

The existing missense allele can already supply a full-length protein pool;
that has not been measured here. Attribution requires more than total BUBR1 or common downstream peptides.
Readthrough can also insert a non-native residue. Its function must be tested;
it is neither automatically normal nor automatically useless.
[Readthrough-product study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5098639/)

## Exposure and medicine status

The current UK manufacturer SmPC describes conditional authorization; its
combined first-authorisation/renewal field does not establish a specific
renewal event. EU authorization was not renewed in March 2025 because
effectiveness in nonsense-mutation DMD was not confirmed. Current local stock
and MVA access are unverified. [UK SmPC](https://www.medicines.org.uk/emc/product/10431/smpc),
[EMA record](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/translarna)

Human plasma binding was measured at 99.6%; estimated free plasma peak is not
measured free brain exposure. Nominal culture, unbound medium, plasma and
intracellular concentration are distinct quantities. A laboratory pilot must
measure its relevant exposure and function; no child dose follows from the
literature. [Binding study](https://doi.org/10.1002/prp2.576)

## The decision the experiment must support

Keep a small ataluren arm in a qualified native-context model, with the
[finite pilot](finite_pilot.md) defining the primary functional readout.
Confirm a signal independently and resolve product origin before calling it
stop-allele rescue. A useful response through another mechanism remains worth
investigating under its correct name. Qualified failure retires the tested
condition; assay failure is unresolved, not a negative medicine result.

Fasudil/hydroxyfasudil and cilostazol/metabolites remain independently testable when a relevant
ciliary or barrier defect is established. They need not wait for ataluren to
fail. NAC remains a comparator. No combination or comparative efficacy ranking
is proposed.

This research update supersedes the earlier first-priority implication. It
does not alter the signed historical baseline or claim laboratory validation.
