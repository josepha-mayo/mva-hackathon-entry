# Before the next faulty division

## Phenylbutyrate to preserve useful neural capacity in BUB1B-associated MVA

Joseph Mayo — Track 2, Rare Disease, Real Kid: MVA Hackathon 2026

Research candidate, 24 October 2026. **No new wet experiment, exact-allele drug rescue or clinical benefit has been demonstrated by our work.**

### The therapeutic proposal

We nominate **phenylbutyrate (PBA)** to reduce the protein stress caused by aneuploidy and preserve useful function in surviving neural cells. The intended benefit is continued maturation, communication and tissue function where cells retain recoverable capacity. This is a medicine hypothesis with a specific causal basis: actual PBA effects in human aneuploid neurons, evidence that PBA can restore vesicle function in another neurological disorder, and experimental evidence that some consequences of chromosome instability can be modified downstream of the chromosome error.

The strongest case is **downstream protection from ongoing injury**. A second opportunity—raising the functional supply of residual BUBR1—is conditional on a recoverable protein defect and does not carry the nomination. Established chromosome changes and earlier developmental loss impose different limits. This report prioritizes a research candidate; it supplies no treatment or dosing recommendation.

### 1. What might still be recoverable?

BUB1B encodes BUBR1, which supports the spindle checkpoint and correct chromosome attachments. The existing public entry proposes L737Ter/N1002K; phase and endogenous transcript/product behavior have not been independently established in this extension. The premature stop could cause transcript decay or a C-terminal truncation. N1002K alters the C-terminal region, but its effect on protein stability versus intrinsic function remains unmeasured here. The linked ClinVar missense record concerns a different nucleotide substitution producing the same N1002K protein change; it does not independently confirm the proposed child allele or its mechanism. [Public entry](https://github.com/josepha-mayo/mva-hackathon-entry), [stop record](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/clinvar/variation/533901/), [missense record](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/clinvar/variation/4600147/)

An ongoing cellular problem offers a different therapeutic opportunity from a completed developmental injury. In adult BubR1-low hippocampus, progenitor production and neuronal maturation were impaired without a detected newborn-cell survival difference. Near-complete cortical deletion instead depleted progenitors and late-born neurons; hypomorphic models differed. BubR1 deficiency can also impair oligodendrocyte production and myelination. The C-terminal pseudokinase domain also supports KARD phosphorylation and PP2A-B56 recruitment; a protein-abundance increase need not restore the appropriate attachment/checkpoint sequence. A cell-survival intervention therefore addresses only one possible limiting process. No particular symptom, progressive decline, ataxia or myelin lesion is assumed in this child. [C-terminal mechanism](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2020.108397), [adult neurogenesis](https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.12594), [cortical study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6522074/), [myelination study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5191862/)

A 2026 complex chromosome-instability fly model provides a causal reason to investigate downstream protection. Genetic enhancement of mitochondrial chaperones or redox defenses restored Pros-positive progeny-cell counts and brain size after spindle-checkpoint disruption. Rapamycin increased neuroblast counts without restoring progeny counts or brain size. These are genetic/developmental and cell-count findings, not PBA treatment, behavioral recovery or BUB1B-patient efficacy. They nevertheless show why surviving cells, progenitor abundance and usable neural output must be distinguished. [Primary study, Figures 6–7](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-70521-0)

### 2. Why this medicine earns priority

| Evidence supporting PBA | Actual observation | Remaining bridge |
|---|---|---|
| Human aneuploid neural cells | Sodium 4-PBA at nominal 100 micromolar during induction reduced aggregate and cleaved-caspase-3 signals in trisomy-13/21 neurons. | Simple trisomies, preventive exposure and marker endpoints; no recovered network function or BUB1B drug rescue. |
| Functional protein rescue | In mutant Munc18-1 models, PBA improved soluble protein and stimulus-dependent synaptotagmin uptake in primary neurons; worm locomotion and neurotransmission-related assays also improved. | Different protein and disease mechanism; functional experiments cannot inherit the separate aneuploid-neuron study's concentration. |
| Opposing neural-function result | A 2026 STXBP1 zebrafish study found no motor or seizure benefit at tolerated PBA exposure. | Severe loss-of-function deletions differ from recoverable missense proteins; this limits generalization without directly refuting client-specific chaperoning. |
| Human physiological response | A randomized 18-person cystic-fibrosis study found partial restoration of nasal ion transport. | A different tissue/client protein and a physiological endpoint, without established overall clinical benefit. |
| Clinical feasibility with opposing evidence | PBA has approved urea-cycle-disorder uses; uncontrolled pediatric neurological reports include functional improvements and serious toxicity. A randomized 107-child SMA trial found no motor advantage: 0.60 versus 0.73 points, p=.70. | Medicinal use and responses in selected other disorders do not establish a safe, effective MVA application. |

[Human aneuploid neurons, Figure 6](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-020-70362-x), [Munc18-1, Figures 6–7](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-018-06507-4), [negative zebrafish study](https://doi.org/10.1002/prp2.70285), [CF physiological trial](https://doi.org/10.1164/ajrccm.157.2.9706088), [SMA randomized trial](https://doi.org/10.1212/01.wnl.0000249142.82285.d6), [uncontrolled pediatric report](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pediatrneurol.2026.06.012), [current medicinal label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=900e7dc0-9afe-4f50-80de-90567bb78519)

A separate 2025 study supplies a bounded functional precedent without a mutant-client mechanism: PBA shortly after neonatal injury improved selected later sensorimotor outcomes. Long-term myelin rescue was not significant; treatment preceded the measured behavioral deficits. Unblinded handling and incompletely documented endpoint-family/litter control limit inference. This is early injury protection in another disease context, not established MVA-deficit reversal. [Primary study, Figures 3–4 and 10](https://doi.org/10.3390/ijms262412099)

The proposal does not require N1002K to be a folding mutation: reducing the wider protein burden could protect cells despite persisting aneuploidy. Its major disconfirmation would be a lack of useful neural improvement despite an informative model and qualified exposure. PBA's multiple actions also leave the precise protective mechanism unresolved. The closest human-neural result does not establish that aggregate reduction mediates functional recovery.

**Secondary opportunity: functional BUBR1 supply.** Expression-matched I909T/L1012P constructs restored checkpoint response in BUBR1-depleted U2OS cells. A separate HeLa depletion experiment distinguished residual protein levels around 6% and 13%. These results support recoverability for particular mutants; they are neither a universal rescue threshold nor patient-cell PBA treatment. N1002K could be unstable, intrinsically defective, or both. More protein would help only if it supplies appropriate function. [Suijkerbuijk 2010, Figure 4](https://doi.org/10.1158/0008-5472.CAN-09-4319)

### 3. Delivery is a missing causal link

Adult urea-cycle-disorder data give total parent-PBA AUCs of 433/508 microgram·h/mL for glycerol/sodium formulations, approximately 2,637/3,094 micromolar·h over 24 hours. Maintained nominal 100-micromolar culture would integrate to 2,400 micromolar·h; its actual integral and free fraction were not measured. The label reports 81–98% in-vitro protein binding. Applying those bounds as a sensitivity calculation gives free-plasma AUC envelopes of 53–501 and 62–588 micromolar·h—not measured free AUC or brain exposure. Short-lived plasma peaks cannot establish sustained neural delivery. Millimolar functional analogies face a larger exposure bridge. [Primary PK](https://doi.org/10.1002/hep.26058), [label, section 12.3](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=900e7dc0-9afe-4f50-80de-90567bb78519)

Glycerol-PBA is a prodrug; parent PBA, phenylacetate and intact prodrug are distinct analytes. Neither CSF entry in animals nor a matching total-plasma AUC establishes a neuronal pharmacodynamic window. The PBA/taurursodiol ALS combination's failed confirmatory trial also argues against a generic clinical-neuroprotection claim; it is a different intervention and disease. No child exposure curve, effective dose or efficacy probability is simulated. [Source-based exposure arithmetic](https://github.com/josepha-mayo/mva-hackathon-entry/blob/main/docs/exposure_time_audit.md), [confirmatory trial report](https://investors.amylyx.com/static-files/64e7442a-9d64-4f70-af5c-008d4b803ebe)

### 4. Let the therapeutic route determine the decisive result

For the **primary neural-preservation route**, the relevant question is whether actual PBA improves a disease-relevant useful neural output at measured, realistic exposure. Survival, maturation, total output and function per survivor must remain separate. Treatment after an established deficit distinguishes recovery from prevention; either can matter, with a correctly bounded claim. An increase in spontaneous activity, neuron number or a stress-marker improvement alone does not establish useful function. Retaining precursor reserve and existing normal functions matters alongside any apparent gain.

A suitable existing neural preparation and an informative reference are prerequisites for interpreting this comparison, not resources claimed to be in our possession. No publicly listed assay-ready BUB1B neural/corrective-reference pair was verified in the bounded search. A convenient blood-cell model cannot resolve neural benefit. Material access and model qualification belong within the authorized research programme. [Neural comparison](https://github.com/josepha-mayo/mva-hackathon-entry/blob/main/docs/neural_protection_comparison.md), [benefit and lineage qualification](https://github.com/josepha-mayo/mva-hackathon-entry/blob/main/docs/child_benefit_decision.md)

For the **secondary cycling route**, a four-condition cycling comparison—MVA and available reference, each with vehicle or one predeclared measured PBA profile—can assess productive division, chromosome-error components, death and nondivision from all eligible founders. This distinguishes useful output from cytostasis or selective loss. Attribution to increased functional BUBR1 requires independent evidence connecting protein abundance or competence to that response. It does not test neural preservation, establish euploid daughters, or validate long-term clone safety. A cycling negative cannot by itself retire the primary neural route. [Finite pilot](https://github.com/josepha-mayo/mva-hackathon-entry/blob/main/docs/finite_pilot.md), [resource-specific first test](https://github.com/josepha-mayo/mva-hackathon-entry/blob/main/docs/first_informative_test.md)

Coriell GM21284 is an EBV-transformed MVA B-cell resource with S738fsX753/R814H; availability and functional competence are unconfirmed. Its frameshift cannot model native L737Ter readthrough. Preserving genomically abnormal cells could also have adverse consequences: useful tissue function and abnormal proliferative-lineage behavior are separate questions in a cancer-predisposition syndrome. No tested PBA benefit/harm balance in MVA is claimed. [Official resource](https://catalog.coriell.org/0/Sections/Search/Sample_Detail.aspx?Product=CC&Ref=GM21284)

### 5. Challengers capable of changing the choice

Independent public-source searches examined candidates beyond the previous shortlist. None located a completed approved-drug-to-BUB1B-function-to-human-benefit chain. PBA remains the conditional proteotoxic-injury lead, with distinct alternatives retained:

| Challenger | Strongest positive | Why it does not presently displace PBA |
|---|---|---|
| Fluoxetine / sFRP3–plasticity | Genetic sFRP3 reduction improves selected BubR1-mouse structural/progenitor measures; actual fluoxetine improves plasticity/memory in one adult trisomy-mouse study. A 2026 pediatric trisomy-21 study reported selected functional gains. | Drug effects on sFRP3 have opposite directions across rodent studies. The 14-enrolled human study lacked placebo and multiplicity control; a separate mouse study found seizures/deaths at higher exposure. No BUB1B drug-function result. |
| Lamivudine / endogenous-retroelement stress | Recognition-memory improvement in adult Ts65Dn mice. | No established BUB1B neural target; companion transcriptomics reuse the same animals. Actual lamivudine reduced NK clearance of complex-aneuploid cells in another experiment. The 12-adult open-label ART-AD study did not demonstrate cognitive improvement. |
| Lithium / neurogenesis | Adult trisomy-mouse neurogenesis, LTP and selected memory recovery, with causal neurogenesis evidence. | Small randomized human Down-syndrome study reported more short-term cognitive difficulties. Checkpoint compatibility and a narrow therapeutic margin remain material. |
| NAC / redox defense | Actual NAC protects human trisomic neural cultures under imposed oxidative challenge; glutathione pharmacodynamics can occur below 100 micromolar in another model. | Closest checkpoint-disruption behavioral rescue used the distinct NACA amide. An ongoing cysteine-responsive redox deficit in BUB1B tissue is unestablished. |
| Elamipretide / mitochondria | Neural/animal positives and a narrow approved Barth-syndrome indication. | No demonstrated BUB1B-responsive mitochondrial lesion; MMPOWER-3 showed no walking advantage in 218 participants. |
| Levacetylleucine / symptom-directed function | Randomized A–T symptomatic benefit, 73 participants. | Could become stronger for a corresponding established symptom; ataxia or a shared reversible lesion cannot be assumed in this child. |

[sFRP3/BubR1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6413750/), [fluoxetine mouse positive](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24269730/), [opposing sFRP3 direction](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2929274/), [human exploratory fluoxetine study](https://doi.org/10.1177/10445463261458603), [mouse fluoxetine harm](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3405721/), [lamivudine behavior](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9344819/), [lamivudine surveillance counterevidence](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8339690/), [ART-AD](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11917871/), [lithium mouse function](https://www.jci.org/articles/view/64650), [DOWNSLIT results](https://www.clinicaltrialsregister.eu/ctr-search/rest/download/result/attachment/2008-008342-20/1/21395), [NAC human-neural challenge](https://doi.org/10.1002/stem.1297), [NAC pharmacodynamics](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4720670/), [NACA behavioral study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11062972/), [MMPOWER-3](https://doi.org/10.1212/WNL.0000000000207402), [A–T trial](https://doi.org/10.1016/S1474-4422(26)00158-4)

The investigational Aurora inhibitor AZD1152 has a conference-level BubR1-hypomorph survival positive, 136 versus 118 days, n=70/group, p=.04, without overt quality-of-life improvement. This deserves weight but cannot be transferred to approved fostamatinib/R406 from a biochemical target match. Native-stop ataluren remains an allele-oriented hypothesis with unresolved transcript, inserted-amino-acid and protein-function questions. Genetic SIRT2 lifespan rescue is not NMN drug-lifespan rescue. These distinctions prevent an attractive target name from becoming unsupported efficacy. [ASCPT LBII-2, printed p92](https://www.ascpt.org/Portals/28/docs/Annual%20Meetings/Annual%20Meeting%20Archive/2013%20Final%20Program.pdf?ver=2018-01-19-101927-487), [NAD/SIRT2 primary](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4194088/), [stop-allele review](https://github.com/josepha-mayo/mva-hackathon-entry/blob/main/docs/stop_allele_challenge.md)

### 6. Executed work and impact

Our executed analyses reproduce public source measurements and expose interpretation limits. The Aurora workbook reproduces 60 percentages, ten means/sample SDs and five group-mean differences; all five tested groups show greater misalignment under the imposed AZD1152-HQPA challenge. This differs from genetic protection against that challenge and from the conference survival contrast. No raw-cell denominators, pairing identifiers or new inferential statistics were invented. The synaptic workbook audit identified SEM/SD-label and supplemental-summary inconsistencies; missing run/donor labels prevent biological inference from pooled ROIs. Neither analysis is an MVA drug-rescue experiment. [Aurora reproduction](https://github.com/josepha-mayo/mva-hackathon-entry/blob/main/docs/aurora_source_data.md), [synaptic-source audit](https://github.com/josepha-mayo/mva-hackathon-entry/blob/main/docs/nall_source_data_audit.md)

The useful contribution is a prioritized medicine hypothesis, a causal explanation of the benefit it might deliver, and evidence that can change that choice. The largest unresolved facts are whether ongoing proteotoxic injury limits useful BUB1B neural function, whether actual PBA rescues that function at relevant exposure, and whether the benefit outweighs adverse effects and abnormal-cell preservation. These are specific biological uncertainties; software tests and mechanistic analogies do not resolve them. No simulation is presented as efficacy evidence.

### Research methods and provenance

Public-source research, source-data arithmetic and writing used OpenAI ChatGPT/Codex Pro with the operator-confirmed training opt-out. Independent final-day challenges used user-requested GPT-6 Astra at max. Initial briefs withheld parent rankings, but supplied memory exposed some candidate names; the reviews were not strictly blinded. No controlled participant payload, patient contact, material acquisition or wet experiment was used in this extension. Earlier source cards and negative evidence remain available in this repository.
