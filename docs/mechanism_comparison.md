# Which allele can supply useful protein?

Research supplement, 22 October 2026. The proposed Leu737Ter + Asn1002Lys
pair remains conditional on confirmation and phase. No exact-genotype
rescue or clinical benefit has been demonstrated.

## Let the measured bottleneck choose the intervention

| Measured result | Retain | Retire or hold |
|---|---|---|
| Independent input preserved; excessive early loss; surviving protein functions | Productive-delivery hypothesis, with folding and assembly as competing causes | A mature half-life alone cannot settle the mechanism |
| Excess mature loss after age, state and dilution controls | Maintenance/clearance hypothesis | Do not automatically choose a chaperone probe |
| Low input or abnormal transcript | Input/transcript explanation | Do not call this protein instability |
| Amount restored but function remains defective | An abundance-independent functional lesion | Abundance-only rescue as a sufficient mechanism |
| Input or recovery cannot be identified | Mechanism unresolved | An estimated maturation yield or a drug screen interpreted as proof |

The [functional-supply counterexamples](functional_supply.md) make several
measurement ambiguities executable. Their invented rates are not the child's
rates, an assay schedule or a drug-response model.

Arimoclomol remains a conditional proteostasis probe. Its other-disease
approval cannot establish an exact-allele response or the required cellular
state. Its inherited nominal culture ceiling also requires measured free and
intracellular exposure and response onset. A delayed chaperone response can
affect later cohorts without recovering a cohort already lost.
[US prescribing information](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=5feffc0e-453d-47fa-91dd-38d4952309bc)

Nicotinic acid is a parked maintenance challenger. Published precursor and
deacetylase evidence leaves untested links between that medicine, the exact
protein pool and function; the inspected label does not establish pediatric
safety and effectiveness. It is not an advanced candidate or a combination
proposal. [Primary pathway study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24825348/),
[precursor-dependence study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17604275/),
[label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/getFile.cfm?setid=bf287412-160b-403f-8eb4-57f29a475902&type=pdf)

## Correct the allele and compare the genotype actually produced

Missense-only correction leaves a stop/WT carrier. Confirmatory equivalence
therefore uses independently derived matched carriers. WT/WT is an additional
reference, not an interchangeable single-correction target. Stop-only
correction instead leaves WT/missense and benchmarks a distinct restoration
route. Double correction is a separate full-genotype reference. Reciprocal
recreation, independent edit events and clone qualification strengthen causal
attribution; images and daughters are not independent edit events.

Where a function is already normal, require preservation within a qualified
equivalence range instead of improvement against a nonexistent defect. A
missense carrier with dysfunction beyond an adequately measured stop-carrier
dose effect raises possible interference: increasing mutant protein must
then be reconsidered.

Other experimental constructs connect the C-terminal domain with attachment
regulation and appropriate checkpoint release. A longer arrest alone is
insufficient. [Primary domain study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33207204/)

## Keep stop restoration separate from repositioned medicines

The public-reference codon calculation supports a Leu-to-opal-stop premise.
A published Leu-decoding suppressor in other cellular disease models could
in principle restore the native amino acid here; an Arg-decoding reagent
would make a different product. That comparator was already identified in
the prior reviewed work. It is not an approved medicine, a new invention or
an exact-allele rescue result.
[Primary suppressor study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40650978/)

Substrate, delivery, exact-context suppression, amino-acid identity,
full-length function and termination specificity each require measurement.
The arimoclomol exposure ceiling and chaperone-response requirement do not
transfer to this different modality. Reporter suppression cannot advance it.

## Measure the protein without mistaking an assay change for rescue

The public-reference calculation finds an added trypsin cleavage site for
the proposed missense change: its reference spanning peptide has 34 residues,
while the substituted spanning peptide has three. They cannot be assumed to
have interchangeable analytical responses. Qualify invariant peptides,
digestion/recovery and orthogonal full-length measurement. If both alleles
supply full-length protein, their combined invariant signal cannot identify
each allele's contribution. No peptide detectability or abundance deficit
is established by this arithmetic.

The reference audit matched independently obtained transcript translation
and protein sequence. Its source receipts and candidate runner remain in
the private research review pending a scoped publication review. They are
outside the frozen signed release and are not an automatic advancement gate.

The useful outcome remains fewer newly generated chromosome errors with
normal divisions preserved, independent daughter-fate assessment, no
abnormal-clone protection and independent replication. No desktop analysis
establishes that this entry can save the child.
