# Which medicine earns the next experiment?

Primary-source decision audit, updated 23 October 2026. These are research questions,
not treatment choices. No medicine has demonstrated rescue of the proposed
exact genotype, clinical benefit, or acceptable risk for this child.

## Decision

**NAC is a conditional research nomination; no medicine is an established
treatment lead.** The [redox-fork design](redox_fork.md) tests protection from
oxidative amplification against release of abnormal-cell restraint. This
is an informative experiment priority, not predicted efficacy or a comparative
drug ranking. The [current decision](treatment_evidence.md) retains arimoclomol
and formulation-specific phenylbutyrate as separate supply hypotheses.

A small qualified renewable-cell phenotypic pilot can proceed alongside
mechanistic attribution. Exact-allele confirmation, candidate-specific
exposure, function and independent replication are needed for stronger claims;
complete turnover identification is not required before every exploratory
condition. None of these experiments has been executed here.

The sharper rival is **defective coordination of checkpoint hold with
attachment/release**, even at normal protein amount. The accompanying
[balanced-function experiment](balanced_function.md) describes a bounded local-recruitment bypass
comparison that can challenge the supply story. It does not nominate a
global phosphatase activator or an engineered recruitment reagent as a medicine.

These routes are not an untested cocktail. A strong clinical label cannot
compensate for an absent target bridge, and a direct pathway paper cannot
compensate for absent medicine exposure and function. No composite score is
assigned to conceal those missing observations.

An attractive-sounding ATP-pocket screen was not promoted. BUB1 is a
different protein, and insect BUBR1 structures cannot validate binding or
stabilization of the proposed human protein. Human pseudokinase work gives
a conformational rationale, not an approved stabilizing ligand; binding an
essential mitotic regulator could also disrupt its function. No qualifying
exact-human-variant binding result was identified in this bounded audit.
[Human pseudokinase study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22698286/),
[insect structural study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41422-019-0178-z)

## The evidence that changes the decision

**Phenylbutyrate has cross-target evidence, with its negatives preserved.**
Guiberson's primary study rescued Munc18-1 dysfunction in neuronal and worm
models. A 2026 SLC6A1 study found functional rescue favoring pharmacochaperoning
over an HDAC-only explanation, including experiments at nominal 2 mM for
24 hours. Its detailed Figure 4 results are variant-dependent; do not turn
the abstract's broad wording into universal rescue. Neither target is BUBR1.
[Guiberson et al., 2018](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-018-06507-4),
[DeLeeuw et al., 2026](https://doi.org/10.1002/acn3.70430)

The negative 2026 zebrafish study found no locomotion or seizure rescue under
its tested conditions. Its body reports locomotion at 25�50 micromolar, but
Figure 1D's caption says 10/25; that discrepancy needs clarification. The
seizure test used an acute 50-micromolar exposure. The reported negative
locomotion Z-prime also limits confidence in assay separation. Null fish,
residual-function missense systems and timing differ. Preserve the negative
without treating it as a precise exposure boundary or definitive test of
every chaperone mechanism or MVA.
[Frick et al., 2026](https://doi.org/10.1002/prp2.70285)

A September 2026 observational parent-interview study included 18 children
with STXBP1/SLC6A1 disorders. Reported improvements were uncontrolled; one
child had severe toxicity with metabolic acidosis and aspiration pneumonia
requiring intubation. This is evidence of human use and a safety concern,
not controlled efficacy or a BUBR1 bridge. The earlier 20-child trial report
was a preprint and is labeled as such, not silently promoted to a randomized
trial or counted as independent confirmation of potentially overlapping cases.
[Observational study, 2026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pediatrneurol.2026.06.012),
[open-label preprint, 2024](https://doi.org/10.1101/2024.11.06.24316676)

**Formulation and analyte matter.** The current RAVICTI label includes
pediatric UCD experience, so the old sodium-product table is not a complete
account of this route. Glycerol phenylbutyrate is a prodrug, hydrolyzed to PBA
and then metabolized to PAA. Its UCD active moiety PAA is not a substitute
for measuring PBA for the proposed chaperone hypothesis. A direct culture
experiment cannot assume intact prodrug provides an equivalent PBA exposure.
The label reports 81�98% in-vitro PBA protein binding. Sodium burden belongs
to sodium formulations; removing sodium does not remove the other risks.
[RAVICTI US label, sections 8.4 and 12](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=900e7dc0-9afe-4f50-80de-90567bb78519)

The adjacent arithmetic receipt converts a reported healthy-adult mean total
PBA peak of 100 micrograms/mL to approximately 609 micromolar using the acid
molecular mass. Applying the label's binding-range endpoints gives an
illustrative 12�116-micromolar free range, **not measured free exposure**.
The 2,000-micromolar nominal culture example is about 3.28 times that reported
total peak. This is neither an exposure ceiling nor a free-exposure ratio:
medium binding, intracellular exposure, duration and pediatric values remain
unknown. No dose is inferred.
[NIST identity and molecular mass](https://webbook.nist.gov/cgi/cbook.cgi?Name=4-Phenylbutyric+acid)

**The NAD branch has context and cancer contradictions.** The direct
SIRT2/BUBR1 paper used NMN for abundance experiments; its hypomorphic-mouse
lifespan extension came from SIRT2 transgenesis, not an NMN treatment trial.
A separate melanoma study found SIRT1 knockdown lowered BUB proteins while
SIRT2 knockdown did not. This limits a universal SIRT2-maintenance story;
it does not establish that this child's cells resemble melanoma.
The SIRT2 target-site result is K668, not the newly introduced K1002. A stable
N1002R comparator would not prove modification-driven turnover or choose a
NAD medicine; residue chemistry can alter several mechanisms at once.
The [mechanism comparison](balanced_function.md) corrects that inference in
the frozen baseline while preserving its original bytes.
[North et al., 2014](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24825348/),
[Singh et al., 2014, Figure 6](https://www.oncotarget.com/article/1898/text/)

A 2025 AACR conference abstract by Kumari and North reports increased
UV-induced skin-tumor multiplicity with prolonged NMN in mice. This is a
primary conference report with limited methods, not a full peer-reviewed
MVA safety study. In contrast, a separate peer-reviewed mouse study found
higher tissue NAD without altered UV-tumor development. These results
neither prove nicotinic-acid harm nor establish cancer safety. They block
the claim that raising NAD or BUBR1 amount inherently reduces cancer risk.
[AACR abstract 4168](https://doi.org/10.1158/1538-7445.AM2025-4168),
[Pihl et al., 2025](https://doi.org/10.1007/s43630-025-00736-5)

**GGA has human pharmacodynamic evidence, but the needed response is not
universal.** A small adult cardiac-surgery study found atrial HSPB1/HSPA1
induction. An HCV cell study's supporting experiments did not find HSP90 or
HSP70 induction in its hepatoma system. These are different contexts, not a
head-to-head conflict. Require the actual relevant basal response, rather
than borrowing a gastric, cardiac or catalog claim.
[van Marion et al., 2020](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.hrthm.2019.07.010),
[Ikeda et al., 2011, supporting Figure S4](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1478-3231.2011.02499.x),
[PMDA medicinal product listing](https://www.pmda.go.jp/PmdaSearch/rdDetail/iyaku/2329012C1026_1?user=1)

## Do not mistake the next faithful division for a clinical surrogate

MVA mouse models with different alleles showed distinct tissue phenotypes
despite similarly reduced BUBR1. Culture aneuploidy did not explain all
phenotypic heterogeneity. The study's mouse L1002P models human L1012P;
neither is the proposed human N1002K. An identical residue number is not an
allele match. A tissue-relevant functional benefit and preserved fitness
constraints must follow the division assay; an in-vitro error reduction is
not a validated surrogate for the child's outcome.
[Primary allelic-effects study, 2019](https://www.jci.org/articles/view/126863)

This also prevents importing progeria medicines solely from a shared aging
phenotype. A primary UPCP study acts through progerin/PTBP1/BUBR1 in HGPS;
it is a mechanistic precedent for transcript/localization control, not an
approved MVA medicine or evidence for lonafarnib in this genotype. Rapamycin
used to trigger an engineered dimerization assay is likewise not evidence
that native rapamycin repairs BUBR1 attachment function.
[UPCP study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s43587-023-00361-w),
[engineered PP2A assay](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-58185-8)

## First experiment and the decision it buys

Do not erase partial mechanistic rescue when excluding a drug class from
advancement. Aurora inhibition improved alignment in engineered HeLa
systems; a separate genetic local-recruitment experiment improved alignment
in MVA fibroblasts. Neither demonstrated completed faithful division in this
allele. Aurora perturbation is therefore a possible assay benchmark, not an
advanced medicine. Preserved nocodazole arrest and aligned chromosomes can
miss other checkpoint or attachment-correction defects. The
[endpoint audit](faithful_completion.md) separates those experiments and
adds the required functional comparison. It also records a neural BubR1-loss
mouse result in which reducing apoptosis did not provide full tissue rescue.
Improved survival alone cannot select a treatment.

1. In accessible, independently characterized renewable models, qualify
   protein/function measurements, the intended basal state, exposure and
   controls. Identify input/recovery for turnover claims; a qualified
   phenotypic pilot can proceed in parallel. Measure division
   completion as well as errors; image counts do not create biological replicates.
2. A recoverable supply defect can justify testing a mechanism class, but
   cannot qualify either named drug. Measure each compound's own relevant
   response, functional effect and exposure/time profile. Failure under the
   inherited nominal screen rule rejects that tested condition; it is not
   a verdict on every clinical exposure or the whole mechanism. Above-cap
   results cannot pass the frozen screen, and a different exposure hypothesis
   requires independent prespecification. Imposed heat shock cannot replace
   basal responsiveness. See the current treatment-evidence decision.
3. For mature-maintenance loss, first test whether the relevant NAD/SIRT2
   link actually controls useful BUBR1. A precursor-induced NAD rise without
   target/function response fails that proposed mechanistic chain under
   the tested conditions. It does not automatically exclude another useful
   phenotypic mechanism. Nicotinic acid needs its own exposure and safety bridge.
4. Advance only after exact-genotype confirmation, appropriate correction
   comparators and independent replication. Require function in the relevant
   tissue/state, faithful completed divisions, daughter-genotype/fate
   qualification, and justified uncertainty bounds on abnormal-clone fitness.
   An uninformative experiment holds the decision; it is not an efficacy negative.

Assay feasibility must determine material requirements, timing, uncertainty,
power and margins before an exact-genotype study. This desk audit has not
established any of those laboratory parameters.
