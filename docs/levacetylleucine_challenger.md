# Levacetylleucine: a stronger human-function analogy to test

Public-source research supplement · 23 October 2026 · no demonstrated MVA benefit.

**Levacetylleucine (N-acetyl-L-leucine, NALL) earns a place beside phenylbutyrate and elamipretide.** Its strongest new evidence is randomized improvement of established neurological function in ataxia-telangiectasia (A–T), a separate inherited genome-maintenance disorder. This is a stronger clinical analogy than an antioxidant marker. ATM DNA-damage signaling and BUBR1 attachment/checkpoint control remain different mechanisms; neither ataxia nor a matching reversible neuronal constraint is established in this child. No overall drug rank follows.

## What the human trial establishes

FDA approved Aqneursa for **ataxia in adults and children with A–T weighing at least 15 kg on 18 September 2026**. The indication does not cover every consequence of A–T. The 73-participant randomized, double-blind crossover trial used consecutive 12-week periods. The primary publication reports an original SARA treatment effect of **−1.88 points, 95% CI −2.70 to −1.06**. The September label reports a distinct **fSARA** effect of **−0.6 points, 95% CI −0.9 to −0.2**. These are different scales/analyses of the same trial, not independent replications or percentages of recovery. [FDA announcement](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/news-events-human-drugs/fda-approves-therapy-treat-ataxia-patients-ataxia-telangiectasia-rare-genetic-disorder), [Martakis et al., 2026](https://doi.org/10.1016/S1474-4422(26)00158-4), [current label, section 14.2](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=0f248e55-d1bb-13f9-e063-6294a90a05ce)

Immediate crossover lacked an intervening washout. A short parent half-life does not establish absent biological carryover; the label says the molecular target and pharmacodynamic time course are unknown. Both treatment sequences support an on-treatment response, while aggregate means cannot resolve period/carryover effects. The full SAP and regulatory endpoint-selection timing were not retrieved in this review. Original SARA and modified fSARA both favor drug; the difference is not grounds to allege endpoint fishing. Trial participants had assessable ataxia and functional eligibility criteria; extrapolation to different deficits remains unresolved. [Registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06673056), [label, sections 12 and 14](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=0f248e55-d1bb-13f9-e063-6294a90a05ce)

The trial demonstrates symptomatic function, not survival, cancer prevention, chromosome correction or long-term disease modification. The publication reserves potential neuroprotection/disease modification for continuing investigation. A separate 60-person NPC crossover trial also showed neurological-function benefit; it cannot supply a BUB1B response. No treatment-related serious event in the short A–T trial does not establish long-term safety during inherited CIN. [NPC trial](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2310151)

## Actual neuronal function, with important contrary findings

Song et al. measured action-potential-evoked synaptic pHluorin recovery in human GBA1-mutant dopaminergic neurons: mean time constants were approximately **105 versus 133 seconds** with/without NALL. This is a real vesicle-handling response, although fluorescence decay includes retrieval and reacidification and is not direct proof of improved transmitter release or network computation. The functional arm used **10 mM for two weeks**. Its 59/89 observations are synaptic ROIs, not independent donors. LRRK2-neuron and mouse motor-learning positives add support; the mouse caption and Methods disagree on administration route. [Song et al., 2026](https://doi.org/10.1172/JCI196137)

Our [executed public-workbook reanalysis](nall_source_data_audit.md) reproduces the main counts/means. Its printed dispersion matches SEM rather than the caption's SD label; the supplemental LRRK2 caption disagrees with its labeled source sheet. Missing culture/run/donor identifiers prevent cluster-aware uncertainty, and available traces lack the recovery interval needed to refit decay. These findings qualify the cell evidence without refuting the separate human A–T result.

The label's mean total plasma peak of 8.3 microgram/mL and molecular weight 173.21 imply about 47.9 micromolar: the nominal 10 mM culture is approximately **209 times that total peak**. This arithmetic is not a human free-brain estimate, an exposure ceiling, or a refutation of the actual A–T clinical effect. It challenges copying that culture mechanism into a clinically qualified BUB1B profile. NALL is the L-enantiomer; the older DL racemate cannot inherit its evidence. [Current label, sections 11–12](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=0f248e55-d1bb-13f9-e063-6294a90a05ce)

Rehm et al. found protection of neurite length during simultaneous nocodazole injury at 1–5 mM, but **no enhanced recovery after injury**. ATP/stress responses varied between healthy and MS neurons. That is meaningful negative evidence against a generic repair claim. Microtubule injury is not a BUBR1 lesion; neither positive nor negative establishes MVA response. [Rehm et al., 2026](https://doi.org/10.3389/fimmu.2026.1866462)

## A bounded comparison

The revised [neural comparison](neural_protection_comparison.md) uses two identified genotypes × **vehicle, one PBA profile, one elamipretide profile and one NALL profile**. Eight conditions assume a common assay-compatible solvent background; retain separate vehicle controls and revise the count if matching is infeasible. NAC remains a protective comparator elsewhere and an optional mechanistic follow-up, not an established positive control for this neural assay.

Freeze drug-specific measured profiles before the efficacy comparison. A post-deficit response, useful evoked output, survival/coverage and precursor/lineage accounting distinguish symptomatic benefit from marker changes or nonspecific suppression. Confirm at most one selected condition independently. Persistence after withdrawal is a separate property; a reversible on-treatment benefit can still be useful. None of these biological comparisons has been performed.

## Other new routes were challenged rather than quietly discarded

**Bumetanide:** a human trisomy-21 study reduced abnormal GABA-evoked calcium responses at 10 micromolar, using five differentiations from one mosaic donor. Direct circuit rescue was in mice. The human observation is not direct GABA-reversal or useful-output rescue. Restricted measured rat brain distribution, uncertain human free CNS exposure and two negative pediatric autism phase 3 trials prevent a generic developmental-benefit claim. This route requires a demonstrated limiting chloride defect and exposure-qualified functional rescue; lower activity alone fails. [Human/mouse primary](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isci.2024.109438), [measured regional PK](https://doi.org/10.1002/bdd.2390), [phase 3 trials](https://doi.org/10.1002/aur.3005)

**Nucleoside salvage:** genuine segregation/daughter-survival positives exist in replication-stressed cancer and pluripotent-cell models. The actual four/five-component research mixtures differ from marketed dC+dT/Kygevvi. Other primary experiments found accelerated forks with increased bridges and micronuclei. Secondary replication stress after aneuploidy need not mean substrate shortage; it can reflect limiting replication machinery or a protective slow state. Neither faster replication nor approval for TK2 deficiency qualifies BUB1B rescue. [Burrell](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature11935), [Halliwell](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.stemcr.2020.04.004), [counterevidence](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44319-024-00207-5), [FDA product identity](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-trials-snapshots/drug-trials-snapshots-kygevvi)
