# Restore the balance of a division, not just the amount of protein

Joseph Mayo — hypothesis challenge, updated 23 October 2026.

## The sharper hypothesis

The proposed missense allele may limit **two coupled functions differently**:
the signal that holds the checkpoint and the attachment/release machinery
that allows an accurate division to finish. Its defect could be insufficient
delivery, defective local coordination, or both. A medicine succeeds only if
it restores the useful balance at the relevant stage. An increased protein
band, stronger checkpoint or longer arrest cannot choose among these worlds.

This is an untested inference for N1002K, not an established lesion. It
strengthens the early-delivery hypothesis by giving it a specific rival that
can survive normalization of protein abundance.
The supply and coordination defects can coexist; the comparison must allow
a mixed result instead of forcing a binary mechanism assignment.

## Why this goes beyond a generic chaperone story

The C-terminal-domain study already identified in the prior work links
domain integrity to KARD phosphorylation, PP2A-B56 recruitment, alignment
and checkpoint release. Its engineered proximity experiment rescued a
phosphorylation readout by bringing N- and C-terminal fragments together;
simply coexpressing untethered fragments did not. That is a research tool,
not evidence that an approved dimerizing drug repairs the native allele.
[Gama Braga et al., 2020, Figures 4–5](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2020.108397)

The 2023 feedback study supplies the important counterprediction: skewing
the BUBR1-associated PLK1/PP2A balance in opposite directions caused
different errors. Excess kinase bias strengthened the checkpoint while
destabilizing attachments; excess phosphatase bias weakened the checkpoint
and made attachments too stable. This was engineered cell biology, not an
N1002K experiment. **Our inference is that "more" can move one readout in a
favorable direction while moving the coupled function in an unfavorable
direction.** Raising protein or globally activating PP2A cannot be presumed
to repair that balance.
[Corno et al., 2023](https://doi.org/10.15252/embj.2022112630)

The relevant MVA precedent is older and more direct than the engineered
retargeting comparison below. Xu et al. restored alignment by genetic local
B56 recruitment in two established MVA fibroblast lines. Their chemical
Aurora-inhibition experiment was in HeLa systems, not those MVA lines; both
used an imposed metaphase block for the reported endpoint. These results
support downstream sufficiency for alignment, not faithful division or a
medicine response in this proposed allele.
[Xu et al., 2013, Figures 3–4](https://doi.org/10.1242/bio.20134051)

## The discriminating experiment

A direct target precedent provides a useful calibration. In the 2012 human
BUBR1 study, permissive culture temperatures restored abundance and a
checkpoint readout for a destabilized K795A construct. This was neither
N1002K nor a medicine result. A proposed **permissive-history / normal-
temperature readout** comparison could ask whether an appropriately supplied
unchanged protein can function after return to the intended assay conditions.
It would require matched WT/correction histories, qualified recovery and
cell-cycle trajectories, and enough retained protein for interpretation.
An apparent benefit observed only during slow, cool mitosis is not evidence
of function at the intended conditions. Failure to retain protein after
return makes this comparison unresolved. Temperature also changes synthesis,
clearance and cell state, so even a positive would not identify folding alone.
This is an experimental design proposal in renewable cells, not a patient
intervention. [Suijkerbuijk et al., 2012, Figure 3 and supplement](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.devcel.2012.03.009)

First establish that unchanged N1002K has a measurable defect, using the
qualified correction/carrier comparisons in the report. In renewable models,
compare WT and unchanged missense protein at appropriately matched,
physiological abundance and cell-cycle stage. Measure localization,
partner recruitment and phosphorylation per qualified protein pool as well
as checkpoint hold, release, attachments and completed faithful divisions.
Normalize neither a mixture of stages nor an unqualified peptide signal.

If abundance is normalized but local recruitment and release remain
defective, a **genetic bypass of recruitment** can distinguish a repairable
downstream bottleneck from a general protein-supply problem. A primary study
rescued alignment in a PP2A-binding-deficient BUBR1 system by recruiting B56
to KNL1 instead. It provides a tool precedent, not this allele's rescue.
[Smith et al., 2019, Figure 3](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2019.07.067)

A qualified bypass comparison needs controlled recruitment, matched
untargeted and recruitment-defective controls, and a way to distinguish
intended phosphatase activity from a tether/scaffold artifact. Test both
insufficient and excessive recruitment; a single maximum-expression result
cannot establish a useful range. Restore attachment/release **without**
losing checkpoint hold or increasing errors. The assay must retain the
division-completion, daughter-fate and abnormal-clone safeguards.

Recruitment amount is still not recruitment regulation. Smith's experiments
show that direct tethering removes phosphorylation-dependent recruitment
and changes what the phosphatase can do. A static alignment rescue therefore
cannot establish restoration of native feedback. Compare recruitment and
release across stages, and distinguish a constitutive tether from a validated
reversible perturbation if temporal sufficiency is being tested. Without
qualified on/off kinetics, do not infer a timing mechanism. This makes
**restoring the regulated sequence of functions**, rather than maximizing
local phosphatase, the meaningful mechanistic target.

This tests downstream sufficiency. Even a positive cannot prove that the
original lesion was folding, that the construct is a therapy, or that a
global phosphatase-activating medicine will reproduce its localized action.
Only feasible, informative comparisons should consume scarce material.

The sharper functional question is whether correct attachments can persist
while incorrect ones are removed before release. A short-term nocodazole
response can survive an Aurora perturbation that compromises another
checkpoint response. Separately, merotelic attachments can evade the
checkpoint and alignment can coexist with segregation errors. Qualify
unattached-kinetochore and microtubule-present challenge cohorts, then
measure actual error resolution and basal faithful completion. Neither a
single arrest response nor cold-stable fibers establishes biorientation.
The [figure-level endpoint audit](faithful_completion.md) specifies the
published counterexamples and the proposed comparison, including the
limits of imaging and imposed challenges. No new assay has been run.

The medicine comparison can then test **different ways of perturbing the
same bottleneck**. Phenylbutyrate has precedent in the cytosolic Munc18-1
system, so its rationale is not limited to ER-trafficking disorders. It is
a challenger to arimoclomol's stress-contingent network hypothesis. Measure
RNA/nascent input, cohort delivery, actual cellular response and balanced
function independently: a transcription-driven increase must not be called
chemical folding rescue. A difference between drugs alone cannot establish
either mechanism or direct BUBR1 binding. Each needs its own exposure bridge.
[Munc18-1 primary study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-018-06507-4)

The chaperone bridge must also be productive, not merely a larger stress
response. A primary reconstituted-client study found that Hsp70 could retain
clients in a folding-incompetent state until transfer to Hsp90. That study
did not test this allele or arimoclomol; it is a counterexample to assuming
that more of one chaperone necessarily means more folded product. If a drug
raises chaperone-associated protein without delivering qualified, balanced
function, hold the bridge. Chaperone association can mark productive
assistance or a stalled client, and cannot settle that distinction alone.
[Morán Luengo et al., 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29706537/)

| Observation | What changes in the hypothesis and drug decision |
|---|---|
| Normalizing unchanged missense amount restores balanced function | Supply rescue remains plausible; the measured early versus mature loss selects a probe class |
| Amount restored; recruitment/release remains defective; qualified local bypass improves both without weakening hold | Prioritize a local coordination defect; retire amount alone as a sufficient mechanism; no medicine is yet qualified |
| More amount strengthens hold but attachment/release or daughter outcomes worsen | Stop that candidate condition; do not call longer arrest rescue |
| Local bypass restores alignment but hold or errors worsen | Reject that bypass as sufficient; alignment alone is a false success |
| Correction helps but neither appropriate abundance nor qualified bypass helps | Reopen other domain/complex functions and the stop-restoration route |
| Comparison, exposure or normalization is unqualified | Mechanism unresolved; no efficacy conclusion |

## An alternative I tested against the literature and did not promote

### A new lysine is a mechanism question, not a mechanism assignment

The frozen baseline proposed N1002R as a comparator and said that a stable
Arg replacement would identify modification-driven turnover rather than
misfolding. That inference is too strong and is not adopted here. Lys and
Arg differ chemically; changing the residue can alter structure, interactions
and clearance together. A stable comparator would narrow possibilities,
not identify their causal order. Folding defects and enhanced modification
can also coexist.

The published SIRT2 work identifies a different residue, K668. It does not
show that SIRT2 acts on an introduced K1002, or that new-lysine turnover would
select a NAD precursor. A modification hypothesis needs qualified site-level
evidence relative to protein amount, a causal perturbation that preserves the
intended protein sequence, and restored useful function. More modification
could be a consequence of defective protein rather than its initiating cause.
[North et al., 2014, Figure 3](https://doi.org/10.15252/embj.201386907)

If remnant proteomics is used, a diglycine-lysine signal is not automatically
ubiquitin-specific: NEDD8 and ISG15 can contribute. Primary work used
additional discrimination controls. Neither enrichment intensity nor global
proteasome inhibition alone establishes a target-specific causal pathway.
No K1002 modification or response has been measured here.
[Kim et al., 2011, Figure 2](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21906983/)

### Recovering the truncated product

A tempting idea is to rescue the stop allele's RNA and use the truncated
protein, avoiding the missense allele altogether. The older 731X replacement
construct supported several mitotic functions. But its name hides a sequence
qualification: the paper says it contains only 715 reference residues; it is
not the exact 1–736 product predicted here. The newer domain work reports
recruitment/release deficits in truncation constructs. Therefore the frozen
report's categorical statement that NMD-only recovery "is not a rescue
route" should be read as **not qualified as sufficient**, rather than proof
that the exact untested product has zero residual function. Its own exact
product/function comparison would be required; do not promote NMD inhibition
from the old checkpoint result.
[Suijkerbuijk et al., 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20516114/)

Amlexanox has positive NMD/readthrough cell literature, but a separate CF
organoid study found it ineffective under its tested conditions. Current
marketed systemic eligibility was not verified here. Digoxin's primary NMD
study expressly notes its effective experimental concentrations exceeded
standard clinical exposure. Neither is promoted to a repositioning lead.
Global RNA surveillance and possible truncated-product interference add
separate questions; the Leu-restoring genetic comparator remains distinct.
[Amlexanox primary study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22938201/),
[negative organoid study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7794695/),
[digoxin NMD study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25064126/)

## What the entry can responsibly contribute

An allele-specific way to decide whether a medicine must increase functional
protein supply or repair the coordination of functions at the chromosome.
It can falsify a seductive drug story before spending scarce material.
Published tool designs and the pre-existing domain hypothesis are credited;
the new contribution here is their application as a decision for this
proposed pair, not invention of those tools or a demonstrated rescue.

The next-stage program must still establish relevant tissue function and
independent replication. No new biological experiment, usable therapeutic
window or clinical benefit has been demonstrated by this desk research.
