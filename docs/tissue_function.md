# Recover useful neural output without exhausting the source

Research supplement · 23 October 2026 · unsigned

**Secondary hypothesis:** a reversible extracellular constraint could limit
useful neural output alongside a BUBR1 defect. Test the tissue state and the
direction of that constraint before choosing a medicine. This is not evidence
of an exact-human-allele rescue, developmental reversal or benefit to the child.

## The positive evidence contains two different experiments

In BubR1 hypomorphic mice, constitutive Sfrp3 loss improved selected body/brain
and corpus-callosum myelin measures. That is a developmental genetic bypass.
Separately, adult dentate-gyrus Sfrp3 knockdown increased labeling during a
two-hour EdU pulse 14 days after intervention. The latter did not establish
completed faithful divisions, durable mature neurons, cognition or myelin
repair. Most experiments used females; the single-animal WT/Sfrp3-KO control
concerns gross morphology, not every endpoint. These are important limits on
the developmental comparison. [Cho et al., 2019](https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.12899)

Separate adult dentate-gyrus work identified mature granule neurons as a
source of sFRP3 and showed that loss of this signal activates quiescent stem
cells. Protein add-back opposed activity-induced proliferation. Human FRZB
encodes sFRP3; an SFRP1 inhibitor is not evidence for this target. This source
identity is not established as the explanation for Cho's developmental myelin
effect. Nor is pathological FRZB overproduction established in MVA: removing
a normal brake can compensate for a different lesion.
[Jang et al., 2013](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.stem.2012.11.021)

A separate follow-up found lower GFAP-positive astrocyte density and selected
Iba1/CD68 activation measures in constitutive genetic crosses at 8–10 weeks.
Total Iba1-positive density was not significantly different; the untreated
activation increase was a trend, and reduction differed by region/genotype.
This supports measuring glial state, not claiming restored glial function or
reversal after gliosis exists. These outcomes belong to this 2020 paper,
not Cho's 2019 experiment.
[Corujo-Ramirez et al., 2020](https://doi.org/10.5213/inj.2040436.218)

The opposite direction can be beneficial. In a seizure-active hAPP mouse
model, restoring reduced sFRP3 restrained early overdivision, preserved the
stem-cell pool and improved spatial discrimination; immature-cell counts did
not increase. This is a different disease state, not an MVA treatment result.
It makes precursor reserve and durable output necessary endpoints alongside
division rate. [Fu et al., 2025](https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.0049-25.2025)

## A causal experiment after the deficit exists

Use renewable material to establish a relevant low-output neural phenotype
and its time course. A low-wild-type-BUBR1 model qualifies an assay, not
N1002K function. Exact-allele claims still require nucleotide identity, phase,
endogenous expression and independent correction/recreation events.

In a competent neuronal system, compare reciprocal variant/corrected source
and responder cells. Hold responder genotype constant while perturbing FRZB
in a qualified source compartment after the deficit is present. Confirm a
secreted-target response, use independent target-specific perturbations and
include physiologically qualified protein add-back. Conditioned medium alone
cannot identify FRZB, and a supraphysiological opposing signal is not a
specific causal rescue.

The useful outcome is sustained production of surviving, functionally mature
descendants per starting lineage, with precursor reserve preserved. Require
improvement of an abnormal endpoint and preservation of already-normal ones,
including division fidelity and daughter outcomes. Evoked neural function
must improve without pathological hyperexcitability. EdU alone fails.

| Result | Decision |
|---|---|
| Durable mature output improves, reserve and fidelity are preserved, and specific add-back reverses the effect | Qualify this extracellular bypass in the tested state; then test a medicine's own target response and exposure |
| EdU increases but mature output does not, or errors/exhaustion increase | Reject the apparent proliferation rescue |
| Corrected source improves output but qualified FRZB perturbation does not | Retain a possible niche effect; deprioritize FRZB under those conditions |
| BUB1B correction restores the phenotype but FRZB perturbation does not | Deprioritize this bypass; correction could also affect another extracellular signal, so intracellular localization is not established |
| Early overactivation precedes precursor depletion | Reject the low-output premise for that state and investigate the required direction separately |

Qualify the glial question separately. BubR1-low mice had reduced precursor
proliferation and fewer oligodendrocytes, not an isolated maturation block.
After a verified myelin deficit exists, distinguish precursor scarcity from
differentiation arrest. Measure compact myelin/nodes, ensheathment and
conduction alongside reserve, with relevant multicellular support. A neuronal
co-culture cannot establish myelin rescue.
[Choi et al., 2016](https://doi.org/10.18632/aging.101032)

## The new evidence does not promote a medicine

| Medicine | Why it remains unqualified for this route |
|---|---|
| Fluoxetine | Mouse and rat studies report opposite FRZB directions; neonatal rat precursor cultures show inhibited oligodendrocyte differentiation. No BubR1 pharmacological rescue or qualified free-brain target-response exposure |
| Levetiracetam | Restoring FRZB in a seizure-active hAPP state is a different intervention direction; no preventive MVA rationale follows |
| Clemastine | Another genetic model's differentiation bottleneck differs; developing WT mice had more differentiated oligodendrocytes but poorer conduction/myelination. Marker improvement is insufficient |

The conflicting fluoxetine directions are retained rather than averaged away.
[Jang et al., 2013](https://doi.org/10.1038/mp.2012.158),
[Okamoto et al., 2010](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.biopsych.2010.04.023),
[Yin et al., 2024](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12264-024-01249-4)
The clemastine functional counterexample is developmental WT mouse work,
not a clinical risk estimate for MVA. Its dose-reporting discrepancy is not
used to set an exposure threshold.
[Palma et al., 2022](https://doi.org/10.3389/fcell.2022.841548)

Arimoclomol and formulation-specific phenylbutyrate are unranked exploratory
compounds; no treatment lead is selected. No medicine becomes effective by
association with this genetic finding. Each needs its own
eligibility, exposure, causal response and useful functional improvement.
