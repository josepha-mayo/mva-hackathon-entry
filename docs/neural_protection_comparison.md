# Phenylbutyrate versus elamipretide: protect useful neural function

Public-source research decision, 23 October 2026. **4-PBA now has the closer actual-drug human aneuploid-neuron stress experiment; elamipretide has distinct mitochondrial and functional-animal evidence. Neither has demonstrated useful neural rescue during BUB1B failure.** The next commitment is a direct, bounded functional comparison, not a clinical drug ranking or combination.

The improved hypothesis is that surviving neural cells may have a modifiable secondary protein-stress or mitochondrial constraint, even while the upstream chromosome-instability defect persists. That constraint is not established in this child. This is separate from assuming that phenylbutyrate binds, stabilizes or repairs the N1002K BUBR1 protein. The existing limited ataluren arm asks a different, stop-allele question.

## What each compound actually contributes

| Question | Sodium 4-phenylbutyrate / parent PBA | Elamipretide / SS31 |
|---|---|---|
| Closest drug evidence | Human trisomy-13/21 NGN2-neuron aggregate, ER-stress and apoptosis-marker improvement | Neuron-specific mitochondrial redox response in living mice; separate animal behavior/neurovascular positives |
| Closest CIN connection | Actual compound tested in human constitutional aneuploid neurons | Fly checkpoint-instability genetics support mitochondrial rescue, but did not test this medicine |
| Useful-function gap | No drug-treated evoked neural-function or post-deficit recovery result in the proximal study | No BUB1B functional rescue; relevant human retinal primary endpoints failed |
| Exposure gap | No qualified free neuronal profile; CSF entry and brain tracer observations differ | No qualified free human neural profile; tissue enrichment and neuronal pharmacodynamics cannot supply it |
| Mistaken success signal | Fewer aggregates or less caspase staining means recovery | Lower mitochondrial oxidation or larger brains means recovery |

Hirata et al. used **100 micromolar PBA from neural induction through day 14**, not the millimolar exposure used in some other target-protein studies. Aggregate, ER-stress and cleaved-caspase signals improved. The main drug endpoint was relative caspase intensity; methods describe NeuN counts without a disclosed drug-treated count result in the main or six supplementary figures. No electrical function was measured. NGN2 rapidly drove cell-cycle exit, so altered induction/maturation and prevention remain possible. Stable trisomy is not ongoing complex mosaic CIN. Corrected-disomy comparisons support the underlying phenotype, not a BUB1B-specific drug response. [Primary study and supplements](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-020-70362-x)

Alternative HDAC inhibitors were not matched for target engagement, and partial opposition by 3-MA does not isolate autophagy. PBA's beneficial mechanism therefore remains unresolved. A response could be useful without settling chemical chaperoning, but it cannot be reported as proven BUBR1 stabilization.

For SS31, a living-mouse neuronal redox reporter improved and dystrophic neurites decreased despite unchanged amyloid plaque burden. That supports a modifiable downstream lesion. The related-peptide comparator, disease context and cranial-window surgery qualify the inference: neuronal pharmacodynamics was observed, while intact-barrier delivery, free neuronal concentration and useful neural computation were not established. [Calvo-Rodriguez et al., 2024](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13024-024-00702-2)

The human counterevidence is closer than muscle studies alone. ReCLAIM-2 randomized 176 participants and missed its acuity and geographic-atrophy primaries, retaining nominal exploratory structural/responder positives and differential attrition. A 12-person chronic LHON trial missed primary acuity and objective ganglion-cell electrophysiology, retaining post-hoc/extension positives. Disease, delivery route and irreversible prior loss differ from MVA. Two adult neurological mitochondrial-disease cases also reported functional gains, but were uncontrolled. None establishes a BUB1B benefit or a universal failure. [ReCLAIM-2](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xops.2024.100628), [LHON](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ophtha.2023.10.033), [case series](https://doi.org/10.1002/ccr3.9591)

The [mitochondrial evidence card](mitochondrial_challenger.md) retains the oocyte developmental-output positive, aged-mouse cognitive/neurovascular findings, sex-dependent nonresponse, Barth evidence and MMPOWER-3 failure. These positives remain reasons to compare SS31, rather than treating its trial negatives as evidence for PBA.

PBA also cannot borrow a general human neuroprotection claim. The 664-participant PHOENIX trial of **PBA plus taurursodiol** missed its primary functional endpoint; the sponsor subsequently discontinued the ALS combination. The primary conference report is not a PBA-alone MVA trial and cannot identify which component failed. It nevertheless counters treating ER/mitochondrial protection as already clinically validated. Marketed UCD phenylbutyrate products remain a separate medicinal use. [Primary conference results](https://doi.org/10.1002/mus.28259), [sponsor regulatory filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001658551/000095017025057669/amlx-ars-2025.pdf)

## Challenge exposure, formulation and safety in both directions

PBA's adult plasma peaks and pediatric urea-cycle-disorder use do not establish the neural exposure needed here. Its short plasma half-life differs from replenished 14-day culture, but does not exclude a durable response to a brief exposure. Small primate intravenous experiments found variable CSF access; baboon tracer imaging found very low brain uptake. Different dose, route, compartment and analyte prevent converting either into a human neuronal concentration or a categorical no-entry claim. [Current sodium-product label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=0c76d25b-abac-41a3-a547-4fa4796bf7a4), [CSF study](https://doi.org/10.1007/s002800000256), [brain tracer study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3769509/)

Measure parent **PBA** and metabolite **phenylacetate/PAA** separately. Glycerol phenylbutyrate requires hydrolysis; intact prodrug in culture is not automatically equivalent to parent PBA. A neural culture also does not reproduce hepatic PAA formation. The EMA label records neurological toxicity from high PAA exposure and developmental animal findings, alongside negative sodium-PBA Ames and rat micronucleus tests. These do not establish therapeutic oral-PBA toxicity or safety in inherited CIN. [Formulation and binding](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=900e7dc0-9afe-4f50-80de-90567bb78519), [Pheburane assessment](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/pheburane-epar-product-information_en.pdf)

SS31 peak plasma comparisons likewise cannot represent sustained culture or neural exposure. FDA animal distribution found minimal brain/eye amounts; large isolated-mitochondrial enrichment is not a BBB partition coefficient. The neuronal redox positive prevents dismissing CNS action solely from homogenate or CSF limitations. PLSCR3-dependent protection in kidney models also prevents equating SS31 with genetic Hsp60/Sod2 rescue. [FDA review](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2025/215244Orig1s000IntegratedR.pdf), [PLSCR3 study](https://doi.org/10.1681/ASN.0000000000000338)

## One comparison capable of choosing a research condition

Use an available renewable genotype/corrective-reference neural pair, identifying the actual correction. Stop-only repair is WT/N1002K, not automatically a fully corrected reference. Phenotype and initial response can be established together. **Recovery requires a documented deficit before dosing**; prevention of further loss is a separate valid question.

After a short, fixed exposure scout, the shared module contains **two genotypes × four assigned conditions: vehicle, one PBA profile, one elamipretide profile and one NAC comparator profile**. Eight conditions precede biological replication. The scout selects interpretable drug-specific profiles using free-medium and intracellular measurements and compact target-response checks. Equal nominal molarity, equal plasma peaks and one arbitrary concentration do not make fair comparisons. The 100-micromolar PBA literature condition is an anchor, not a selected clinical concentration. A single failed profile cannot eliminate a drug or class.

The shared functional endpoint is a prespecified evoked neural response per randomized well, interpreted with survival, coverage and starting-cell accounting. Keep total functional output and function per surviving neuron distinct. Prefer a neuron-enriched postmitotic preparation if its deficit persists there. In precursor cultures additionally measure functional descendants per enrolled precursor, remaining reserve, division errors and abnormal-lineage expansion. More activity caused by excessive bursting fails the useful-function criterion. A postmitotic benefit need not repair every chromosome or raise BUBR1.

Use one compact supporting readout per mechanism—protein-stress handling for PBA, mitochondrial function for SS31—without making either the efficacy endpoint. Independent preparations/origins and runs, randomized wells and masked scoring support inference; cells do not create extra donors.

Choose a condition from a **direct between-drug functional contrast**, useful-effect uncertainty, survival/reserve tradeoffs and exposure plausibility. A positive P value for one drug and a negative P value for the other is not a significant difference. Confirm at most one selected profile in a fresh comparison, including response persistence after washout. A tie remains a tie. Marker-only improvement fails; an underexposed negative remains unresolved. No biological screen has been performed here.

This design replaces the prior two-elamipretide-exposure neural module. It can occupy the single appropriate tissue-module slot alongside the limited cycling-cell experiment; it does not require every ciliary, barrier and neural platform or justify a clinical cocktail.
