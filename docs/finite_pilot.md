# A finite first decision: useful output before mechanism completion

Joseph Mayo · MVA 2026 Track 2 · 23 October 2026. **Proposed laboratory work only.** No exact-genotype rescue, wet experiment, model availability, exposure window, patient benefit, or clinical dose is established. A cell biologist and analytical scientist must review feasibility and freeze the remaining assay parameters. This research design uses public sources; no controlled participant material was accessed.

**Recommendation:** nominate a small ataluren feasibility arm alongside one phenotype-matched downstream arm. Do not make ataluren success or failure the entrance requirement for a qualified downstream experiment. In an appropriate renewable proliferating model, compare ataluren with vehicle and NAC using useful daughter output per starting founder. In parallel, run **one** ciliary, barrier or mitochondrial-neural module when an appropriate renewable platform is available. The first controlled pilot may establish the relevant phenotype and drug response together using concurrent vehicle and corrective references. A restoration claim requires a measurable deficit; a completed prior phenotype study or perfect mechanism is not an entrance requirement. If the relevant platform is unavailable, the comparison is not yet executable; a generic fibroblast division result cannot rank every tissue-support hypothesis.

The decision is which defined drug–function bridge deserves confirmation and, eventually, scarce material. It is not which medicine a child should receive. The published BUBR1 allele studies distinguish deficient amount from deficient function, and different alleles produce different organismal phenotypes. Neither a normal protein band nor a culture chromosome endpoint is an established clinical surrogate. [Suijkerbuijk 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20516114/), [primary allelic-effects study](https://www.jci.org/articles/view/126863).

## 1. Why ataluren deserves an arm, not an exclusive funnel

There are useful positives and credible alternatives. In SMC1A patient lymphoblastoid cells, ataluren increased protein/partner association and improved a selected-metaphase structural-aberration endpoint; this is a different gene and does not measure all starting lineages. Conversely, ataluren changed metabolism and reduced proliferation in FANCA **missense** as well as nonsense models, with proliferation effects also in controls. A cleaner chromosome sample can therefore accompany a non-readthrough effect. [Di Nardo 2026](https://doi.org/10.1002/epi.70150), [Balbi 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41420-026-02983-6).

UGAA and low stop-transcript abundance do not, by themselves, close the branch. The primary pulmonary-hypertension study reported an ataluren-responsive SMAD9 R294X UGAA case with approximately 10% mutant transcript in cDNA. That is a positive native-context counterexample, not a BUB1B prediction. Ataluren effects vary across systems; independent reporter studies and a 2026 comparative study also found weak or absent activity in tested settings. [Drake 2013, Table 1](https://doi.org/10.1165/rcmb.2013-0100OC), [McElroy 2013](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.1001593), [2026 comparative study](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12967-026-08058-5).

Ataluren-mediated UGA decoding produced Arg, Trp and Cys in studied reporters, with different proportions between human and yeast systems. The proposed L737Ter allele therefore does not imply restoration of Leu737, and the measured fractions must not be copied to BUB1B. A full-length product can be useful without native Leu, or defective despite being full length. A reported non-monotonic response also argues against testing one high concentration and declaring the branch negative. [Roy 2016](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1605336113). A sequence-level inference further limits the SMAD9 analogy: an Arg insertion could restore its native R294, whereas that insertion changes native BUBR1 Leu737. This is not a measurement of the inserted residue in the SMAD9 study.

## 2. Qualification is short and explicit

Use the confirmed model nucleotide identities, phase and native transcript architecture, not merely the labels L737Ter/N1002K or an intronless overexpression construct. The public proposed stop is UGAA; the actual renewable model still needs identity checks. Record authentication, passage, mycoplasma status, baseline chromosome/copy-number composition and pre-existing multinucleation. An immortalized model with altered p53/RB behavior can test some molecular questions while distorting arrest, selection and clone-fitness questions.

Prefer two independently derived genotype/correction pairs, carried through matched editing and expansion procedures. The first corrective benchmark is **stop-only repair, WT/N1002K**; it tests what native-Leu restoration can accomplish while leaving the other allele unchanged. It is not WT/WT. A fully corrected reference is useful during qualification to distinguish assay insensitivity from insufficient stop-only repair. If stop-only repair fails while full correction works, that is adverse evidence for stop restoration alone in this model, rather than a reason to call every endpoint unqualified. Pre-expansion genetic correction is a prevention benchmark; it does not demonstrate reversal of established mosaic damage.

Before drug outcomes are unmasked, prespecify the function to assess, a practical observation horizon, imaging conditions that do not materially alter division, and how assay repeatability will be estimated. Concurrent vehicle and corrective-reference cultures can establish whether a deficit is present during this first pilot. Record the smallest effect worth confirming and the largest losses of output/survival that would change the decision. These values require laboratory judgment and baseline data; **this memo supplies no invented effect threshold, power, patient-equivalent sample size or safety margin**. A pilot with only one origin remains possible, but its generalization is explicitly limited.

Stop-RNA abundance and bulk BUBR1 measurements can be collected concurrently with the screen. Complete turnover, folding, modification, recruitment and tRNA-insertion mechanisms are not prerequisites for the first phenotypic test.

## 3. Module A: eight conditions, one primary functional endpoint

The proposed cycling-cell module contains eight assigned conditions per matched origin pair:

| Model | Conditions | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Exact genotype | Vehicle | Basal functional deficit and treatment comparator |
| Exact genotype | Four ataluren concentrations | A bounded exposure curve, allowing a non-monotonic response |
| Exact genotype | Two NAC concentrations | Independent protective comparator; not an assertion that two concentrations optimize NAC |
| Matched stop-corrected WT/N1002K | Vehicle | Native-sequence restoration benchmark |

Concentrations are chosen and frozen after basic solubility/medium qualification, not optimized repeatedly against the best-looking image. Equalize vehicles. A practical **capacity example**, not a power calculation, is two origin pairs × three independently started runs × two wells for each assigned condition = **96 imaged culture wells in total**. The same condition is randomized across plate positions within each run; cells are nested observations. Use a bounded parallel molecular panel rather than destroying the imaged cultures: for example, vehicle, one prespecified middle ataluren condition and corrected reference at baseline/24 hours. Expand that molecular panel only for a selected hit or an interpretable failure.

**Primary endpoint: apparently faithful productive division yield.** Enroll all prespecified eligible viable founders before treatment. For each founder, score whether its first division by a frozen time H completes cytokinesis without a visible segregation error, bridge, newly formed micronucleus or binucleation, and whether both resulting daughter branches retain at least one viable member at the same post-cytokinesis time D. If a daughter divides again, follow that branch and retain every death/error event rather than marking division as death or losing the branch from the record. Branch persistence does not mean that no descendant died; those deaths remain explicit components. Baseline abnormalities are recorded separately and never relabeled as newly generated.

\[
Y = \frac{\text{founders with a qualifying first division and two surviving daughter branches}}{\text{all enrolled founders}}.
\]

For a sufficiently cycling model, **H = 48 hours and D = 24 hours** is an illustrative schedule, requiring acquisition through 72 hours for late divisions. The scientist must select H and D from untreated baseline behavior and assay stability before comparing drugs. These durations do not imply that every model responds within 24 hours. Enrollment after drug pre-incubation is unacceptable for this estimand because it discards early death and selection. A later exposure schedule would require its own all-founder definition.

Always show the components beside Y: first-division completion per founder; observed errors per founder and per first completed division; failure of cytokinesis; death before division; live nondivision; daughter death, nondivision and reproduction; and unresolved tracking. Error rates conditional on division compare drug-selected subsets and are not alone a causal fidelity estimate. A drug can improve output without reducing the error propensity of dividing cells, so those are different conclusions. Nondivision is not automatically toxicity or clinical harm, but it cannot count as successful productive division.

This primary endpoint measures **observed useful cellular output** and blocks a pure selective-killing or cytostatic explanation for an increased numerator. It does **not** prove euploid daughters, restore their pre-existing karyotype, prove BUBR1-mediated action or measure long-term tumor risk. Cells with chromosome errors can continue dividing and accumulate further errors in the next cycle; first-cycle appearances and survival therefore require confirmation. [Santaguida 2017](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.devcel.2017.05.022).

## 4. Module B: choose one function; run it without waiting for ataluren

| Function to qualify in an appropriate model | Small parallel module | Primary function and necessary context | What cannot win |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cilium-dependent stimulus response in quiescent exact-genotype cells; test the deficit against concurrent references | Vehicle; two fasudil concentrations; two hydroxyfasudil concentrations; corrected vehicle. Six conditions, each with stimulus-present and stimulus-absent sibling wells. | Recovery of the endogenous signaling response toward the corrected range, with basal activity reported separately; ciliary receptor localization and cell coverage support interpretation. Use a qualified endogenous GLI1/PTCH1 response, with a receptor-appropriate stimulus, rather than a firefly-only reporter. | More/longer cilia, more basal Gli, or more ciliated survivors without useful regulated signaling. Parent and metabolite retain separate identities and response curves. |
| Barrier function in an appropriate renewable human endothelial/co-culture model; measure leak against concurrent references | Deficient vehicle; deficient cilostazol parent alone; deficient parent plus both active metabolites at the same parent exposure; corrected vehicle; corrected mixture. Five conditions. | Reduced tracer permeability with concordant electrical resistance, stable viable coverage and recorded starting permeability; distinguish coverage changes from junction function. | Junction staining alone, a higher resistance caused by altered coverage/composition, or an arbitrary cell line that has no validated barrier phenotype. |

Run **one** of these bypass modules in the first decision, chosen by available appropriate platforms, assay competence and biological relevance. Existing evidence of a reproducible deficit strengthens that choice, but phenotype establishment and initial response testing may occur in the same controlled pilot. Name its corrective reference explicitly: stop-only correction if informative for the selected function, or a separately qualified fuller correction. Do not silently label either genotype WT/WT. Do not build a BBB platform merely to fill a drug matrix. The optional eight-condition mitochondrial-neural module is specified below and in its linked card. Among available platforms, prespecify the most informative feasible experiment and reserve the others. If no relevant tissue platform is available, Module A is a bounded partial comparison, not proof that ataluren outranks the downstream candidates.

Ciliary and cycling modules require different states. Serum starvation changes both cell-cycle behavior and drug binding; nominal concentrations in low-serum cilia assays cannot be assumed exposure-equivalent to those in cycling cells. BUBR1-linked cilia studies establish a disease-related route, but not this genotype's phenotype. The 2025 ROCK study tested other genes, with nonresponding TMEM67 models and compound-specific endpoints. The Gli experiment has unresolved parent/metabolite identity: Figure 3g and Supplemental Data 4 say fasudil, while the caption and Methods say hydroxyfasudil. Neither assignment is treated as established. Belumosudil moves to a hit-triggered orthogonal confirmation, subject to its own measured-free exposure bridge. [Miyamoto 2015](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2015.01.003), [Smith 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s43856-025-00847-1).

For a Hedgehog-function claim, use a qualified native-ligand response. SAG can check downstream SMO responsiveness but cannot alone establish restored ligand regulation upstream of SMO. A high-exposure-only hit is a mechanism finding with unresolved translation, not an exposure-qualified medicine lead.

For barrier work, the low-BubR1 mouse permeability phenotype was age-dependent, whereas the cilostazol functional experiments used a separate rat endothelial system. Neither establishes a leak in the child. A post-deficit repair claim requires documented leak before treatment; prevention and improvement during early reoxygenation are different schedules. [Yamazaki 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26883501/), [Horai 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23224787/).

The human cilostazol endothelial positive used added forskolin in every condition. Omit added forskolin during inference if the qualified barrier remains competent; otherwise retain the conditioned scope and do not claim standalone rescue. Record serum/hydrocortisone and other conditioning. The revised five-condition design tests the active-metabolite contribution and generic enhancement; a corrected-model ceiling can hide the latter. One mixture is a bounded feasibility test, not a dose-response exclusion. Match parent/metabolite sampling times or label an unmatched mixture as an exposure sensitivity experiment; separately observed adult peaks are not necessarily concurrent. Confirm free medium exposure. See the [adversarial tissue review](tissue_candidates.md) for human trial negatives, reporter-identity uncertainty and primary PK sources.

Selumetinib is deferred from this first pilot after actual-drug counterevidence. Ataluren or NAC does not gain evidence merely because another route is weakened.

## 5. Only a hit receives the focused mechanism/confirmation package

Take at most one exposure per successful route into an independently started confirmation. If two candidates survive, compare those conditions on the **same qualified functional endpoint**; do not combine division yield, Gli and permeability into an invented overall efficacy score.

| Observation to resolve | Minimal next measurement | Interpretation limit |
|---|---|---|
| Ataluren improves output, or increases full-length protein | Allele-resolved stop transcript; N- and C-terminal/full-length protein assessment; qualified product-specific proteomics on the selected condition | Total full-length BUBR1 can come from N1002K. Common downstream peptides establish neither allele of origin nor function. |
| Whether the stop is actually decoded | Seek a peptide spanning residue 737 with the inserted residue; use validated variant standards and appropriate digestion. A phased N1002-containing peptide can support origin if the haplotypes and assay permit. | The existing N1002K substitution creates a tryptic cleavage site. Unequal peptide recovery cannot be read as unequal allele expression; a negative below assay sensitivity remains unresolved. Full-length size and transcript/splice identity help exclude truncated/reinitiated alternatives. |
| Product appears, but useful division does not improve | One model-qualified checkpoint hold-and-release or attachment-error-recovery challenge, selected from the measured basal defect; stage-matched BUBR1/partner localization | Longer arrest or better fixed alignment is insufficient. Do not stack several spindle poisons onto the primary cells. If needed, model the **observed** inserted residue at controlled abundance to distinguish product incompetence from insufficient supply. |
| Useful phenotype appears without demonstrated stop product | Retest the selected condition in the matched no-PTC corrected background, with its own baseline; investigate the demonstrated response | Benefit in a no-PTC model supports a non-readthrough component. No benefit there cannot prove specificity because its phenotype may already be at a ceiling. Report an unresolved/non-readthrough hit honestly. |
| Imaging suggests fidelity rescue | Linked terminal daughter copy-number/genome assessment in a prespecified subset, with image-error-positive and image-error-negative lineages both represented | Sequence/cytogenetic data are needed for a genome claim. Inherited mosaicism and new imbalance differ; unrelated endpoint cells cannot assign an event to a particular mother. |
| Ciliary signaling improves | Washout/re-entry cohort followed through completed cytokinesis and daughter outcomes, with the functional concentration retained; terminal genome assessment if advancing | Persistent quiescence, cytokinesis failure or preferential loss of unciliated cells can imitate structural rescue. No extra proliferation requirement is imposed on a genuinely postmitotic function. |
| Barrier function improves | Independent repeats with exposure confirmation, viable coverage, and the same tracer/TEER endpoints; a focused cAMP/PKA readout | Target engagement supports mechanism but is not efficacy. An inhibitor alone is not specific causal proof. Parent/metabolites require separate exposure assessment. |

Genetic stop repair is a useful positive benchmark for native sequence, not a model of ataluren's mixed insertion products. A selective stop-allele perturbation could test causal dependence after a hit, but its feasibility and baseline effects must be qualified. Neither a large panel of single-allele models nor perfect site-resolved mass spectrometry is an entrance requirement for the first phenotype screen.

Terminal genome collection is destructive: the same cell cannot be collected promptly after division and also complete the later D follow-up. **Default confirmation design:** prospectively assign a separate early-genome cohort, image its division and recover both daughters soon after cytokinesis where feasible; a matched live cohort supplies the complete D follow-up. This links each sampled genome to its observed division but does not link that destroyed cell to its later survival. An alternative is collection after D, with explicit acknowledgement that recovered cells may be descendants and that survival, reproduction and recovery have selected the available sample. Choose the scheme before unmasking. Paired daughter genomes help detect discordance but do not reconstruct every mother's prior genome, and whole-genome amplification has dropout/resolution limits. Publish the sampling rule and failed recovery. A small confirmation subset can validate or challenge the imaging interpretation; it cannot certify absent genomic harm across the population. Continue descendant follow-up separately for abnormal-lineage persistence/expansion rather than treating a 24-hour observation as a clone-safety certificate.

## 6. Decisions that move the portfolio

| Prespecified outcome | Decision for the next commitment |
|---|---|
| Reproducible increase in apparently faithful output, fewer observed errors without a loss of completion, and product-specific evidence compatible with stop decoding | Advance ataluren to linked genome/function confirmation and an exposure bridge. Call this a renewable-model hit until those results exist. |
| More full-length product but unchanged/worse function, despite an informative genetic correction benchmark | Do not advance on protein. Resolve quantity versus insertion-product function once; prioritize a bypass with its own positive functional evidence. |
| Lower selected-metaphase abnormalities or lower ROS, but lower all-founder output, more nondivision, or selective loss | Reject the fidelity-rescue interpretation. Protective cytostasis may remain a separate tissue-support hypothesis only if it preserves a meaningful tissue function and its reserve/output tradeoff is measured. |
| Ataluren shows a useful response without assignable stop product | Keep a phenotypic hypothesis; do not call it readthrough. Product assay failure is not proof that readthrough is absent. Compare against the NAC phenotype and the qualified bypass endpoint, rather than declaring stop specificity. |
| Adequate measured exposure, interpretable RNA/product assays and a correction-responsive model, but no useful ataluren response over the frozen range | Retire **that tested ataluren bridge**. Do not automatically escalate concentration, add NMD inhibition or conclude that every suppression technology fails. |
| Low substrate, poor solubility/delivery, insensitive product assay, or no usable functional deficit | Unresolved feasibility, not a drug-efficacy negative. Permit one documented technical repair/repeat; do not continue an unlimited search for a positive. A qualified bypass need not wait. |
| NAC alone improves the same functional endpoint with maintained output | Prioritize confirmatory redox work at that exposure. Two comparator conditions establish neither global NAC superiority nor that redox is the sole mechanism. |
| Fasudil or hydroxyfasudil restores regulated signaling, and subsequent re-entry remains competent | Prioritize that specific ciliary route even if BUBR1 abundance is unchanged. Reject a harmful exposure without extrapolating harm to every ROCK drug/concentration. |
| Cilostazol improves a demonstrated barrier deficit with viable coverage | Prioritize barrier-support confirmation; normalizing every existing chromosome is not required to call a barrier effect. This remains distinct from genomic correction and clinical neurologic benefit. |
| PBA or elamipretide improves evoked neural function with maintained survival/output and reserve | Confirm the selected drug-specific profile independently, using a direct functional comparison and supporting mechanism readout; distinguish useful function from selective survival and abnormal-lineage expansion. Neural benefit need not normalize every chromosome. |
| No route passes its qualified functional test | Preserve the informative negatives and model limitations; no winner is nominated from a prettier surrogate. |

## 7. Exposure precedents, not BUB1B prescriptions

| Primary experiment | Published concentration/exposure | Design consequence |
|---|---|---|
| SMC1A lymphoblastoid protein/RNA panel | 0.5, 1.5 and 3 micrograms/mL ataluren for 24 h | A short molecular precedent. Do not assign this regimen to its separately reported chromosome panel. |
| FANCA metabolic/proliferation study | 2.5 micromolar ataluren for 72 h; 5/10 micromolar also evaluated | Effects beyond readthrough and growth changes can occur in the same broad range as readthrough experiments. |
| Roy reporter experiments | NanoLuc response peaked around 10 micromolar in a tested system; human product-identity experiment used 30 micromolar | Test a bounded curve; neither the peak nor insertion mixture predicts BUB1B. |
| Smith ciliary study | Fasudil rescue at 1 micromolar in some engineered RPE1 models, with cell-number toxicity at 10 micromolar or above; belumosudil 0.5–5 micromolar for 48 h in ordinary RPE1 | Low and high conditions cannot be pooled, and this is not an exact-genotype safety window. |
| Horai barrier study | 1/10 micromolar cilostazol had functional effects; 1 micromolar was added during 3 h reoxygenation after 6 h oxygen/glucose deprivation | Do not count every tested lower concentration as effective or call early reoxygenation a chronic-deficit reversal. |
| NAC oocyte study | 500 micromolar during 18 h in-vitro maturation in Sod1-null mouse oocytes | A meiotic oxidative-stress precedent, not a somatic BUB1B exposure target. [Nago study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11751902/) |

These are literature anchors for scientist-led assay development, **not selected BUB1B concentrations or human doses**. Record compound identity, lot, formulation, vehicle, serum/protein content, medium changes and exposure duration. Check drug recovery/stability in the actual assay medium; determine unbound medium exposure for the selected confirmation condition and investigate intracellular availability when interpreting a negative. A nominal cell concentration is neither free target exposure nor a patient Cmax. A cellular hit can be valid while its human exposure bridge remains unqualified. Avoid reducing-dye viability assays as the sole cell-count readout, especially when testing metabolic/redox-active compounds.

## 8. Reproducibility and the actual inference unit

- The **randomized drug-treatment unit is the independently assigned culture well/insert**. Independent editing origins and backgrounds establish biological replication and generalization; they are not interchangeable with wells, runs or patients. Repeated runs from one clone do not create new donors. Analyze within-origin treatment contrasts, show origin/run effects, and retain wells as clusters rather than treating each cell as an independent treatment replicate.
- Daughters, frames, fields, cilia, kinetochores and repeated TEER measurements are nested observations. For barrier co-cultures, the shared preparation/differentiation batch can correlate several inserts. Three organoids or hundreds of cells from one preparation do not establish three independent donor responses.
- Freeze field selection and exclusion rules before drug labels are revealed. Use masked image adjudication with an error codebook and audit a random sample of apparent normal divisions. Report resolution limits, missing frames and phototoxicity controls.
- Record founder ID, origin, batch, well, condition code, treatment time, first-mitosis/cytokinesis times, each daughter/branch ID, errors, death, nondivision, final follow-up and reason for missingness. A daughter born late receives its own full D interval. Mother follow-up cannot substitute for daughter follow-up.
- Missing founders remain in the denominator. Report Y as an observed-success lower bound and an upper bound allowing unresolved founders to succeed; do not quietly mark missing outcomes normal. Analyze reasons and treatment dependence of missingness. A lost error-positive lineage is not evidence of biological death.
- Select at most one exposure per route using a rule frozen before unmasking. Confirmation uses a fresh run and preferably an additional independent origin, with sample size informed by observed variance and the prespecified useful/harm margins. Dose searching and confirmation are separate. A nonsignificant harm test is not equivalence or safety.
- Baseline and terminal aneuploid fractions describe state mixtures; neither alone estimates production of new errors. Clonal editing/expansion may already have selected survivors before founder enrollment. Preserve provenance and starting composition rather than claiming that later randomization erases that bottleneck.

## 9. The next research commitment

**Now:** a small ataluren allele-directed feasibility hypothesis; a direct PBA/elamipretide neural-protection comparison; separate fasudil/hydroxyfasudil ciliary conditions; cilostazol parent/metabolite barrier comparison; and NAC as a protective comparator. Choose one appropriate downstream module, rather than requiring all of them. Belumosudil is reserved for confirmation and selumetinib is deferred. The portfolio is evidence-based but unranked for clinical efficacy. These are research nominations supported by a proposed decision design, not performed experiments or efficacy results.

**Before scarce participant material:** obtain the scientist's feasibility review, an available authenticated renewable platform, reproducible assay behavior, a bounded functional hit or a clearly defined high-information unresolved question, a suitable exposure/readout plan, and a material-efficient confirmation protocol. Confirm the linked genome interpretation before claiming genomic rescue. A phenotypic hit with incomplete mechanism can still justify a carefully scoped ex-vivo question if its uncertainty is explicit; completing every turnover, tRNA and tissue mechanism is not universally required first. Routine ethical/material permissions remain for the responsible investigators; this memo neither requests nor accesses the material.

**Before clinical translation:** relevant tissue benefit, exposure feasibility and independent genomic/abnormal-lineage assessments remain separate obligations. This experiment cannot establish reversal of prenatal injury, seizure benefit, cancer prevention or suitability for administration.

Direct primary findings and newly proposed design choices are separated throughout. No raw biological dataset was reanalyzed. The design requires scientist review of model availability, measurement feasibility, exposure conditions and the decision criteria before laboratory execution.

## New alternative tissue module: neural protection comparison

The [neural-protection comparison](neural_protection_comparison.md) supplies an optional eight-condition module: two explicitly identified genotypes by vehicle, one PBA profile, one elamipretide profile and one NAC comparator profile after a short fixed exposure scout. It can replace the ciliary/barrier module when the neural platform is relevant and ready. It is not an obligatory expansion of every assay. The primary tissue readout is evoked function with survival and starting-cell accounting; precursor cultures also require functional descendant output, reserve and division/abnormal-lineage outcomes. The cycling-module founder-yield definition does not become a universal endpoint for postmitotic cells. A recovery claim requires a documented pretreatment deficit; otherwise phenotype and response may be established concurrently. No neural exposure, efficacy ranking or treatment recommendation is established. The 100-micromolar PBA precedent measures prophylactic stress/apoptosis markers, not useful post-deficit recovery. Compare drug-specific measured profiles and direct evoked-function contrasts; one positive versus one negative P value is not evidence of a difference. An underexposed negative remains unresolved, and one profile cannot eliminate an entire medicine. Require independent confirmation and assess persistence after washout.
