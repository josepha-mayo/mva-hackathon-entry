## Executive decision

**Single conditional probe:** arimoclomol, used only as an exposure-gated ex-vivo probe.

**Why this probe:** if the uncharacterized p.Asn1002Lys allele proves to be an expressed, fold-unstable but intrinsically functional BUBR1 hypomorph, stress-dependent chaperone amplification could raise functional BUBR1 abundance. MIPLYFFA's FDA indication is for use in combination with miglustat for a different disease, and its current label provides estimated pediatric steady-state serum concentrations. Unrelated preclinical cell studies report improved biochemical processing and activity of mutant GCase or reduced aggregation of transfected P23H rhodopsin; neither constitutes BUBR1 evidence, patient rescue, or clinical benefit.

**Why this is not yet a drug answer:** there is no direct arimoclomol evidence for BUB1B, p.Asn1002Lys, MVA, spindle-checkpoint rescue, or chromosome-segregation rescue. Pharmacologic HSP90 inhibition depleted the tested unstable I909T and L1012P BUBR1 constructs, whereas arimoclomol studies most clearly document stress-contingent HSP70-family induction. This is an unproven chaperone bridge, not a demonstrated target match. The current FDA-approved label says arimoclomol's clinical mechanism in NPC is unknown. Published Gaucher-cell studies used nominal culture concentrations far above the label-estimated pediatric mean serum peak.

**Exposure rule:** all micromolar values below are culture-media concentrations, not patient dosing instructions. Test 0.2, 0.5, 1, and 2 micromolar as the primary concentration window. Only a result at no more than 2 micromolar can satisfy the concentration gate, and only after exposure duration, unbound medium concentration, and intracellular exposure are characterized. Five micromolar is mechanistic exploration and cannot advance the candidate by itself; a signal confined to 50–100 micromolar or above is a repositioning **NO-GO**.

**Biological rule:** first confirm that the variants are in trans and that p.Asn1002Lys causes an exact-allele defect. Cis phase or a functionally WT-like missense allele rejects the submitted causal chain. If exact correction fails across independent, quality-controlled clones, causal attribution remains unresolved and the program does not advance; one failed edit or clone is not sufficient to declare biological falsification.

**Outcome rule:** lower bulk aneuploidy is not accepted as rescue. In a founder cohort enrolled before treatment, first-division errors must fall both per completed division and per enrolled founder while division completion remains equivalent. Daughter outcome, abnormal-clone fitness, toxicity, and final aneuploid fraction are measured separately.

**Decision now:** GO only for orthogonal confirmation and direct phase (Gates 0–1). Hold RNA, editing, and every drug experiment until those gates pass; hold arimoclomol specifically until trans phase and an exact N1002K stability defect are demonstrated. NO-GO for administration, dosing, combination therapy, or any cure claim.

## 1. What is observed, inferred, and unknown

The Track 1 research hypothesis contains two heterozygous *BUB1B* calls on the shared MANE Select transcript NM_001211.6:

1. `c.2210T>G`, p.(Leu737Ter), a premature-stop allele; and
2. `c.3006T>G`, p.(Asn1002Lys), an uncharacterized missense allele.

The following evidence labels are deliberately noninterchangeable.

| Link in the causal chain | Current status | What the evidence supports | What it does not support |
|---|---|---|---|
| Both calls exist in the supplied single-sample call set | Observed in challenge data | A two-variant research candidate | Orthogonal confirmation, germline origin, or phase |
| p.Leu737Ter is loss-of-function-compatible | Public database plus inference | A nonsense allele in a gene where loss of function is disease-relevant | Participant-specific NMD, zero protein, or clinical causality |
| p.Asn1002Lys lies in the C-terminal BUBR1 domain | Sequence/domain fact | A plausible region for structural or functional disruption | Instability, pathogenicity, or HSP responsiveness |
| The pair is in trans | Unknown | Nothing yet | Compound heterozygosity cannot be claimed until measured |
| p.Asn1002Lys lowers abundance or half-life | Unknown | A testable hypothesis based on other BUBR1 missense alleles | Exact-allele mechanism |
| The pair impairs chromosome segregation | Unknown for this genotype | A mechanistically coherent experiment | Diagnosis or phenotype attribution |
| Arimoclomol restores exact-allele function | Unknown | A screening question | Treatment efficacy or clinical benefit |

### 1.1 Precision on the ClinVar anchor

As of 2026-08-26, [ClinVar VCV000533901.9](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/clinvar/variation/533901/) shows a germline Pathogenic/Likely pathogenic variant-level aggregate with criteria provided, multiple submitters, no conflicts, two contributing submissions, and two-star review status. The MVA1-specific [RCV000641226.9](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/clinvar/RCV000641226/) is Pathogenic with criteria provided, one submitter, and one-star review status. That submitter states that p.Leu737Ter has not been reported in the literature in individuals with BUB1B-related conditions and classifies it from the expected premature-stop consequence plus the established BUB1B loss-of-function disease mechanism. These are database assertions, not participant-specific confirmation or case evidence.

A bounded ClinVar and PubMed search on 2026-08-26 found no ClinVar record for `c.3006T>G` and no indexed PubMed title/abstract record for `c.3006T>G`, `Asn1002Lys`, or `N1002K`. A different nucleotide substitution, `c.3006T>A`, encodes the same p.Asn1002Lys change and is classified as Uncertain significance with criteria provided by one submitter in [VCV004600147.1](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/clinvar/variation/4600147/), last evaluated 2025-09-19 under the broad trait “Inborn genetic diseases.” It is not MVA-specific evidence and does not classify the challenge allele.

The supplied call set is also a candidate input, not a locus-complete survey: call-format records cannot exclude copy-number, structural, or noncoding alleles at this locus or elsewhere, and exon-dosage or read-level evidence is outside the released data. Gate 0 orthogonal confirmation therefore verifies the two reported calls; it does not certify that this pair is the entire causal genotype — which is why every downstream stop rule is written against the measured pair rather than a presumed diagnosis. Population context is bounded but consistent: gnomAD v4.1.1 records c.2210T>G at 120/1,614,020 alleles and c.3006T>G at 1/1,614,226 alleles, with no homozygotes reported—both far below any maximum credible allele frequency for this ultra-rare recessive condition [Karczewski et al., 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32461654/); [Whiffin et al., 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28518168/). Under an independence approximation—an assumption, not a measurement, for two alleles 10.9 kb apart that could sit on one haplotype—the naive expected trans-double-carrier frequency is on the order of 10^-10, consistent with the syndrome's extreme rarity and with allelic heterogeneity rather than a recurrent pair. This is an order-of-magnitude consistency check, not evidence of phase, pathogenicity, or participant-specific causality.

### 1.2 Disease-class bridge

The original human MVA series identified truncating and missense *BUB1B* mutations in affected families [Hanks et al., 2004](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15475955/); the current review of the syndrome's developmental, ageing, and cancer biology is [Malumbres and Villarroya-Beltri, 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39169218/). MVA is also genetically heterogeneous, not a single-gene entity: biallelic *BUB1B* mutations account for only a proportion of cases, with biallelic *TRIP13* and *CEP57* families forming the other molecular classes [Snape et al., 2011](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21552266/); [Yost et al., 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28553959/). The classes are not interchangeable — childhood embryonal tumors concentrate in the *BUB1B*- and *TRIP13*-mutant, spindle-checkpoint-impaired group, while *CEP57*-mutant and unexplained MVA in the reported series showed minimal checkpoint deficiency and no embryonal-tumor excess; that stratification is the disease-class reason the §5 counterscreen is written for a checkpoint-defective germline context.

In patient cells, restoring TRIP13 function rescued checkpoint proficiency and segregation accuracy [Yost et al., 2017] — an in-disease precedent for the exact-correction causal anchor this method requires. A 2025 whole-genome glioblastoma-predisposition study independently documented germline `BUB1B c.2210T>G (p.Leu737Ter)` — the same variant evaluated here — classified pathogenic and recessive loss-of-function, with a large somatic deletion removing the remaining allele in the carrier's tumor; the authors stopped short of assigning tumor causality [van Opijnen et al., 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41168197/). Because c.2210 occupies the middle base of codon 737, the T>G substitution necessarily creates TTA→TGA — a UGA opal stop, generally the most readthrough-permissive of the three termination codons in mammalian contexts — positioned well upstream of the final exon junction, so the transcript is expected to be NMD-competent; that expectation is consistent with the absent truncating-mutant transcripts reported by Suijkerbuijk et al.

Suijkerbuijk et al. subsequently described biallelic cases as a missense allele paired with a truncating allele and linked absent truncating-mutant transcripts plus increased missense-protein turnover to low BUBR1 abundance. Across patient-derived lymphoblastoid and fibroblast lines, they found an impaired mitotic checkpoint and chromosome-alignment defects. Separately, in BUBR1-depleted U2OS replacement assays, forced expression of I909T or L1012P to WT-comparable abundance fully restored the nocodazole-response readout [Suijkerbuijk et al., 2010](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2887387/). This was mutant-construct restoration of one engineered checkpoint readout, not rescue of a patient, organism, or p.Asn1002Lys.

Those findings establish an experimental allele class, not the mechanism of p.Asn1002Lys. Independent experimental mutation or removal of BUBR1's C-terminal pseudokinase domain decreased KARD phosphorylation and PP2A-B56 recruitment and impaired checkpoint silencing and chromosome alignment [Gama Braga et al., 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33207204/); those constructs were not p.Asn1002Lys and do not establish a clinical-variant mechanism. Whether the domain is catalytic at all is contested—other work reports BubR1 is a bona fide kinase that phosphorylates CENP-E to switch kinetochore–microtubule attachment, though its crystal structure is Drosophila protein [Huang et al., 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31201382/). This disagreement does not change the gating: under either catalytic-status model a fold-unstable N1002K fails, and the Gate 4 abundance/stability readout plus Gate 6 functional assays are defined independently of which model holds. Mouse L1002P corresponds to human L1012P, not human N1002K, and mouse-human and allele-specific effects were substantial [Sieben et al., 2020](https://www.jci.org/articles/view/126863). The exact missense allele must therefore be allowed to falsify the stability hypothesis.

The published alleles split into two classes with opposite implications: abundance-defective models (the Suijkerbuijk-engineered I909T and L1012P constructs) could in principle respond to stabilization or abundance rescue, while abundance-stable functional alleles — including the published abundance-stable missense nearest this locus, C945Y, which did not alter BubR1 expression or localization [Hahn et al., 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27239782/) — would fail a stability screen by mechanism, not by assay sensitivity.

A third risk is dominant interference: an abundance-normalized N1002K protein that still disrupts kinetochore function would make stabilization worsen rather than rescue, which is why the function-when-abundant test gates the screen rather than accompanying it.

Public sequence and structure evidence supplies a bounded reason to test that hypothesis, not proof that it is true. N1002 lies in the annotated BUBR1 protein-kinase domain and is conserved in the checked mouse, rat, frog, and Drosophila homologs. At N1002, the wild-type [AlphaFold DB model for O60566](https://alphafold.ebi.ac.uk/entry/O60566) has pLDDT 91.06; this is per-residue local coordinate confidence, not a mutant-effect score. Reproducible local analysis gives RSA 0.1422 (48.07th percentile within the annotated kinase domain), consistent with partial but unremarkable domain-relative burial, and predicts three short side-chain-to-backbone polar contacts. The mapped homologous Asn has the same contact topology in experimental Drosophila structures [6JKK](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/6JKK) and [6JKM](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/6JKM); the aligned deposited construct is only 27.5% identical to the human region, so this is homolog-level—not human structural—evidence.

The official 2023 AlphaMissense catalogue [Cheng et al., 2023](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adg7492) gives N1002K a score of 0.9229, but that is in-silico prioritization; no experimental human structure contains atoms at N1002, and a different nucleotide substitution encoding the same protein change remains a single-submitter ClinVar VUS. This evidence prioritizes an exact-allele stability assay; it does not establish pathogenicity, folding loss, or drug responsiveness.

In-silico predictors disagree at this locus: a public DynaMut2-class stability analysis of this exact variant reports a non-destabilizing output for N1002K while the AlphaMissense effect score above is high — neither class of prediction substitutes for measurement, and the disagreement is the reason abundance, half-life, and function are measured directly at Gates 4–6. For the abundance-defect class generally, CRISPR editing at a recurrent germline *BUB1B* variant produced biallelic in-frame-deletion clones with decreased BubR1 expression or stability, premature chromatid separation, and chromosomal instability in nontransformed prostate epithelial cells [Silva et al., 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39014450/) — direct precedent that edited *BUB1B* variants in a nontransformed background yield measurable checkpoint and segregation defects, and itself an editing-QC caution: that study recovered only unexpectedly biallelic clones, the artifact class Gate 3's structural controls exist to catch.

### 1.3 Causal graph

The proposed chain is:

`trans phase` → `hypothesized stop-allele transcript depletion` + `hypothesized N1002K defect` → `insufficient functional BUBR1` → `checkpoint/attachment failure` → `new segregation errors per division`

Arimoclomol enters only between a demonstrated N1002K folding/stability defect and functional BUBR1 abundance. It cannot repair cis phase, restore the stop-bearing transcript if that transcript is depleted, or prove the upstream genetic model.

## 2. Why arimoclomol is the conditional probe—and where the hypothesis is weakest

### 2.1 Regulatory and exposure facts

FDA approved MIPLYFFA in 2024. Its current indication is MIPLYFFA in combination with miglustat for neurological manifestations of NPC in adult and pediatric patients aged two years and older. The [current FDA-approved labeling, revised March 2026](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=5feffc0e-453d-47fa-91dd-38d4952309bc&type=display) says the mechanism underlying clinical effects in NPC is unknown. It reports estimated pediatric steady-state mean ± SD serum trough and peak concentrations of 206 ± 60 and 523 ± 194 ng/mL. The label gives citrate molecular weight 505.90 g/mol and a rounded strength equivalence of 124 mg arimoclomol to 200 mg arimoclomol citrate, implying a base-equivalent molecular weight of approximately `505.90 × 124 / 200 = 313.7 g/mol`. On that basis, 206 and 523 ng/mL correspond to approximately 0.66 and 1.67 micromolar. These are label-estimated mean serum concentrations in pediatric NPC under recommended dosing, not unbound, intracellular, sustained, or MVA-specific exposures.

The label records hypersensitivity, animal embryofetal and fertility findings, reversible creatinine increases associated with OCT2 inhibition, reduced dosing frequency for eGFR 15 to less than 50 mL/minute, and no PK evaluation below eGFR 15 mL/minute. It does not establish safety or efficacy in MVA or with arimoclomol monotherapy; even in NPC, the label states that data were insufficient to determine effectiveness without miglustat.

### 2.2 Mechanistic support

In primary Gaucher fibroblasts and a human neuronal model, arimoclomol increased HSP70-family proteins and improved mutant GCase maturation, lysosomal localization, and residual activity [Fog et al., 2018](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6306395/). In transfected human SK-N-SH cells, 1 micromolar arimoclomol for 24 hours significantly increased Hsp70 and reduced P23H rhodopsin inclusions and insoluble material [Parfitt et al., 2014](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4047904/). That nominal concentration is of the same order as, but below, the label-estimated pediatric mean serum Cmax; a constant 24-hour culture exposure is not equivalent to a transient systemic peak, and the label reports an approximately four-hour half-life in healthy adults. Neither model tested BUBR1. The most mechanistically detailed cellular study is in NPC: arimoclomol increased TFEB/TFE3 nuclear translocation, upregulated CLEAR-network expression including *NPC1*, and enlarged the pool of mature mutant NPC1 that reached the lysosome across three different mutant genotypes [Shammas et al., 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40215728/) — evidence that the drug can raise functional abundance of a misfolded mutant protein genotype-independently, though at nominal concentrations up to 400 micromolar, far above the label-estimated pediatric mean serum peak.

A randomized NPC trial contains a limited, uncontrolled within-arm observation: in the arimoclomol arm, PBMC HSP70 increased from baseline at month 12 in the 11 paired samples available (`P = 0.001`) [Mengel et al., 2021](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9293014/). Central-laboratory sample loss left only four placebo paired samples, so the authors could not use placebo as a control for HSP70. This within-arm change is compatible with, but does not establish, an arimoclomol-induced human HSP70 response; it does not demonstrate BUBR1 engagement, explain the labeled clinical effect, establish an MVA-relevant exposure, or predict N1002K rescue.

The patient-derived Gaucher-cell work is also central counterevidence: HSPA1A induction used 100–400 micromolar, residual GCase-activity studies used 50–800 micromolar, and the authors noted a beginning cytostatic effect at 400–800 micromolar. Dividing those nominal culture concentrations by the estimated pediatric mean serum peak gives a roughly 30–480-fold concentration ratio. This is not an exposure-equivalence calculation because it omits unbound concentration, culture-medium binding, intracellular partitioning, exposure duration, tissue distribution, and metabolites. Fog et al. also disclosed Orphazyme funding and use of proprietary Orphazyme arimoclomol citrate; independent replication is required.

### 2.3 Unproven chaperone bridge

Suijkerbuijk et al. found that five-hour geldanamycin treatment severely depleted the tested I909T and L1012P BUBR1 constructs, while MG132 prevented the enhanced turnover, supporting HSP90-dependent folding and proteasomal routing in that engineered assay. Published arimoclomol studies describe it as a co-inducer or amplifier of a pre-existing heat-shock response and most consistently document HSP70-family induction; they do not establish an HSP90-directed molecular target. The FDA label leaves the clinical mechanism unknown. There is no direct evidence that arimoclomol at a nominal culture concentration of no more than 2 micromolar creates the chaperone state required by N1002K; this gate is a conservative screen, not a label-validated cellular exposure threshold. Two further caveats bound interpretation. First, arimoclomol's in-vivo benefit is not confined to heat-shock-protein induction: in FUS-R521G ALS mice it reversed cognitive deficits independently of HSP induction [Pelaez et al., 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38972779/), so the prespecified HSF1/HSPA1A dependency measurements—not an assumed heat-shock route—are what classify any observed response. Second, a nominal-matched total concentration is not exposure matching: free drug must cross the membrane and reach the kinetochore, where BUBR1 participates in Mps1-initiated mitotic-checkpoint-complex assembly [Sethi et al., 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40410156/); measured intracellular exposure, not nominal dosing, governs the read.

The screen therefore requires, in order:

1. participant- or exact-isogenic N1002K instability;
2. a prespecified chaperone-response signal at no more than 2 micromolar—for example, HSF1-state and HSPA1A/B responses—without calling this a validated direct molecular target;
3. increased soluble, correctly localized full-length BUBR1;
4. improved checkpoint and chromosome-segregation function; and
5. no apparent benefit explained by cytostasis, toxicity, or abnormal-clone selection.

### 2.4 Negative clinical evidence

Arimoclomol failed to improve efficacy outcomes in two completed randomized trials. In phase 3 ORARIALS-01, the modified-intention-to-treat CAFS score did not differ between arimoclomol and placebo (0.51 versus 0.49; `P = 0.62`), and adverse events led to discontinuation in 16% versus 5%; the authors concluded that higher dosing likely would not have been tolerated [Benatar et al., 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38782015/). In the completed randomized inclusion-body myositis trial, the month-20 IBMFRS mean difference was -0.99 (95% CI -2.23 to 0.24; `P = 0.12`), with adverse-event discontinuations in 18% versus 5% [Machado et al., 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37739573/). The ALS authors also stated that biomarker data were insufficient to exclude all future HSP-response strategies. Neither trial tested BUB1B or MVA, but both oppose any claim that generalized heat-shock-response amplification is predictably clinically effective. The ALS paper disclosed Orphazyme funding; the IBM paper disclosed FDA Office of Orphan Products Development and Orphazyme funding.

### 2.5 Why the alternatives are comparators, not co-leads

The selection matrix was frozen around evidence that could falsify this exact mechanism; approval status alone does not rank a drug.

| Candidate | BUB1B-direct evidence | Human pharmacodynamic observation | Human-exposure information | Key limitation/counterevidence | Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Arimoclomol | none | uncontrolled within-arm PBMC HSP70 increase in 11 paired NPC samples; only four placebo pairs, so no controlled HSP70 comparison | label-estimated pediatric mean serum Cmax (not unbound or intracellular); unrelated 1-micromolar/24-hour culture result is not PK-equivalent | high concentrations in many Gaucher-cell assays; randomized ALS and IBM trials did not improve their prespecified efficacy outcomes; NPC mechanism unknown | single conditional ex-vivo probe |
| Teprenone / GGA | none | oral HSP70-family induction in human gastric or atrial tissue | no BUBR1-relevant systemic or pediatric information | HSP70-independent UPR/CHOP at ≥100 micromolar and apoptosis in mesangial cells | orthogonal comparator only |
| Sodium phenylbutyrate | none | no exact-mechanism human bridge used here | adult single-dose label PK only | sodium burden; pediatric PK gap; mechanistically nonspecific | pharmacologically distinct comparator only |

Arimoclomol leads because it has the least-bad combination of a bounded low-micromolar cellular observation, a small uncontrolled within-arm human HSP70 observation, and current label exposure information—not because it is proven, unique, or BUBR1-directed. The exact-allele Gate 4 stop prevents those indirect observations from carrying a failed stability premise.

- **Teprenone / geranylgeranylacetone:** PMDA lists teprenone as the active ingredient of Selbex, a marketed Japanese anti-ulcer medication [PMDA product record](https://www.pmda.go.jp/PmdaSearch/rdDetail/iyaku/2329012C1026_1?user=1). Oral GGA increased HSP70-family proteins in human gastric or atrial tissue [Yanaka et al., 2007](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17684364/); [van Marion et al., 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31302249/). The cited studies do not establish systemic or pediatric exposure relevant to BUBR1. At concentrations of at least 100 micromolar, GGA induced an HSP70-independent ER-stress/UPR program including CHOP; in mesangial cells it also induced apoptosis [Endo et al., 2007](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17702888/).
- **Sodium phenylbutyrate:** BUPHENYL is indicated as adjunctive chronic therapy for specified urea-cycle disorders. Its [current label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=463a36fa-3eb2-4326-8bd0-c8c7a11bca3a&type=display) reports an adult fasting phenylbutyrate peak of 218 micrograms/mL after a single 5-g tablet dose—approximately 1.17 millimolar using the label's molecular weight of 186—and a 0.77-hour half-life. The label states that PK studies were not conducted in the primary neonatal, infant, and child population and records approximately 124–125 mg sodium per gram of sodium phenylbutyrate. It is retained only as a pharmacologically distinct laboratory comparator, not as a pediatric exposure bridge or dosing proposal.

- **Nonsense-suppression and NMD-modulation classes (considered, declined as leads):** p.Leu737Ter is a premature-termination allele, so nonsense-readthrough agents could in principle restore some full-length BUBR1 from it — a mechanistically orthogonal route to the missense allele. The class is declined as a lead here: mammalian readthrough typically restores only a small fraction of normal protein output; the only marketed readthrough agent, ataluren, lost EU conditional authorization in March 2025 because effectiveness was not confirmed and has never held FDA approval; and aminoglycoside-class agents carry chronic pediatric oto-/nephrotoxicity concerns — although the class is not clinically dead (ELX-02 produced response signals in a small Phase 2 nonsense-Alport study), so this is an allele-context decision, not a claim that readthrough can never work. Within this method's own exposure gate, aminoglycoside carryover is already a controlled confound class — the canonical readthrough reagents can themselves generate a false rescue signal. NMD inhibition without readthrough is likewise not a rescue route: a stabilized 737Ter transcript would yield a residues-1–736 product that retains the N-terminal checkpoint arms and the KARD/PP2A-B56-binding motif but loses the entire C-terminal pseudokinase domain — including the catalytic-equivalent residues, the Plk1 sites T792/T1008, and the C-terminal helix essential for CENP-E binding — so it forfeits the domain-dependent promotion of KARD phosphorylation and checkpoint silencing [Gama Braga et al., 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33207204/); Gate 2 assays for that truncated product diagnostically, and Gate 3's L737*/WT carrier control defines the dose-sufficiency question directly — in the published autosomal-recessive MVA family series heterozygous carriers are clinically unaffected, although a heterozygous BubR1-MVA-allele mouse model showed reduced life- and healthspan [Wijshake et al., 2012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23300461/), so the carrier class sets a dose floor rather than a WT ceiling — and restoring the missense allele toward full function remains the coherent single-allele strategy. A future readthrough arm would be a separate experimental direction with its own gate design; no combination therapy is proposed.
- **Proteasome inhibitors:** MG132 is retained solely as the in-vitro degradation-pathway control used by Suijkerbuijk et al. No proteasome inhibitor is proposed as a repositioning candidate.
- **BUBR1-abundance-axis comparators (mechanistically closest, no drug bridge):** The only direct BUBR1 abundance pharmacology in the surveyed literature is post-translational: SIRT2 deacetylates BubR1 at lysine 668, blocking its ubiquitylation and proteasomal targeting; in BubR1-hypomorphic mice, SIRT2 overexpression raised BubR1 abundance and extended lifespan, and the NAD+ precursor NMN increased tissue BubR1 levels in vivo [North et al., 2014](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24825348/). Constitutive BubR1 overexpression in mice independently protects against aneuploidy and cancer and extends healthy lifespan [Baker et al., 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23242215/). Together these support the premise that more BUBR1 abundance is protective — the direction this method tests. The class is retained as comparator, not co-lead: the acetylation site (K668) is not this participant's residue (N1002K), neither SIRT2 overexpression nor NMN is a medicinal product with a pediatric exposure bridge, and no SIRT2-activating drug with BUBR1 evidence exists. If Gate 4 confirms an abundance defect and Gate 5 is negative, this axis—not another chaperone—becomes the next hypothesis class, entering through the same exposure-bounded screen.
- **Paralog and vulnerability classes (disqualified by design):** BUB1 and BUB1B encode paralog checkpoint proteins that are frequently confused; BUB1 inhibitors (e.g., BAY-320, BAY-524, BAY 1816032) do not abolish the spindle checkpoint and are retained only as paralog-selectivity controls [Baron et al., 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26885717/); [Siemeister et al., 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30429199/). MPS1/TTK inhibitors (reversine, AZ3146, BAY 1217389) do abolish the checkpoint and are harm-direction controls [Santaguida et al., 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20624901/); [Hewitt et al., 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20624899/); [Wengner et al., 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26832791/). Separately, aneuploid cancer cells depend on KIF18A and die on its depletion [Cohen-Sharir et al., 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33505028/)—an approach that prunes error-bearing daughters rather than correcting errors, which is precisely the selection-confound this method's generation-versus-selection design exists to expose; it is disqualified as a rescue route for a pediatric developmental disorder regardless of efficacy.
- **M-phase pacing (context-limited, not a candidate):** controlled prolongation of meiosis I reduced the aneuploidy-producing class of lagging chromosomes in mouse oocytes [Mihajlović et al., 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34428397/), and prolonged mitosis in somatic cells risks cohesion fatigue and nondisjunction — the opposite direction of rescue — so checkpoint *stringency*, not duration, is the lever; no somatic-cell pacing intervention with a pediatric exposure bridge exists.

Exact N1002K correction is the causal anchor. Expression-matched full-length WT BUB1B is a complementary pathway-rescue control; it cannot substitute for exact correction or be interpreted as a drug-equivalent result.

## 3. Sequential experiment: every gate can stop the program

### Gate 0 — orthogonal confirmation, identity, and governance

Confirm both variants from a second DNA aliquot using an orthogonal clinical-grade assay: concretely, bidirectional Sanger sequencing of an independent amplicon per variant—the long-standing reference confirmation chemistry [Rehm et al., 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23887774/)—paired with allele-discrimination ddPCR for absolute allele counts, where a variant fraction materially below 0.5 routes to the Gate 1 mosaicism workup rather than passing as confirmation [Hindson et al., 2011](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22035192/). Verify trio identity and Mendelian consistency before interpreting segregation. Use residual clinical material or an already obtained fibroblast culture; do not request an invasive pediatric biopsy solely for this research. Keep coded samples and a prespecified incidental-findings and return-of-results pathway through qualified clinicians.

**Stop:** either call fails orthogonal confirmation, specimen identity is unresolved, or governance does not permit the planned use.

### Gate 1 — direct phase

Perform PCR-free Cas9-targeted long-read sequencing across the approximately 10.9-kb interval with two independent guide configurations. Require at least 30 high-quality full-span molecules per recovered haplotype, strand/guide balance, and concordant allele linkage—30 concordant molecules exclude a ~10% cross-haplotype contamination rate at ~95% confidence by the rule of three, the order at which a misassigned minority haplotype would flip the phase call. PCR-free Cas9 enrichment avoids PCR-generated chimeras and amplification bias, but it does not eliminate guide-site, fragment-length, allelic-recovery, or strand bias [Gilpatrick et al., 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32042167/); independent guide configurations are therefore required. If parental DNA is available through approved channels, genotype both parents independently and require trio consistency as orthogonal evidence. Parental availability strengthens but does not replace direct molecular phasing, and its absence must not be represented as phase resolution. Segregation is definitive only for inherited alleles: if each parent carries exactly one variant and trio identity is verified, trans follows by inheritance under the segregation logic of the interpretation guidelines [Richards et al., 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25741868/); a variant absent from both parents is de novo or parental-mosaic in origin and cannot be phased by segregation—direct spanning molecules remain required [Roach et al., 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20220176/); [Acuna-Hidalgo et al., 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27894357/). The spanning-molecule readout also presumes an intact diploid interval: a hemizygous deletion, segmental isodisomy, or low-fraction mosaic allele at this locus can break or bias molecule recovery and mimic cis, so an exon-dosage or copy-number check accompanies any trans call [Biesecker and Spinner, 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23594909/); [Nakka et al., 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31607426/).

If Cas9 enrichment under-recovers—spanning an ~10.9-kb interval presupposes intact high-molecular-weight DNA—prespecified fallbacks keep the direct-molecule requirement: droplet-based duplex phasing validated on compound-heterozygous variant pairs 1–200 kb apart [Regan et al., 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25739099/); read-backed phasing from long-read whole-genome sequencing [Wenger et al., 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31406327/); [Jain et al., 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29431738/) with haplotype assembly on spanning reads [Patterson et al., 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25658651/); cobarcoded linked reads [Wang et al., 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30940689/); or allele-specific long-range PCR accepted only with documented mispriming and crossover-amplification controls [Wu et al., 2005](https://doi.org/10.1002/humu.9369).

- **Trans:** proceed with the pair hypothesis.
- **Cis:** reject the submitted pair; N1002K may still be studied as variant biology. The defensible continuation under cis is diagnostic, not therapeutic — because heterozygous carriers are clinically unaffected, a compound allele on one chromosome with WT on the other carries no rescue rationale; instead re-open the locus-completeness search for a cryptic second hit (exon-dosage/CNV analysis, segmental isodisomy or uniparental disomy, deep-intronic splice alleles), since only a hidden third allele would re-engage a BUB1B program.
- **Unresolved:** functional work may continue, but no participant-specific compound-heterozygous claim advances.

### Gate 2 — allele-specific RNA and stop-allele fate

Measure total BUB1B RNA by RT-ddPCR against a validated multi-gene reference set chosen for stability across proliferation states [Vandesompele et al., 2002](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12184808/), with a proliferation-matched comparator and MIQE-standard reporting [Bustin et al., 2009](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19246619/). Total abundance must be normalized carefully because BUB1B transcription is cell-cycle-regulated through CDE/CHR promoter elements [Myslinski et al., 2007](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkm239); [Whitfield et al., 2002](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12058064/) — the within-sample allelic ratio is immune to this confound, the total-RNA readout is not, and UPF1/SMG1 perturbation itself slows proliferation. Quantify allelic ratios at c.2210 and c.3006 by UMI amplicon sequencing plus orthogonal dual-probe ddPCR, normalized to gDNA controls, with a prespecified per-site molecule floor (≥50 UMIs or ≥100 ddPCR droplet events per allele per replicate, across ≥3 independent RNA isolations) because allele counts are overdispersed relative to binomial and a near-zero mutant count is informative only above the floor [Castel et al., 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26381377/). Include DNase treatment and no-reverse-transcription controls — contaminating gDNA inflates the cDNA stop-allele fraction toward a false "persists" call — and re-run the untreated patient-line baseline in parallel with every perturbation batch because NMD efficiency drifts with cell state.

The depletion call is statistical, not visual: the stop-bearing fraction of total BUB1B cDNA, normalized to its gDNA fraction, must fall below a frozen bound (mutant fraction &lt; 0.25 of total BUB1B cDNA) under a one-sided test. The two measured sites carry a built-in reciprocity check — under trans phase a single depleted haplotype shows the same depletion as a *decreased* c.2210 mutant fraction and an *increased* c.3006 mutant fraction, with f_mut(c.2210) ≈ 1 − f_mut(c.3006); nonreciprocal imbalance flags allele dropout or differential N1002K-chromosome expression rather than NMD.

Test two nonoverlapping UPF1 perturbations and an independently optimized SMG1-pathway perturbation — delivered as siRNA/CRISPRi or the SMG7–UPF1 disruptor NMDI14 rather than wortmannin/caffeine-class chemicals, which are nonspecific PIKK-family inhibitors [Martin et al., 2014](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24662918/). Genetic perturbation is preferred over cycloheximide/puromycin because global translation arrest is itself a stressor that confounds the total-RNA readout. Each perturbation arm must pass a prespecified efficacy floor to count as valid — target depletion of at least 80% by immunoblot plus derepression of a named canonical NMD substrate (GADD45B or the SRSF3 poison-exon isoform) by a prespecified fold — so that a failed knockdown cannot masquerade as an NMD-insensitive transcript.

Support the specific NMD model only if the untreated stop-bearing transcript falls below the frozen depletion bound and two valid independent perturbations each raise the mutant fraction toward parity by a prespecified fold, with viability and assay controls passing. Two published precedents inform the prior: BUB1B truncating and splice-mutant transcripts were already shown significantly reduced in MVA patient cells [Matsuura et al., 2006](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16411201/), and no truncated BUBR1 product was detectable in MVA patient lysates — so the N- and C-terminal antibody truncated-product check is *expected to be negative*, and a null antibody result must not be spun as evidence against the transcript model. Consistent with the model, c.2210 falls in exon 17 of 23 with six downstream exon–exon junctions, satisfying the ≥50–55-nt downstream-EJC rule [Nagy and Maquat, 1998](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9644970/); [Lindeboom et al., 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27618451/). If the transcript persists, revise the null model rather than forcing an NMD label; targeted long-read cDNA sequencing checks splice and full-length isoforms — including nonsense-associated exon skipping that removes the PTC, an escape route the allelic ratio alone cannot name.

A persistent fragment also demands a functional verdict, not only detection: a residues-1–736 product retains the N-terminal checkpoint arms, both kinetochore-localization routes, and the KARD motif, so it could act inert, hypomorphic, or dominant-interfering — sequestering Cdc20/Bub3 or misdirecting PP2A-B56 in the soluble pool are the plausible interference channels, and engineered truncations have produced interference phenotypes in other systems [Tsurumi et al., 2004](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15611331/). The prior is low — no truncated product was detected in patient lysates, the murine X753 analog is unstable [Sieben et al., 2020](https://www.jci.org/articles/view/126863), and the near-identical 731X construct is functional rather than toxic [Suijkerbuijk et al., 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20516114/) — but "low" is not "excluded." If the fragment is detected, add a competition arm (ectopic exact-1–736 at calibrated-to-endogenous levels) and a true-null comparator (a heterozygous deletion or early-frameshift line producing no stable product), because L737*/WT cannot otherwise separate haploinsufficiency from interference — both read as carrier defects. If interference is demonstrated, the rescue logic inverts: the goal becomes depleting the stop-bearing transcript or product (allele-selective silencing, or *enhancing* rather than inhibiting NMD), and the suppressor-tRNA rung remains compatible because it converts the fragment back to full length.

### Gate 3 — endogenous isogenic allelic series

Build a near-diploid, p53-intact nontransformed allelic series, with confirmation in patient-derived cells or a second nontransformed background. Escalate rather than constructing every line at once: the minimal causal series is WT/WT, the exact trans test genotype, and its exact-corrected derivative; add carrier-dose, instability-comparator, same-site, and assay-floor controls only when the preceding gate justifies them.

| Genotype | Purpose |
|---|---|
| WT/WT | normal baseline |
| L737*/WT | truncation-carrier control |
| WT/N1002K | missense-carrier dose control |
| L737*/N1002K, confirmed trans | exact test genotype |
| L737*/N1002= | same-site editing control after normal splicing is verified |
| L737*/L1012P | known MVA-associated instability comparator, not an N1002K surrogate |
| exact corrected compound clone | primary causal rescue |
| acute BUB1B depletion | assay-floor control |

Use six clones per genotype nested within three independent editing events, with two independently derived clones per event, for the feasibility stage. These are three—not six—independent highest-level units. Confirm local sequence and phase, fingerprint identity, mycoplasma status, modal karyotype, copy-number integrity, and relevant off-targets. Reject unexplained aneuploid or structurally altered clones. Before confirmatory inference, use blinded event-level variance to determine whether additional independent edit events or biological backgrounds are required; three events are a feasibility floor, not an asserted universal power calculation—at k=3 events the decision rule's minimum-detectable reduction sits near the frozen 0.75 margin, so the floor certifies only strong effects. The implemented power machinery (`assay_power` in the released package) prices detection probability with a Wilson lower bound of at least 0.8 under analyzer-faithful simulation at the pilot-estimated event-level variance before outcome unblinding, and the blinded internal pilot exists to re-estimate that variance without touching outcomes [Wittes and Brittain, 1990](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2345839/).

The editing process itself is a falsification surface and is controlled accordingly. Double-strand-break repair can produce on-target chromothripsis or megabase-scale truncations [Leibowitz et al., 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33846636/); [Cullot et al., 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30850590/), can generate copy-neutral LOH invisible to copy-number QC—which could silently rewrite the clone's true genotype at the locus (homozygosing the corrected haplotype mimics stronger-than-possible rescue; homozygosing the truncation mimics failure)—and selects for p53-pathway suppression or TP53 mutation [Ihry et al., 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29892062/); [Haapaniemi et al., 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29892067/), which would falsely relax the completion or arrest phenotype. The required control set therefore includes a mock-edit arm (delivery procedure only), an editing-process arm (same protocol at a safe-harbor locus), early-passage founders with passage-number tracking, TP53 status confirmation under the p53-intact restriction above, copy-neutral LOH assessment in addition to copy number, and breakpoint-aware structural QC (long-read or equivalent); reprogramming-based alternatives are themselves suspect because pluripotency derivation can conceal somatic chromosomal instability [Hamada et al., 2012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22952451/).

The correction modality is constrained by the edit chemistry, not by preference. Both variants are T>G transversions, so exact correction requires a G→T reversion on the coding strand — outside cytosine- and adenine-base-editor chemistry entirely; prime editing is the only mature modality that installs all twelve substitution classes [Anzalone et al., 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31634902/), with published transversion correction in patient HSPCs (sickle HBB at 15–41% allele correction [Everette et al., 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37069266/)) and nonsense/transversion correction in patient iPSCs and organoids [Bulcaen et al., 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xcrm.2024.101544). The prespecified stack is mRNA-encoded PEmax plus a stabilized epegRNA and nick sgRNA by nucleofection — epegRNA roughly triples editing in primary fibroblasts [Nelson et al., 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34608327/), the MLH1dn arm roughly doubles PE3 [Chen et al., 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34653350/), and PE6-class enzymes extended gains in patient-derived fibroblasts [Doman et al., 2023](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2023.07.039). Expected recovery is ~10–40% pool-level editing in pluripotent cells, lower in fibroblasts, and ~5–15% correctly-edited heterozygous clone recovery, so pools are deep-sequenced before any cloning and a 96–384-clone screen is budgeted per site — the pools-first two-step [Bonnycastle et al., 2024](https://doi.org/10.1089/crispr.2023.0066). Nickase-based editing still engages p53 — transient p53DD boosts prime editing roughly 2–3× in pluripotent cells, which is itself evidence the stress axis responds — and editing bottlenecks select TP53-inactivated clones [Enache et al., 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32424350/) on top of the recurrent dominant-negative TP53 expansions documented in pluripotent culture [Merkle et al., 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28445466/); p53DD, if used at all, is transient mRNA only, and every recovered clone carries TP53 coding sequencing plus a functional induction assay. Because BUB1B sits on chr15q15 in a line that already missegregates, on-target chromosome loss is a named risk [Nahmad et al., 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35773341/) and chr15 copy number is checked post-edit; off-target profiling uses nickase-compatible assays (nDigenome-seq, TAPE-seq, or PE-tag) per guide rather than DSB-based surrogates. Two honesty flags stand: MMR-evading silent substitutions inside the RT template would make the correction near-exact rather than exact — such a clone is a separate genotype class, not the exact-corrected anchor — and no published study has prime-edited a checkpoint-defective line, so clone recovery and TP53 outcomes are measured empirically rather than assumed.

Single-cell cloning is the feasibility bottleneck, and it is the step the published field has deliberately avoided: functional MVA work has used SV40-transformed fibroblasts or EBV lymphoblasts [Suijkerbuijk et al., 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20516114/) or forward-edited tractable lines [Ochiai et al., 2014](https://doi.org/10.1038/srep06848), because adult dermal fibroblasts clone at roughly 5% and an aneuploid founder plus p53-dependent post-missegregation arrest [Thompson and Compton, 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20123995/) drive effective recovery toward the low single digits — and a "clone" recovered from a chromosomally unstable line is a karyotypic mixture, not an isogenic unit. Expansion additionally counterselects the phenotype: aneuploid progeny are purged from regenerating compartments in vivo [Pfau et al., 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27313317/) and missegregating cells arrest in culture, so the expanding fraction is the least-aneuploid — phenotype dilution with passage is measured as a QC output, not assumed away. The design therefore carries a declared non-clonal fallback: when clone recovery fails, the founder→daughter imaging design on genotyped uncloned populations is itself the biological unit, and the three-event hierarchy becomes three independent editing runs on bulk populations at matched passage, each endpoint-karyotyped. Heterozygous parental fibroblasts and mock-edited patient cells carried through the identical nucleofection bottleneck are the near-isogenic controls; transient cDNA or inducible BUB1B re-expression is the field-precedented rescue anchor [Suijkerbuijk et al., 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20516114/). If immortalization is required for material it is hTERT only — SV40 large-T ablates p53 and would mask the very arrest biology under measurement — and iPSC derivation remains a parallel arm rather than a prerequisite, since reprogramming succeeds in checkpoint-defective backgrounds but yields predominantly aneuploid, karyotypically unstable clones that must be re-karyotyped after expansion [Hamada et al., 2012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22952451/).

### Gate 4 — exact N1002K abundance and stability

Declare the abundance baseline before measuring it: in the trans genotype there is at most one full-length-producing allele (if Gate 2 confirms stop-transcript depletion), so a perfectly stable N1002K yields roughly half of WT/WT full-length BUBR1. The comparators are therefore the single-full-length-allele dose — the L737*/WT carrier and the corrected clone — never WT/WT totals, which would fabricate an abundance defect by allele dose alone.

Use a C-terminal antibody to measure the full-length missense product without counting a possible L737* fragment — with three validation requirements carried over from the antibody-validation pillars [Uhlen et al., 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27595404/): a mapped C-terminal epitope that excludes residue ~1002 (an epitope spanning 1002 could lose reactivity to N1002K and mimic instability in both blot and imaging readouts), demonstrated equal reactivity to ectopic WT and N1002K protein, and concordance with a second non-overlapping antibody or with proteomics before any low-signal result is interpreted. Quantify steady-state protein by total-protein normalization plus targeted proteomics (PRM/SRM) that includes the tryptic peptide spanning residue 1002 — the N→K substitution introduces a new tryptic cleavage site and a ~14-Da shift, enabling within-sample allele-fraction quantification in the WT/N1002K carrier — together with at least two shared proteotypic peptides and heavy-isotope spike-in standards for absolute molecules-per-cell estimates [Gerber et al., 2003](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12771378/). Because an aggregation-prone allele can partition into insoluble pools and be misread as degraded, measure soluble and insoluble fractions separately.

Measure half-life by cycloheximide chase with pulse-SILAC as the orthogonal readout [Schwanhäusser et al., 2011](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21593866/), under a prespecified design rather than ad-hoc timepoints: chase duration capped, at least five timepoints spanning the expected half-life, a declared decay model with confidence intervals — a non-trivial fraction of proteins decay non-exponentially, so a forced single-exponential is itself a confound [McShane et al., 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27720452/) — and per-timepoint viability and cell-cycle QC, because global translation arrest is a stress perturbation that shifts the apparent dilution-versus-degradation balance [Eden et al., 2011](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21233346/) and a multi-hour chase in cycling cells mixes interphase-stable protein with the APC/C–Cdc20-driven mitotic-exit pool (BUBR1 degradation at mitotic exit is gated by K250 acetylation [Choi et al., 2009](https://doi.org/10.1038/emboj.2009.123)). If the CHX arm fails its QC, pulse-SILAC is primary; tag-based pulse-chase, auxin-degron chase [Nishimura et al., 2009](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19915560/), or bleach-chase imaging supply a third line when the two primaries disagree by more than a prespecified factor. Because the chaperone-rescue premise assumes the fold-unstable → proteasome route, the degradation pathway is measured rather than inherited: MG132 and lysosomal-inhibitor (bafilomycin A1 or chloroquine) chase arms distinguish proteasomal from lysosomal clearance, and a per-clone proteasome-capacity QC (UPS reporter or proteasome-activity assay) controls clone-level proteostasis flux so a high-flux clone cannot masquerade as an unstable allele [Bence et al., 2001](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11375494/).

Match cell-cycle state, and quantify kinetochore-localized BUBR1 under a named spindle-disruption protocol (nocodazole dose and duration) with ACA/CREST centromere co-staining for per-kinetochore intensity ratios — kinetochore BUBR1 is strongly microtubule-state-dependent, expanding into crescents under depolymerization and dropping several-fold at metaphase attachment, so uncontrolled fixation timing or mixed mitotic stages make intensity comparisons meaningless [Hoffman et al., 2001](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11451998/). Stage-gate to prometaphase, blind the quantification, and compare against WT and corrected-clone comparators.

Residue 1002 sits in a densely functional C-lobe neighborhood — six residues from the functionally required Plk1 site T1008, ten from the documented destabilizing MVA allele L1012P, and fifty-seven from the documented non-destabilizing functional allele C945Y — so the function-when-abundant panel must resolve which sub-defect fired, not only whether abundance dropped. The panel therefore extends beyond bulk kinetochore intensity to the causal chain the domain regulates: KARD phospho-state (pS670/pS676/pT680) and kinetochore B56α intensity measure the PP2A-B56 recruitment arm that PKD integrity promotes [Gama Braga et al., 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33207204/); CENP-E co-immunoprecipitation, kinetochore CENP-E intensity, and the CENP-E pS2639/BUBR1 pT608 tension loop measure the alignment arm [Guo et al., 2012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22801780/); and a phospho-readout on T1008 (or in-vitro Plk1 phosphorylation of the recombinant domain, WT versus N1002K) tests phospho-regulation directly. An N1002R comparator allele is budgeted for the abundance-defect branch specifically: the N→K substitution installs a new lysine — a potential ubiquitin/acetyl acceptor — so if N1002R is stable, the lesion is neo-PTM turnover rather than misfolding, and the SIRT2/NAD+ axis (not a chaperone amplifier) is the matching rescue class; ubiquitin/acetyl-lysine mass spectrometry on immunopurified protein discriminates the same two models. A N1002K protein normal on all of these but still defective routes to the supplementation rung rather than forcing an abundance story.

Before unblinding N1002K outcomes, freeze biologically meaningful effect and equivalence margins from external assay performance and control biology. A blinded control-only pilot may estimate variance and sample size, but cannot choose margins after showing favorable separation. A stability-hypomorph call requires materially lower full-length abundance and half-life, concordant orthogonal turnover evidence, and rescue by exact correction. A non-decisive Gate 4 result fails closed — the arimoclomol branch stays shut — because an inconclusive stability readout is not evidence of stability. If N1002K falls within the prespecified WT-equivalence range for abundance and half-life, the stability hypothesis is rejected and the **arimoclomol branch stops**. Candidate-agnostic functional characterization may continue, but WT-equivalent abundance and half-life stop this stability-rescue rationale.

Function-when-abundant is a separate, prior requirement: before the stabilization branch may open, the missense allele must be shown functional when its abundance is normalized — a missense that remains defective at abundance **stops** the stabilization hypothesis outright, not merely holds it. The normalization instrument must not itself confound the readout: proteasome inhibition is the naive choice but blocks cyclin B1/securin destruction and imposes mitotic arrest, which would masquerade as restored checkpoint strength against Gate 6's own endpoints — the specified instruments are therefore titratable expression calibrated to endogenous abundance or auxin-inducible-degron tuning [Nishimura et al., 2009](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19915560/), or inhibitor pulse-then-washout with recovery, each with its own perturbation controls. "Functional" is bound to a frozen subset of the Gate 6 readouts — nocodazole-arrest maintenance, the MPM2/pH3 checkpoint fraction, and monastrol-washout chromosome alignment — plus a proximal spindle-checkpoint-strength endpoint, kinetochore Mad2/Cdc20 recruitment or mitotic-checkpoint-complex formation, which scales with checkpoint signal strength [Collin et al., 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24096242/); pass margins are frozen the same way as Gate 6's. Pharmacochaperone activity is variant-specific even within a target class [Tedman et al., 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40553789/), so rescue of a neighboring destabilized allele would not license rescue here; the allelic series, not the literature class, decides.

### Gate 5 — exposure-gated arimoclomol screen, only after a Gate 4 stability defect

Use vehicle and arimoclomol at 0.2, 0.5, 1, and 2 micromolar. Only a response at no more than 2 micromolar can satisfy the concentration gate. Five micromolar is mechanistic exploration and cannot advance the candidate by itself; concentrations from 50 micromolar upward may be retained only to diagnose a nontranslational mechanism.

Concentration matching alone is not human exposure matching. Quantify total and unbound medium concentration and intracellular arimoclomol by a qualified LC-MS/MS method validated fit-for-purpose under the bioanalytical framework before use [FDA Bioanalytical Method Validation, 2018](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/bioanalytical-method-validation-guidance-industry); [ICH M10, 2022](https://www.ich.org/page/ich-m10-bioanalytical-method-validation): at least six non-zero matrix-matched calibrators in both complete medium and cell lysate, a stable-isotope internal standard or justified surrogate, LLOQ at or below 50 nM, ±20% accuracy/precision at LLOQ and ±15% above, and prespecified recovery, matrix-effect, carryover, dilution-integrity, and bench-top/freeze-thaw/autosampler stability acceptance. Report concentration-time profiles and include a pulse/washout arm informed by—but not claimed to reproduce—the label's approximately 0.5-hour adult `tmax` and four-hour adult half-life; the pediatric label supplies serum point estimates, not intracellular exposure or a validated MVA pharmacokinetic model [Cudkowicz et al., 2008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18551622/). Because pharmacology responds to free rather than nominal or total concentration, unbound—not total—medium drug controls the exposure gate, and batch serum fraction is recorded: arimoclomol is only ~10% plasma-bound, but weak-base lysosomal sequestration concentrates cell-associated drug that is not assayable free, so total recovery can read high while the effect-relevant unbound pool reads low [Bohnert and Gan, 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23798314/); [Henneberger et al., 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31230094/); [de Duve et al., 1974](https://doi.org/10.1016/0006-2952(74)90174-9).

Intracellular measurement washes cells under uniform cold conditions, assays cell-associated parent drug normalized per 10^6 cells or per mg protein, pairs a matched spent-medium sample at the same medium-to-cell ratio to bound extracellular carryover, and does not sum glutathione, glucuronide, or hydrolysis products into the parent analyte [Mateus et al., 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23631740/). Salt form, lot, purity, and vehicle are reported, and as a reported OCT2 inhibitor and MATE1/2-K substrate its endogenous transporter expression is a documented cross-arm variance factor rather than a ranking rationale.

If intracellular exposure does not reach the prespecified window—or total medium recovery falls outside the qualified range—the arm fails the exposure gate and cannot advance regardless of phenotype; the nominal bath concentration carries no evidentiary weight. Run drug × proteotoxic-stress factorial arms so a stress-contingent response is distinguished from constitutive drug activity.

Measure:

- HSF1 state, HSPA1A/B and related HSP70-family induction, and HSP90/co-chaperone abundance as prespecified pharmacodynamic-response measurements—not assumed direct-target engagement;
- soluble and insoluble full-length BUBR1;
- BUBR1 half-life, complex assembly, and kinetochore localization;
- PP2A-B56 recruitment;
- viability, apoptosis, cell-cycle distribution, UPR/CHOP, and general proteotoxicity;
- matched WT and corrected-clone effects.

Mechanistic-dependency controls test whether any observed response requires HSF1 or HSPA1A/B; they do not identify a direct arimoclomol target. An abundance clamp that equalizes BUBR1 protein across arms tests whether a functional change exceeds the amount of restored protein. Full-length WT BUB1B provides a pathway-rescue ceiling. Teprenone and sodium phenylbutyrate serve only as orthogonal comparator controls.

**Stop:** no prespecified chaperone-response signal at no more than 2 micromolar under the measured exposure conditions; signal confined above 2 micromolar; no prespecified drug × genotype benefit in BUBR1/checkpoint endpoints after accounting for the contemporaneous WT and exact-corrected drug responses; adverse WT perturbation; UPR, toxicity, or cell-cycle arrest accounts for the result; or N1002K protein rises without correct localization.

### Gate 6 — checkpoint and chromosome segregation

Co-primary functional measurements are:

1. live-cell time in mitosis after nocodazole plus an orthogonal 4N MPM2/pH3-positive checkpoint fraction; and
2. predefined segregation errors in the first attempted unperturbed division of mothers enrolled before treatment, including lagging chromosomes, nondisjunction, bridges, multipolar division, and centromere-validated micronuclei.

Advancement requires **both** co-primary families to pass their frozen margins in the favorable direction under an intersection-union rule; one successful endpoint cannot compensate for failure of the other. Enroll and randomize the mother-cell cohort before exposure, then classify each mother's first attempted division into mutually exclusive outcomes: pre-division death/non-completion, completed without a scored error, or completed with at least one scored error. Report both the conditional error probability per completed first division and the cumulative error-bearing-division probability per enrolled founder. A clean-fidelity claim requires concordant reduction in both, plus containment of the division-completion contrast within its prespecified equivalence band; otherwise report a mixed or non-estimable result. The pediatric completion band is a relative completion ratio inside `[0.95, 1.10]`; the planned real-experiment analysis additionally requires at most a 0.05 absolute completion drop (a dual criterion — the absolute clause binds per edit event, so event-heterogeneous completion loss cannot hide inside a passing pooled ratio, and a large completion drop is itself the harm in a growth-disorder setting). The synthetic benchmark implements the ratio band only; the absolute-drop clause is part of the estimand spec above and remains unimplemented in the aggregate toy — stated here so the two criteria are not conflated. A division is counted once even if it has multiple error features; a blinded adjudication hierarchy assigns category labels for secondary analyses and prevents double-counting. Later-generation and state-stratified rates are secondary. Additional doses and secondary endpoints remain multiplicity-controlled and cannot rescue a failed co-primary result.

Secondary measurements include cyclin B1/securin degradation, chromosome alignment after monastrol washout, premature chromatid separation, and shallow single-cell DNA sequencing after fixed population doublings. Checkpoint-silencing kinetics — the interval from full kinetochore attachment to anaphase onset — are measured separately from arrest strength, because a silencing-defective allele produces segregation errors alongside normal or even prolonged arrest readouts: the two directions must not be pooled into a single "checkpoint strength" score, or a silencing defect would masquerade as intact checkpoint function [Gama Braga et al., 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33207204/).

The independent edit event is the highest-level biological unit; clones are nested within edit event, and repeated thaws are technical run blocks rather than new biological replicates. The feasibility design uses three edit events, two independently derived clones per event, three run blocks per clone, and 250 tracked mother-cell observation opportunities per run—enough to bound a ~90% founder completion proportion to roughly ±4 percentage points (Wilson half-width), the same order as the completion band's lower edge. Confirmatory event count is set from blinded event-level variance before outcome unblinding. Randomize plate position, blind sample IDs, and lock analysis before outcome unblinding. Exact correction must restore at least half of the mutant-control gap and satisfy prespecified equivalence margins to both the WT and the matched carrier-state control — the carrier class is not a perfect WT ceiling, so a corrected clone must clear the dose floor while approaching the normal ceiling. A failed positive control invalidates the run. A blinded pilot may update variance and sample size, but not outcomes, mechanisms, or success margins.

Use one prespecified primary exposure plus a monotone dose-response trend for confirmatory inference; control additional dose contrasts against vehicle with prespecified Dunnett many-to-one adjustment [Dunnett, 1955](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1955.10501294). Add an acute or polyclonal perturbation arm to detect clone-selection artifacts that can arise during prolonged derivation. A finding that exists only in one clone, one edit event, or chronically selected clones does not advance.

## 4. The differentiator: generation versus selection

### 4.1 Why bulk aneuploidy is ambiguous

A lower final fraction of abnormal cells can arise from at least four different processes:

- fewer new segregation errors;
- selective death or arrest of abnormal daughters;
- fewer completed divisions in all cells;
- altered persistence of pre-existing abnormal clones.

Aneuploid daughters can undergo p53-dependent arrest after chromosome missegregation in nontransformed diploid-cell experiments [Hinchcliffe et al., 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27136267/). Valind et al. combined measured missegregation and aneuploidy prevalence with modeling of aneuploid-cell fitness, showing why prevalence reflects both error generation and selection [Valind et al., 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23894657/). Single-cell karyotype reconstruction shows the same composite in tumor evolution—lineage-resolved data, not snapshots, separate punctuated copy-number change from gradual drift [Bollen et al., 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34211178/). Cross-sectional karyotype prevalence is therefore a composite outcome, not the primary rescue endpoint.

The decomposition itself has established antecedents, and this method does not claim otherwise: coupling damage indices to concurrent proliferation/cytotoxicity measurement is standard practice in the cytokinesis-block micronucleus cytome assay family and under [OECD Test Guideline 487](https://www.oecd.org/en/test/test-no-487-in-vitro-mammalian-cell-micronucleus-test.html) [Fenech, 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33076531/), Valind's work above already models prevalence jointly from missegregation and aneuploid fitness, and single-cell methods now infer karyotype fitness landscapes at scale [Beck et al., 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41455703/). What is added here is the assembled fail-closed rescue-decision contract and its receipt-bound benchmark — not the decomposition idea.

### 4.2 Planned full-lineage estimands—not the aggregate benchmark

Perform 72–96-hour lineage-resolved imaging in gridded microwells with chromosome labeling, a death marker, automated segmentation, fixed blinded manual adjudication, and recovery of selected lineages for endpoint single-cell DNA sequencing. The acquisition regime is specified rather than assumed, because the microscope can manufacture the phenotypes being measured: spinning-disk or light-sheet illumination rather than laser-scanning confocal, far-red emission, minimal z-sampling, and a frame interval no coarser than ten minutes for division-outcome classification—anaphase missegregation events resolve in minutes and coarser sampling aliases them [Thompson and Compton, 2008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18283116/); [Janssen et al., 2011](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21960636/).

A prespecified phototoxicity qualification compares imaged founders against sham-handled non-imaged wells for intermitotic time, completion, and death; any imaged-versus-dark difference invalidates the run under the same fail-closed rules as the measurement-drift audits [Icha et al., 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28749075/); [Laissue et al., 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28661494/). The chromosome label is itself a perturbation: the default is a cell-permeable far-red DNA dye qualified by a labeled-versus-unlabeled pilot on completion and micronucleus rate, because published work shows this dye class can itself induce anaphase chromosome entanglement and DNA damage—label dose is evidence, not convenience [Lukinavicius et al., 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26423723/); [Rajendraprasad et al., 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37726128/); a genetically encoded alternative [Kanda et al., 1998](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9545195/) passes the same Gate 3 clone QC as the disease alleles.

Microwell occupancy is image-verified at t=0, only single-founder wells enroll, and a founder emigrating or two tracked lineages sharing one segmented region triggers a prespecified arm-blind censoring rule [Rettig and Folch, 2005](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16131075/); [Skylaki et al., 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27824848/). Endpoint recovery uses position-indexed single-cell pickup preserving the imaging-to-library link, a prespecified low-bias amplification chemistry with diploid and known-aneuploid spike-ins per batch, and a declared bin-resolution floor—amplification non-uniformity and allele dropout can both create and erase apparent aneuploidy [de Bourcy et al., 2014](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25136831/); [van den Bos et al., 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27246599/). At least twenty percent of founders are double-scored by independent blinded adjudicators with a prespecified agreement floor reported as Cohen's kappa on the first-attempt label set, disagreements resolve under a stated arbitration rule, and automated-versus-manual concordance is published per arm [Landis and Koch, 1977](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/843571/).

Enroll and randomize a founder cohort before exposure. Score the first attempted division of every founder—including death or non-completion—before daughter fate is known, then follow both daughters for death, arrest, and another division. Later divisions and endpoint karyotype strata are secondary rather than substitutes for the fixed-cohort primary window.

| Symbol | Estimand | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| `gC` | conditional probability of any prespecified new segregation error in the first completed division of a pre-treatment-enrolled mother | per-completed-division generation component |
| `gE` | probability of an error-bearing completed first division per pre-treatment-enrolled mother | fixed-cohort cumulative component; interpreted with death/non-completion |
| `s` | probability a daughter from an adjudicated error-positive division produces another generation within 48 hours—approximately two fibroblast intermitotic intervals, long enough to capture a delayed daughter division without drifting into later generations—with death and no division retained as competing outcomes | post-event selection/retention |
| `λ` | completed divisions per viable cell-hour plus intermitotic-time distribution | cytostasis/proliferation |
| `pA(t)` | abnormal-cell fraction at a fixed time | composite secondary outcome |

The primary generation decision requires concordant favorable `RRgC` and `RRgE` contrasts plus division-completion equivalence under the pediatric band — relative completion ratio inside `[0.95, 1.10]`, with the additional at-most-0.05 absolute completion-drop clause applying to the real-experiment estimand (not yet implemented in the aggregate benchmark). The synthetic benchmark below enforces the same ratio band, so its "clean signal" metric is the operative one; a mere generation-component flag that coexists with non-equivalent completion is reported separately and cannot advance a candidate. Daughter outcome and division-intensity contrasts are estimated separately. A genetic or drug perturbation can remain biologically interesting if effects are mixed, but it cannot be called improved chromosome-segregation fidelity when only the conditional denominator improves or completion differs materially. Equivalence is judged by two one-sided tests on the completion ratio against the frozen band; the [0.95, 1.10] bounds are an informed default for a growth-disorder context—a wider band would accept clinically meaningful completion loss—and the real experiment must declare and defend its own band before unblinding. The value of this separation is not hypothetical: paclitaxel's cytotoxicity derives from multipolar chromosome missegregation followed by daughter arrest or loss [Zasadil et al., 2014](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24670687/), a mechanism this estimand set would correctly score as failure rather than rescue.

The decision procedure is fixed before unblinding. Primary inference is the paired event-level log-ratio interval on `gC` and `gE` at one-sided alpha 0.025, and completion equivalence is confidence-interval containment in the predeclared 0.95–1.10 band—equivalent to two one-sided tests at alpha 0.025 per side [Schuirmann, 1987](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3450848/), deliberately stricter than the conventional 0.05 convention and asymmetric because completion loss is the harm direction in a growth-disorder context. The pair is an intersection-union combination operating on the joint effect-completion profile, not two independent tests [Berger and Hsu, 1996](https://doi.org/10.1214/ss/1032280304): because `gE` equals `gC` times completion probability, the flags are algebraically coupled, and the estimands diverge exactly when asymmetric death or non-completion would let selection masquerade as rescue—which is why both are required rather than counted as independent replication. Daughter fate is a fixed-horizon cumulative-incidence estimand with death and non-division retained as competing outcomes rather than censoring nuisances, and `λ` is a division-intensity estimand over viable cell-hours; a founder censored before attempt resolution stays in the enrolled denominator under a composite intercurrent-event strategy, and treatment-dependent dropout asymmetry is itself a fail-closed QC condition [ICH E9(R1), 2019](https://database.ich.org/sites/default/files/E9-R1_Step4_Guideline_2019_1203.pdf).

Two identification refinements are declared before any analysis. First, the enrolled-founder cumulative-incidence estimand is the causal primary: it is a composite strategy in which pre-division death or unresolved attempts count against rescue, so no post-treatment conditioning enters the causal contrast. The per-completed-division conditional rate is descriptive only, because conditioning on completion compares arm-specific subpopulations defined by a post-treatment event — the truncated-by-death problem [Frangakis and Rubin, 2002](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.0006-341X.2002.00021.x); [Chiba and VanderWeele, 2011](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21354986/); [Montgomery et al., 2018](https://doi.org/10.1111/ajps.12363). If a conditional effect is ever reported causally it must be declared as a principal-stratum estimand (effect among always-completers), which is not point-identified at this scale and is reported as bounds. The same truncation applies one level up: differential clone-establishment rates between arms would condition on post-edit survival, so the estimand population is enumerated at plating, not at clone recovery. Second, the completion-equivalence decision is a four-region rule on the ratio interval: fully inside the [0.95, 1.10] band passes equivalence; entirely above the band is an overshoot flag — treatment-driven hyper-completion is a harm signal in a growth-disorder context, not rescue — and fails; straddling either bound is inconclusive rather than a favorable trend; entirely below fails. The band is asymmetric on the ratio scale because loss is the harm direction, the effective margin is therefore the nearer 0.05 edge — the equivalence gate is deliberately less powered than the band width suggests — and the upper edge is structurally unreachable whenever control completion exceeds roughly 0.91, a disclosed ceiling on the detectable overshoot regime rather than an oversight.

Mixed models (treatment and edit event fixed, crossed batch and imaging-field intercepts, clone nested within event) are prespecified sensitivity analyses only—at the three-event feasibility floor a random event-level variance component is unidentified [McNeish and Stapleton, 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27269278/). Gate multiplicity follows a fixed-sequence serial-gatekeeping chain: a later hypothesis is reached only if every prior gate passes, so no alpha penalty accrues across gates [Westfall and Krishen, 2001](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0378-3758(01)00077-5); within the terminal screen the vehicle contrasts use prespecified Dunnett many-to-one adjustment at family-wise 0.025 [Dunnett, 1955](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1955.10501294), and all remaining secondaries are exploratory with unadjusted intervals. Cells are nested observations, never independent biological replicates. These timestamped estimands specify the future assay analysis; the implemented aggregate-count stress test below is intentionally narrower.

At the three-event floor the events are fixed strata, not a sample of a superpopulation: the confirmand is the finite-population average effect over the three observed edit events, reported alongside each per-event contrast and its minimum so a dead or null event cannot hide inside a passing average; claims about a population of edits would require on the order of ten or more events and are not made here [Cameron et al., 2008](https://doi.org/10.1162/rest.90.3.414); [MacKinnon and Webb, 2017](https://doi.org/10.1002/jae.2508). Alpha accounting is closed end-to-end: the confirmatory family is exactly the paired event-level contrasts and the completion-equivalence check combined by intersection-union at one-sided 0.025, plus the terminal screen's Dunnett-adjusted vehicle contrasts at family-wise 0.025; the blinded internal pilot is restricted — final enrollment cannot shrink below plan, and the pilot may move variance and size only, never margins, estimands, or gate order [Kieser and Friede, 2003](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14652861/); every other interval in the report is descriptive, carries no multiplicity adjustment claim, and cannot reverse a gate outcome.

### 4.3 Implemented synthetic stress test

This repository implements a deterministic, standard-library **paired hierarchical fixed-cohort first-attempt aggregate-count stress test** in `mva_hackathon.generation_selection`; it is not a timestamped lineage or competing-risk implementation. Each pre-enrolled founder contributes one first attempted division classified as no-completion/death, completed-no-error, or completed-error. Each simulated arm follows a hierarchy of three edit events, two clones per event, and three run blocks per clone. The analyzer is restricted to observed aggregate counts and independently observed calibration counts; generator parameters and realized truth are evaluator-only objects. The edit event—not the clone, run, or cell—is the highest inferential unit. A version-stable pseudorandom generator, strict schema, and fail-closed runner make the final receipt reproducible under its recorded environment; determinism under the recorded seed certifies that the published numbers regenerate, and the supplementary seed sweep below covers master-seed robustness over the same generator—but neither demonstrates robustness under arm-correlated batch or site effects or within-lineage dependence beyond the configured toy.

The predeclared v3 configuration runs 1,000 independent vehicle-treatment comparisons in each of 22 scenarios, for 22,000 comparisons total. The scenarios cover:

1. a no-change null and a strong generation reduction;
2. three local generation ratios—0.75, 0.6875, and 0.625—for an effect-specific power curve;
3. pure error-daughter pruning and preservation;
4. generic cytostasis and generic toxicity;
5. a sparse mixed generation, selection, and cytostasis case scored only for fail-closed behavior;
6. arm-specific event-sensitivity and event-specificity drift;
7. division-detection drift;
8. informative marginal daughter follow-up;
9. moderate cytostasis with a completion ratio near 0.87 — inside the looser legacy band, outside the enforced pediatric band;
10. a strong generation effect combined with that same moderate cytostasis;
11. a sub-threshold generation effect (true ratio 0.775, short of the 0.75 reduction boundary) combined with small arm sensitivity drift below audit power — declared as a documented-boundary probe rather than a detection scenario;
12. a deliberately miscalibrated shared calibration panel;
13. a marginal-preserving outcome-selective follow-up attack — selective under-follow-up of died daughters tuned so the pooled arm marginal stays inside the audit bound — combined with a true rescue and a real error-daughter-pruning confound, quantifying the masking hole disclosed below;
14. treatment event sensitivity sized to sit just inside the arm-audit equivalence band — the audit's own operating edge under a true rescue;
15. a true rescue with a completion ratio just outside the pediatric band edge — whether the band refuses at its boundary, not only deep inside it; and
16. a degenerate zero-division treatment arm scored only for fail-closed refusal.

All 22 configured gates passed in the deterministic release probe: 18/18 asserted scenarios (including both fail-closed cases) plus 4/4 declared boundary probes. The headline metrics:

| Metric | Observed at the bound seed |
|---|---|
| Strong rescue flagged | 905/1,000 (Wilson lower 88.5%) |
| Strong rescue **clean** under the pediatric band | 655/1,000 (Wilson lower 62.5% vs declared ≥55% floor) |
| Max false-generation upper bound, required non-generation scenarios | 0.564% |
| Clean signals, rescue + moderate cytostasis | **0/1,000** |
| Clean signals, marginal-preserving selective follow-up attack | 260/1,000 (upper 28.8%, declared ≤40%) — a published hole, not a hidden one |
| Audit-edge sensitivity drift caught / clean signals | 349/1,000 caught; 361/1,000 clean (upper 39.1%, declared ≤50%) — a published audit edge |
| Clean signals, rescue just outside the completion band | 1/1,000 (upper 0.56%, declared ≤2%) |
| Zero-division arm refused | 1,000/1,000 fail-closed |
| Measurement-drift audits (sensitivity / specificity / division / follow-up) | 1,000 / 992 / 1,000 / 999 flagged |
| Pure pruning and preservation flag sets | exact match in 964/1,000 and 834/1,000 |
| Effect-size detection at ratios 0.75 / 0.6875 / 0.625 | 22.5% / 41.4% / 60.1% — simulation results, not real-study power |
| Supplementary five-seed sweep | 5/5 seeds accepted; false-generation ceiling seed-invariant at 0.564% |

Of the flagged strong-rescue comparisons that did not certify clean, 250 carried an unresolved competing completion and 35 detected the effect internally but could not deconvolve and emitted no flag — none can advance a candidate. Every required or fail-closed scenario stayed within its declared ≤1% clean-signal ceiling. The detection-floor pool is disclosed in the receipt (`detection_floor_pool`): only uncompromised required generation scenarios feed the floor; the all-required-generation minimum (80.86%, including the deliberately dirty rescue-plus-cytostasis case) is published alongside it rather than hidden. All scenario hazard ceilings are bound in the frozen configuration file before each receipt run, so a ceiling can never be tuned to the data it certifies; declared ceilings and observed values are published side by side in the receipt. Because deterministic reproduction from one seed is not robustness, a supplementary bound receipt replays the identical configuration across five predeclared independent master seeds: all five passed all 22 scenarios with acceptance criteria met (5/5 seeds, receipt `TRACK2_SEED_SENSITIVITY.json`), and the minimum required-generation detection Wilson lower bound ranged only 88.52%–88.63%. This narrows but does not eliminate the seed-dependence caveat—the sweep exercises the same synthetic aggregate-count generator, so it answers "was the release seed lucky?" and nothing more. Two further resolution disclosures apply: at 1,000 replicates the Monte Carlo standard error near the 0.025 level is on the order of half a percentage point, so boundary-adjacent rates resolve coarsely (the observed 0.564% false-generation ceiling is compatible with a true hazard anywhere up to roughly 1%); and because every scenario shares one generator family, the receipt certifies counting-pipeline correctness—arithmetic, gate logic, and fail-closed behavior under that family—not robustness to model misspecification. The timestamped estimands of §4.2 (competing risks, hierarchical pairing, intercurrent-event handling) are specified above, not validated by this toy.

The new middle-ground scenarios expose the honest edges of the method:

- **Moderate cytostasis alone** (scenario 9) generated a generation flag in only 1/1,000 comparisons — a slower-division signal does not masquerade as rescue. Notably, the suite's own injected ~13% division reduction is also largely invisible to the cytostasis flag; the [0.75, 0.95) detection dead zone buys specificity with blindness and is a disclosed trade-off, not a certification of this effect's safety. The symmetric selection dead zone exists too: a relative error-daughter-reproduction ratio in (0.75, 1.25) trips neither pruning nor preservation flags and does not touch the completion gate, so modest selection can coexist with a clean signal.
- **Rescue mixed with moderate cytostasis** (scenario 10) uses the same strong rescue as the clean detection scenario, so the only difference is the injected ~13% completion loss: the generation component was still flagged in 833/1,000 comparisons but produced a clean signal in **0/1,000**. The completion-equivalence rule correctly refused to let the rescue signal pass while completion was non-equivalent. Under the looser legacy band a substantial fraction of these comparisons would have certified clean — the enforced pediatric band is what now prevents a cytostasis-contaminated run from advancing.
- **Sub-threshold effect plus small sensitivity drift** (scenario 11) is a published residual weakness: a true effect just below the detection boundary combined with sensitivity drift too small for the arm audits to detect produced a clean false-advance signal in 263/1,000 comparisons (95% Wilson upper bound 29.1%) and a generation flag in 348/1,000 (upper 37.8%), held to declared ceilings of ≤30% and ≤40% respectively. The scenario is gated as a boundary probe — those hazard bounds, not the usual flag-set and estimator assertions, are what it must satisfy — and is retained in the receipt precisely so this boundary is visible rather than hidden; the protocol's defense is that Gate 5's exposure-matched replicates, empirically sized calibration panels, and the pre-registered effect floor—not this aggregate toy—govern a real decision.
- **Miscalibrated shared panel** (scenario 12) produced no generation flags and 951/1,000 no-detectable-component calls: symmetric panel miscalibration cannot reach the ratio-level decision layer because arm-versus-arm audits, not the shared panel, carry the drift-detection burden. Calibration panels are drawn independently per scenario so Monte Carlo noise is decorrelated across the suite rather than shared.
- **Marginal-preserving selective follow-up** (scenario 13) is the quantified version of the masking hole disclosed below: under-following only the died daughters while the pooled treatment marginal stays ≈1.01× vehicle's — inside the 0.85 audit bound, so the follow-up audit never fires — suppressed the real error-daughter-pruning flag to 360/1,000 (from ~96% under full follow-up) and inflated the followed-cohort reproduction estimate by a ~0.27 log-ratio. A true rescue riding on that masked confound produced clean signals in 260/1,000 comparisons (Wilson upper 28.8%), held to a declared ≤40% hazard ceiling. The protocol's defense is the same as for scenario 11: outcome adjudication, prespecified follow-up quotas by outcome class, and timestamped lineage data — layers this aggregate toy does not model — govern a real decision.
- **Audit-band-edge sensitivity drift** (scenario 14) sizes treatment event sensitivity so the arm-versus-arm audit ratio lands just inside the no-bias band: the audit caught the drift in only 349/1,000 comparisons while 361/1,000 emitted clean signals on measurement-biased data (declared ceiling ≤50%). The band is a detection instrument with an operating edge, not a wall; this is why the exposure gate and the prespecified calibration-panel sizing, not the audit alone, carry the measurement-integrity burden.
- **Completion-band-edge rescue** (scenario 15) places a true rescue at a 0.929 completion ratio — just outside the 0.95 lower equivalence edge: 1/1,000 clean (declared ≤2%). The band refuses near its boundary, not only deep inside it, so a real rescue cannot ride a borderline completion loss through.
- **Zero-division arm** (scenario 16) is the degenerate-input receipt: every replicate refused a call through `insufficient_information` (1,000/1,000) — an arm with no divisions cannot be scored, only refused.
- The sparse mixed case (scenario 5) refused to issue a biological call in 852/1,000 comparisons — 716 generation-signal-incomplete-deconvolution plus 136 insufficient-information — evidence of fail-closed behavior; of the 148 interpretable comparisons, 139 (93.9%) carried a generation flag, none clean. This unfavorable-looking number is reported because interpretable-but-dirty replicates are where a selection-confounded method would falsely advance.

The analysis uses one observed-data information/QC rule in every scenario. An endpoint with inadequate event counts, unstable calibration, or a detected follow-up artifact returns `insufficient_information`, suppresses the corresponding biological flag, and contributes to the reported non-estimable fraction; no scenario-truth list can waive a failing endpoint. A clean generation signal requires statistically supported, concordant reductions in both conditional error per completed first division and error-bearing completed divisions per enrolled founder, plus a division-completion ratio confidence interval wholly contained in the predeclared 0.95–1.10 equivalence band — the same pediatric rule as the planned experiment. Intervals and performance summaries operate at the edit-event level and include Monte Carlo uncertainty. Across scenarios whose measurement survived the audits, observed interval-coverage Wilson lower bounds ranged 0.897–0.986 against the nominal 95% target and worst absolute log-ratio bias was 0.086 (about 9% multiplicative). Inside the few surviving replicates of the measurement-invalidated drift scenarios the estimator tail is worse — under injected event-specificity drift, eight surviving replicates showed absolute bias up to 0.344 with coverage lower bound 0.137 — exactly the behavior the fail-closed audits exist to suppress.

One reusable external truth-labeled calibration panel is shared across arm comparisons; it is not regenerated per arm or used as treatment-specific truth. Its reference labels are assumed to be established independently of treatment assignment and blinded to the benchmark analyst. Separate blinded arm-specific positive, negative, and division audits test measurement drift and trigger fail-closed invalidation; they do not recalibrate the treatment comparison.

Daughter reproduced/died/other label misclassification is not generated, corrected, or audited in v3, so those labels are assumed adjudicated. Outcome-dependent missingness deliberately constructed to preserve identical arm-level marginal follow-up is not identifiable from these aggregate sufficient statistics, and the follow-up audit keys on marginal deficit — an outcome-selective inflation pattern that keeps the pooled marginal inside its bound can partially mask a real error-daughter-pruning flag; scenario 13 quantifies that attack and bounds its false-clean hazard. Aggregate counts also cannot separate pre-division death from other causes of non-completion, and later daughter outcomes are secondary. Hierarchical dependence in the toy is generated on biological parameters only — an edit-event-scoped *technical* artifact (one bad antibody lot corrupting every clone of one edit event) is not modeled, and neither is cross-arm carryover or contamination, where material from one arm enters the other's counts. Measurement parameters are constant within a replicate — per-arm label sensitivity collapsing across imaging windows, or other time-varying drift, is not modeled and would dilute under the pooled arm audit. These boundaries require external outcome adjudication and timestamped lineage data; the benchmark does not establish general measurement-artifact robustness. It also does not model arm-interleaved versus arm-blocked execution order, arm-correlated site or batch effects, interaction noise between confound classes, or multiple-candidate multiplicity — a real multi-candidate screen requires its own comparison-wise error control — and replayed external cases would be illustrative, not independent validation of the analyzer.

The most important stress comparison is qualitative and explicitly limited to the configured toy bulk dynamics: a bulk-only endpoint can call improvement when the modeled new-error probability is unchanged because abnormal daughters are pruned or division completion falls. The component analysis keeps observed event-call probability, followed event-positive daughter reproduction, division completion, general daughter outcome, measurement quality, and bulk composition separate rather than collapsing them into one “rescue” label.

Reproduce the receipt with:

```powershell
python scripts/run_generation_selection_benchmark.py `
  --config configs/track2-generation-selection-benchmark.json `
  --output local_dev/track2-generation-selection.json
```

The versioned receipt is [TRACK2_GENERATION_SELECTION_BENCHMARK.json](TRACK2_GENERATION_SELECTION_BENCHMARK.json). Before real data are unblinded, the protocol implementation must add timestamped lineage IDs, competing outcomes, field/batch effects, empirically justified calibration, dropout and censoring models, and the prespecified mixed-effects analyses above. The aggregate-count benchmark tests recoverability only for its configured effect sizes and artifact scenarios—not real-data power, imaging accuracy, biological correctness, candidate activity, treatment efficacy, or benefit to a person.

## 5. Oncology and pediatric safety boundary

MVA is a cancer-predisposition disorder. Any intervention that amplifies proteostasis could help abnormal or malignant clones survive. HSF1 can support malignant-cell proliferation and survival [Dai et al., 2007](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17889646/). A short ex-vivo normalization of one chromosome endpoint cannot settle long-term tumor risk.

Relevant counterevidence must also be retained: the current MIPLYFFA label reports no increased tumor incidence in two-year rat and 26-week rasH2-mouse carcinogenicity studies at systemic exposures approximately eight- and eleven-fold the human exposure by AUC. Those negative animal studies reduce, but do not eliminate, concern in a cancer-predisposed germline context that was not studied.

The ex-vivo counterscreen therefore asks whether arimoclomol:

- increases survival, reproductive output, or clonogenicity of existing aneuploid cells;
- protects transformed cells or alters response to likely oncology agents;
- reduces antitumor stress responses — aneuploid human cells already show HSF1 deficiency and impaired HSP90-dependent protein folding, so proteostasis amplification could preferentially protect abnormal clones [Donnelly et al., 2014](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25205676/);
- creates an apparent benefit by killing cells or preventing division;
- amplifies HSF1 beyond engaged-stress co-induction into checkpoint antagonism — sustained HSF1 overexpression binds Cdc20, inhibits APC/C activity, and increases aneuploidy and micronuclei in tumor cells, an effect stronger in p53-defective backgrounds [Lee et al., 2008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18059335/); the exposure gate, the participant-line p53-intact context, and the prespecified HSF1-dependence controls bound this risk, but a hyper-amplified HSF1 signature at qualifying exposure is a stop signal.

Label-derived hypersensitivity, embryofetal, fertility, renal/OCT2, and drug-interaction risks are external clinical constraints. This ex-vivo screen cannot clear them.

Any increased malignant/aneuploid clone fitness is a program-level stop even if BUBR1 abundance rises. No oncology therapy should be changed on the basis of this proposal.

## 6. Falsification and decision table

| Result | Predeclared interpretation | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Either variant fails orthogonal confirmation | input hypothesis invalid | stop |
| Variants confirmed cis | submitted recessive pair rejected | stop participant-pair program |
| Phase unresolved | compound-heterozygous status unknown | continue variant biology only |
| Stop transcript persists and is NMD-insensitive | specific NMD-null model rejected | assay truncated product and revise |
| N1002K abundance and half-life are WT-equivalent | stability/chaperone premise unsupported | stop arimoclomol; functional biology may continue with a different candidate |
| N1002K abundance, localization, checkpoint behavior, and segregation outcomes are all WT-equivalent | exact-allele causal chain unsupported in the tested system | stop participant-specific functional program |
| Exact correction fails to rescue | causal attribution unresolved | investigate clone/background; do not advance |
| Arimoclomol lacks the prespecified chaperone-response signal at ≤2 micromolar under acceptable measured exposure | concentration/mechanistic bridge fails | stop repositioning |
| Effect appears only at ≥50–100 micromolar | nontranslational mechanism only | no-go for repositioning |
| Protein increases without checkpoint/segregation correction | marker change, not functional rescue | stop |
| Bulk abnormal fraction falls but `gC`/`gE` do not concordantly fall under completion equivalence | selection, cytostasis, or mixed process | do not call rescue |
| `gC` and `gE` fall but abnormal-clone retention rises | mixed result with oncology concern | stop pending independent safety work |
| `gC` and `gE` concordantly fall under completion equivalence at a prespecified nominal concentration ≤2 micromolar with medium and intracellular exposure characterized, exact correction triangulates the mechanism, and ex-vivo clone-fitness/toxicity counterscreens show no prespecified hazard signal | bounded preclinical screening signal | independent replication; still not treatment, efficacy, or safety evidence |

No branch demonstrates clinical benefit. A negative result is valuable: it prevents an unsupported medicine from advancing and clarifies whether N1002K is a stability, non-abundance functional, or WT-like allele in the tested systems. If Gate 4 finds no stability defect, the arimoclomol fork ends and no other surveyed repositioned candidate survives the same gates — the comparator table shows every alternative fails earlier at the mechanism-bridge or human-exposure rung.

The program then degrades into an ordered fallback ladder rather than a void, with each rung entering through the same exposure-bounded gates instead of skipping them:

1. **SIRT2/NAD+ abundance axis** — the named next hypothesis class [North et al., 2014](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24825348/); [Baker et al., 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23242215/). Only NMN/NR-type NAD+ precursors belong on this rung: nicotinamide *inhibits* sirtuins and would push the axis the wrong direction.
2. **Engineered suppressor-tRNA arm, gated on the Gate 2 transcript result** — c.2210T>G creates a UGA opal stop, the most readthrough-permissive stop class; anticodon-edited tRNAs that decode it have restored full-length protein from PTC alleles in mouse and patient-cell models, and uniquely also inhibit NMD, synergistically raising the target transcript [Wang et al., 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35322228/); [Albers et al., 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37258671/); [Lueck et al., 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30778053/). The rung exists only if Gate 2 finds substrate (a persistent or perturbation-recoverable stop transcript); the class is preclinical — no sup-tRNA has dosed a human and delivery is the binding constraint — and the readthrough product carries Trp/Arg/Cys rather than Leu at position 737, so it re-enters Gates 4–6 as a *new missense allele*, not as a confirmed rescue. Gentamicin-class agents are excluded as leads for chronic pediatric oto-/nephrotoxicity, and ataluren's EU withdrawal plus the published firefly-luciferase readthrough artifact [McElroy et al., 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23824517/) keep it as a comparator at most.
3. **Sub-case comparators** — L-glutamine (an approved pediatric oral product whose HSF1/HSP70 co-induction places it in the same mechanistic class as arimoclomol; it addresses only an *exposure* failure, never a mechanism failure [Singleton and Wischmeyer, 2008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18596307/)) and PP2A-B56 biased-stabilization agents such as DT-061, which exist only for the recruitment-defect sub-case — zero kinetochore evidence and an oncology-developed class in a cancer-predisposition setting make it a flagged comparator, not a lead [Leonard et al., 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32315618/).
4. **WT BUB1B supplementation (gene-therapy class)** — for the branch where N1002K remains defective at normalized abundance: ectopic WT BUB1B restored checkpoint activity in MVA patient cell lines and sustained BubR1 expression protects mice from aneuploidy — but the BUB1B ORF fits a single AAV and no published AAV-BUB1B program exists, so this is a research-direction class entering the same gates, floored by the unaffected-carrier state [Wijshake et al., 2012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23300461/).

Classes explicitly *not* advanced on this ladder: low-dose HSP90 inhibition (it depleted, not stabilized, the unstable BUBR1 constructs), HDAC6 inhibition (hyperacetylates HSP90 and impairs client folding — the wrong direction), TFEB/lysosomal-biogenesis agonists (BUBR1 turnover is proteasomal), proteasome inhibitors as rescue (control reagent only), and any "checkpoint-strengthening" small molecule — no such pharmacology exists; Mps1, PLK1, and Aurora B inhibitors all weaken arrest or error correction and are harm-direction controls here.

If lineage-resolved imaging proves infeasible at execution, the fallback outcome measure is a fixed-cohort first-division micronucleus/endpoint-karyotype design under the same generation-versus-selection estimands — a coarser instrument, not a different claim.

## 7. Innovation, impact, and scalability

### Scientific rigor

The proposal separates phase, transcript fate, exact-allele abundance, direct function, drug concentration and time profile, outcome generation, clone selection, and toxicity. Exact correction is the causal anchor; expression-matched WT BUB1B is a pathway-rescue control. Label-estimated serum concentrations inform a conservative screening boundary; they do not establish human-equivalent cell exposure.

### Potential impact

The first useful output is not a prescription. It is a staged, decisive answer to three questions. The immediate GO is only for Gate 0 confirmation and Gate 1 phase; every costly downstream layer is conditional on the preceding result:

1. Is the pair truly in trans?
2. Is N1002K a rescuable functional-protein deficit?
3. Does an approved-drug probe reduce new errors, or merely change which cells remain?

A positive result nominates a bounded preclinical direction — concretely, the first de-risked, exposure-realistic ex-vivo go/no-go for a repurposable approved drug in a disease with no approved therapy, an answer a clinical team could evaluate through approved channels rather than infer from literature. A negative result is nearly as valuable: it retires a plausible-looking candidate before it can absorb a family's hope or a trial slot, and it leaves a reusable mechanistic profile of the exact allele. It does not itself protect or clinically benefit the child.

The conditional probe is also not the whole discovery program. The same firewall gates a prespecified second lane: a phenotype screen of the marketed/available subset of an approved-medicine collection through a qualified screening partner (protocol `track2-candidate-search-v3`, versioned in this repository), so the deliverable is a ranked, gate-audited candidate list — the targeted-mechanism lane and the collection screen lane each carry their own exposure rationale and neither inherits the other's result.

The participant-facing output is deliberately modest: no research result is sent directly to the family and no drug instruction is produced. Through organizer-approved clinical channels, a qualified team could translate validated results into a one-page status record stating what was confirmed, what remains unknown, which gate stopped, and why no treatment conclusion follows. That makes a negative result legible instead of disappearing and minimizes additional sampling by prioritizing residual material and exact stop rules.

### Innovation

Arimoclomol itself is not claimed as novel, and the generation-versus-selection decomposition is not either — proliferation-corrected genotoxicity assays, joint missegregation–fitness modeling, and single-cell karyotype-fitness inference are established antecedents (§4.1). The innovation is the assembled contract: the exact-allele and exposure funnel coupled to an implemented false-rescue firewall that separates new-error generation from daughter outcome, cytostasis, and measurement failure under a receipt-bound, fail-closed decision rule. The aggregate-count benchmark distinguishes only the biological effects and measurement artifacts explicitly configured in its final receipt; it is not evidence that the classifier will succeed on real biological data.

Field context sharpens the difference: in the bounded, non-exhaustive survey of public Track 2 approaches identified during preparation, entries predominantly rank or score drug candidates from literature and in-silico evidence, or propose pharmacologic elimination of aneuploid cells — a strategy this contract is explicitly designed to reject as false rescue rather than certify. This entry contributes the orthogonal phase gate, exact-correction causal anchor, function-when-abundant gate, measured-exposure gate, generation-versus-selection estimands with completion equivalence, prespecified oncology stop rules, and a cryptographically bound reproducibility package as one falsifiable system; none of the approaches we reviewed carried more than a subset of those components.

### Scalability

The reusable unit is a five-stage assay contract:

`phase/RNA` → `protein stability` → `exact function` → `generation-selection deconvolution` → `independent replication`

The software component uses the Python standard library, is config-driven, and runs its predeclared synthetic comparisons on a CPU without participant data. Its public observed-run schema, calibration layer, independent mechanism flags, and receipt-bound acceptance gates can be reused in other studies that must separate event generation from post-event outcome. Adaptation still requires a gene-specific functional assay, empirically calibrated observation model, measured exposure, and disease-specific safety boundary; one cell-line workflow cannot classify every VUS.

Execution is modular and outsourceable rather than “fast”: a clinical genetics laboratory owns confirmation and phase; an RNA/editing core owns the gated allelic series; a proteomics/LC-MS core owns abundance and exposure; an imaging core exports the prespecified aggregate-count schema; and an independent site repeats only a fully qualified positive. Each handoff has a bounded artifact—phase record, allele-function scorecard, measured-exposure table, blinded count table, and replication decision—so a failed gate stops downstream spend. The public software and reporting contract can be reused without sharing this participant's controlled data. The contract ports directly to the other MVA molecular classes (biallelic TRIP13 and CEP57 families, which share the segregation-error phenotype but differ in checkpoint involvement) and more generally to any chromosome-instability or bulk-fitness-confounded rescue screen where selection can masquerade as rescue — the generation-versus-selection estimands and the completion-equivalence rule transfer unchanged; only the gene-specific functional assay must be rebuilt.

Wet-lab duration and cost are not claimed from a desktop estimate. They should be quoted by the executing clinical/research laboratories after sample availability, editing strategy, sequencing depth, and replication sites are fixed. What this entry does fix is the ordering and the stop structure: Gates 0–1 (orthogonal confirmation and direct phase) serialize before any cellular work; RNA characterization and residual-material recovery run in parallel with editing design; Gates 2–4 (abundance, function-when-abundant, stability) serialize because each licenses the next; the probe screen opens only after a qualified stability defect; and independent replication runs only on a fully qualified positive. Material availability follows a no-invasive-biopsy policy: residual clinical material is the first source, an established line the second, and a new collection requires fresh clinical authorization — the analysis plan does not presume any new sampling. Every gate also carries a prespecified stop, so spend halts at the first failed rung rather than at the end of a budget.

## 8. Limitations

1. The variants have not been orthogonally confirmed or phased in the evidence available to this entry.
2. p.Leu737Ter NMD and p.Asn1002Lys abundance/function are inferred hypotheses, not participant measurements.
3. There is no direct BUB1B, N1002K, MVA, checkpoint, or chromosome-segregation evidence for arimoclomol.
4. In an engineered U2OS assay, pharmacologic HSP90 inhibition depleted the tested I909T and L1012P constructs; arimoclomol studies most consistently document HSP70-family induction. Applicability to N1002K is unknown.
5. Label-estimated pediatric mean serum concentrations are below the nominal culture concentrations used in many positive mutant-protein experiments; this comparison is not PK equivalence.
6. MIPLYFFA's FDA indication is for use in combination with miglustat in NPC, not for MVA or monotherapy; the label says the clinical mechanism is unknown.
7. Cell correction is not organismal or clinical benefit. Developmental timing, tissue specificity, pharmacology, and cancer risk remain unresolved.
8. The synthetic simulator validates declared software behavior only. It does not validate real imaging, real effect sizes, or the proposed medicine.
9. Patient-derived cultures represent one child; repeated wells do not create population-level evidence.
10. Bounded searches can miss unindexed, unpublished, non-English, or newly released evidence. Regulatory status and challenge rules require refresh before the irreversible submission.
11. Exact-correction controls can carry their own editing artifacts — on-target chromothripsis or truncations, copy-neutral LOH, p53-pathway selection, and passage drift — so a corrected clone is itself a hypothesis until the Gate 3 structural, LOH, and TP53 status controls pass.

## 9. Methods-form abstract (under 500 words)

We propose arimoclomol as a single exposure-gated ex-vivo screening hypothesis for a candidate BUB1B-associated MVA genotype containing p.Leu737Ter and p.Asn1002Lys. The proposal is not a treatment recommendation. Phase is unresolved, the stop-allele transcript has not been measured, and the missense allele has no direct functional evidence. The program therefore begins with orthogonal confirmation and direct phase. Cis phase rejects the pair.

If trans is established, allele-specific RNA and independent NMD perturbations test the stop-allele model. An endogenous isogenic allelic series then tests whether p.Asn1002Lys lowers full-length BUBR1 abundance or half-life, disrupts localization or PP2A-B56 recruitment, weakens the spindle checkpoint, or increases chromosome-segregation errors. Exact correction is the causal anchor; expression-matched WT BUB1B is a pathway-rescue positive control.

Arimoclomol was generated as a candidate through a mechanism-first primary-literature and regulatory search. MIPLYFFA's FDA indication is for use in combination with miglustat for neurological NPC in patients aged at least two years and its label provides estimated pediatric serum concentrations. Patient-derived Gaucher cells, transfected human rhodopsin-model cells, and NPC fibroblasts (TFEB/CLEAR-driven enlargement of the mature mutant-NPC1 pool across genotypes) show HSP70-family and mutant-protein biochemical effects, but there is no BUB1B-direct evidence and the label says the clinical mechanism in NPC is unknown. Fog et al. used 100–400 micromolar for HSPA1A induction and 50–800 micromolar in residual GCase-activity studies, versus a label-estimated pediatric mean serum peak of approximately 1.67 micromolar. That nominal ratio is not PK equivalence. Randomized ALS and inclusion-body-myositis trials did not improve efficacy outcomes; neither disease is BUB1B-associated MVA, but the null results are counterevidence against assuming a broadly effective heat-shock-response therapy. We therefore screen 0.2–2 micromolar, treat 5 micromolar as non-advancing exploration, and reject repositioning if no result occurs at no more than 2 micromolar under acceptable measured exposure.

The main methodological innovation separates true reduction of new segregation errors from selective loss/preservation of abnormal clones, cytostasis, toxicity, and measurement bias. In a pre-treatment-enrolled founder cohort, the proposed real experiment uses lineage imaging to estimate first-division errors both per completed division and per enrolled founder, with completion/death as competing outcomes; daughter retention and later division intensity remain separate. The implemented paired hierarchical aggregate-count benchmark tests whether its simplified analysis distinguishes configured biological effects from measurement artifacts while respecting edit-event nesting and observed-data non-estimability. Bulk-only results are scoped to the configured toy dynamics. This is a software and study-design stress test, not biological validation or a full lineage-analysis implementation.

The candidate advances only if exact N1002K dysfunction exists, arimoclomol produces the prespecified chaperone-response signal at no more than 2 micromolar under acceptable measured exposure, soluble and correctly localized full-length BUBR1 increases, checkpoint function improves, both fixed-cohort generation metrics concordantly improve under completion equivalence, exact correction triangulates causality, and ex-vivo oncology/clone-fitness counterscreens show no prespecified hazard signal. This would remain a preclinical screening result, not evidence of clinical safety or benefit. Any treatment, dose, combination, or cure claim remains out of scope.
