# Track 2 program gates

Status: synthetic software contract only. This document names no subject-specific gene, allele, or medicine.

A filled community template is not a wet-lab result. These gates recompute phase, hypomorph eligibility, and independent-site replication so a ticked boolean cannot advance a program.

## Confirmation

- Accepts the community confirmation record.
- Pass only when specimen identity is resolved, a second aliquot was used, the layout is complete, both strands were counted, both alleles are `both_alleles_observed`, and the producer is `synthetic_handoff` or a k-mer layout whose digest matches the pinned FASTQ-name digest **and** whose nested `kmer_confirmation` object shows both alleles at k=31 and k=51 with reverse-complement counting **and** whose layout object shows eight complete files (`n_files` equals `expected_n_files`, `incomplete` is false). A digest-only k-mer tick cannot pass. Nested k-mer ticks on a truncated five-file layout cannot pass. An unlinked tick cannot pass. An RNA or RNA-derived library cannot confirm genomic genotype even when those floors are complete (`rna_not_genomic`). Unlabeled library class cannot stand in (`library_molecule_required`). Genomic DNA from an unmatched genotype line cannot stand in (`unmatched_confirmation_specimen`). Unlabeled confirmation specimen cannot stand in (`confirmation_specimen_required`).
- A declared both-alleles-observed call is a stop when those floors are not met (`declared_overstrong`). Incomplete inputs hold. This is not a variant caller.

## Phase

- Accepts the community phase record.
- Requires two distinct guide configurations and both haplotype molecule counts at or above the declared floor. Duplicate config ids cannot confirm trans.
- Each guide configuration must record a `linkage_call` of trans, cis, or unresolved and a `strand_balance`. Missing linkage cannot confirm trans (`linkage_not_recorded`). Disagreeing guides hold (`linkage_disagreement`). Per-guide full-span molecule counts must also meet the floor (`guide_span_floor`). Strand balance outside `[0.35, 0.65]` holds (`strand_imbalance`).
- A declared trans call is overridden to unresolved when molecule floors fail.
- Cis stops the pair story only when independent guides agree on cis. Trans guides cannot upgrade a cis declaration (`declared_linkage_mismatch`).
- Agreeing trans linkage still holds when the recovered split is compatible with allelic dropout (`dropout_compatible`). Dropout rejection is required before trans can pass. It is not itself a diagnosis.
- A computational haplotype caller cannot confirm trans even when molecule floors and dropout rejection pass (`computational_phase_not_molecule`). An RNA-seq phase cannot stand in (`rna_phase_not_genomic`). Unlabeled phase method cannot stand in (`phase_method_required`). Spanning molecules from an unmatched genotype line cannot stand in (`unmatched_phase_specimen`). Unlabeled phase specimen cannot stand in (`phase_specimen_required`). Spanning molecules on the confirmed assay line, or parental genotypes with `phase_specimen=parental`, may pass. Do not bump the phase schema; `phase_method` and `phase_specimen` default to unlabeled.

## Hypomorph

- Accepts the community allele-function scorecard.
- The missense row must declare `allele_specificity`. Only `exact` can count as function identity. Analog, homolog, or nearby alleles stop (`analog_as_exact_function`). Analog recreation folds into the same reason; do not mint it as a second family.
- A missense stability defect on the exact allele is required before a chaperone-style probe is eligible. Analog, homolog, or nearby alleles cannot stand in.
- Positive stability or function calls must declare `assay_class`. `computational_structure` or `computational_pathogenicity` cannot make a probe eligible (`computational_not_assay`). Unlabeled positives hold (`assay_class_required`).
- Exact correction must reverse every missense-positive function endpoint **in a wet cellular basal endogenous assay on the confirmed assay line**. A still-positive corrected row stops (`correction_did_not_reverse_defect`). A computational corrected row cannot reverse a wet missense defect (`computational_correction_not_assay`). An unlabeled corrected row cannot reverse it either (`unlabeled_correction_not_assay`). A cell-free biophysical corrected row cannot reverse a cellular missense defect (`cell_free_correction_not_cellular`). Unlabeled correction system class cannot stand in (`unlabeled_correction_system_class`). An imposed-stress corrected row cannot reverse a basal missense defect (`imposed_stress_correction_not_basal`). Unlabeled correction condition class cannot stand in (`unlabeled_correction_condition_class`). An ectopic WT cDNA parked in `exact_corrected` cannot reverse an endogenous missense defect (`ectopic_correction_not_endogenous`). Unlabeled correction expression class cannot stand in (`unlabeled_correction_expression_class`). An unmatched-line correction cannot reverse an assay-matched missense defect (`unmatched_correction_not_assay_matched`). Unlabeled correction specimen cannot stand in (`unlabeled_correction_specimen_class`). Analog, homolog, or nearby parked on `exact_corrected` cannot reverse an exact missense defect (`analog_correction_not_exact`). Unlabeled correction specificity cannot stand in (`correction_specificity_required`).
- Reciprocal recreation must restore every missense-positive function endpoint **in a wet assay**. A healthier or off-target correction clone whose reintroduced-missense row stays negative stops (`recreation_did_not_restore_defect`). A computational recreated row cannot restore a wet missense defect (`computational_recreation_not_assay`). An unlabeled recreated row cannot restore it either (`unlabeled_recreation_not_assay`). Analog, homolog, or nearby recreation cannot restore an exact missense defect (`analog_as_exact_function`); unlabeled recreation specificity cannot stand in (`recreation_specificity_required`). Those unlabeled and analog-recreation stops are the same `function.analog_or_computational` family, tested in program gates, not a second method-delta family. Missing the `recreated_missense` class is malformed, not a method-delta family.
- A missense-positive function call must be **basal**. A defect confined to imposed extrinsic stress cannot make a probe eligible (`imposed_stress_not_basal_defect`). Unlabeled condition class cannot stand in (`condition_class_required`). Putting the missense back only under imposed stress cannot restore a basal defect (`recreation_imposed_stress_not_basal`). Disease mitotic failure is not imposed extrinsic stress. A chaperone PD tick remains a separate hold (`pd_not_checkpoint`); it is not this family.
- A missense-positive function call must be **cellular**. A cell-free biophysical row labeled wet, including a purified-protein thermal shift, cannot make a probe eligible (`cell_free_not_cellular_defect`). Unlabeled system class cannot stand in (`system_class_required`). Putting the missense back only in a cell-free assay cannot restore a cellular defect (`recreation_cell_free_not_cellular`). This is independent of computational-as-assay and of imposed extrinsic stress.
- A missense-positive function call must be **endogenous**. An ectopic transgene, cDNA, or overexpression row cannot make a probe eligible (`ectopic_not_endogenous_defect`). Unlabeled expression class cannot stand in (`expression_class_required`). Putting the missense back only as an ectopic construct cannot restore an endogenous defect (`recreation_ectopic_not_endogenous`). Unlabeled recreation expression class cannot stand in (`recreation_expression_class_required`). This is independent of cell-free biophysical and of imposed extrinsic stress.
- A missense-positive function call must come from the **confirmed assay line**. An unmatched genotype line cannot make a probe eligible (`unmatched_not_assay_matched_defect`). Unlabeled specimen class cannot stand in (`specimen_class_required`). Putting the missense back only on an unmatched line cannot restore an assay-matched defect (`recreation_unmatched_not_assay_matched`). Unlabeled recreation specimen cannot stand in (`recreation_specimen_class_required`). This is independent of ectopic expression. Do not bump the scorecard schema; `specimen_class` defaults to unlabeled.
- A wild-type positive stability call fails closed.
- Both missense stability endpoints negative stops the probe. Both not assessable cannot pass.
- A compound-row defect cannot replace a missing missense result.
- The `missense_carrier` row (WT/N1002K, one mutant allele) is the promised carrier-dose control and is required in the class set. For each function endpoint, a carrier call that is positive in a valid basal cellular endogenous assay on the confirmed line while the `stop`-class carrier comparator is validly negative means the missense allele dysfunctions beyond one-allele dose loss — possible dominant interference — and the stabilization branch stops (`dominant_interference_possible`): adding back mutant protein could worsen the phenotype. Carrier-positive with the stop comparator unmeasured, unlabeled, or otherwise uninterpretable holds instead (`carrier_dose_control_required`): the dose interpretation is not assessable, and that is not a falsification. Carrier-positive with a validly positive stop comparator is dose-consistent — ordinary haploinsufficiency — and the gate proceeds to the stability checks. Carrier positives are held to the same assay/condition/system/expression/specimen class contract as missense positives, and carrier-specific holds cannot be overwritten by the compound-positivity override.
- Checkpoint-ready is a separate flag. It stays false until the missense checkpoint endpoint is positive. Stability-only eligibility can still open an isogenic assay; it cannot open replication.
- Optional chaperone or aneuploidy-stress PD endpoints are pharmacodynamic markers, not target engagement. They cannot make checkpoint-ready true. A PD-positive missense row without checkpoint is a hypomorph hold (`pd_not_checkpoint`) and cannot open an imaging campaign.

## Transcript

- Accepts the community transcript record.
- Unmeasured stop-class or missense RNA holds. A depleted stop transcript plus expressed missense can pass only from an allele-specific RNA method on the confirmed assay line. A computational NMD predictor cannot stand in (`computational_transcript_not_assay`). A protein-surrogate row cannot stand in (`protein_not_transcript`). Unlabeled transcript method cannot stand in (`transcript_method_required`). RNA from an unmatched genotype line cannot stand in (`unmatched_transcript_specimen`). Unlabeled specimen cannot stand in (`transcript_specimen_required`). Missense not expressed stops. Truncated-product or persistent stop RNA holds the chaperone-style pair program until the model is revised.

## Replication

- Accepts the community replication record.
- Stop if clone-safety stopped or the exposure object is a stop.
- Hold if co-primary endpoints are not declared concordant.
- Hold if the nested clone-safety, exposure, and computed-concordance objects are not all passes. Green booleans alone cannot advance.
- A ticked concordance boolean cannot override a computed miss. A computed pass cannot override a site that still declared discordant.
- Concordance is computed from lineage counts, nested by edit event **and clone**: both error-per-completed-division and error-bearing-completion-per-enrolled-founder must fall in **every** clone that has both arms, completion must stay inside the pediatric band, competing pre-division death, dropout, or no-division cannot rise, at least two clones per event are required, and at least three edit events are required. A hero clone cannot pool a discordant clone into a pass. Unlocked, unblinded, or missing lock/blind labels cannot pass. One pretty table cannot pass.
- Count identity: the blinded imaging table and the lineage table must map to the same arm × event × clone × run keys and all thirteen shared nonnegative-integer counts, including zero-valued positive- and negative-daughter outcomes and the three first-attempt competing-risk counts, before concordance or replication. The three optional daughter-slot and multipolar-division fields are identity-compared when present. Blinded event, clone, and run keys must be positive integers; numeric strings, fractions, and Booleans are not coerced into identity. An omitted zero is not silently supplied. Competing-risk labels on the lineage table forbid treating the blinded rows as a rescue claim. After those numeric checks pass, every measured-exposure row must carry a canonical semantic profile id and unique measurement-execution id, and every treatment result must resolve exactly once to an eligible measured-exposure execution through that recomputed profile digest, founder-derived support-record anchor, declared functional support, assay run, culture batch, sample relation, and actual timing. All declared supports and per-record assay-run ids must be consumed; unlinked rows cannot declare functional assay context or evade audit by dropping a profile id; functional execution ids are globally unique across every aggregate row and arm, including unpaired vehicle rows; measurement and vehicle-control record ids are disjoint; and an unstratified analysis cannot pool different semantic profiles. Repeated measurement executions of the same profile across batches are allowed. Each paired vehicle resolves to a distinct explicit control record with a matched endpoint. Missing, stale, unrelated, duplicated, unused, pooled, exploratory, pulse-only, or temporally impossible links hold at count identity. The gate then verifies the v3 allocation: treatment is applied per functional-execution well, randomization and analysis use exact same-row biological pairs, and biological replication is at the clone/edit-event level. The v4 input digest and its separate pre-reveal record bind the full arm-blind manifest, exact support ordering, 0.75-filtered support count and digest, algorithm, generator, and selection rule before an experiment-bound v3 seed commitment is written to a second record. Lock, input record, seed record, reveal, selected-vector record, final plan record, and exposure must occur in that order with four distinct record ids. An accepted raw 256-bit draw indexes the canonical support directly; the incomplete modulo bucket is rejected without synthesized redraw. Both allocation and inference use digest-bound explicit left-to-right binary64 reductions. Both raw generation differences must be lower and both equal-pair-weighted one-sided p-values must be at most 0.05 after the frozen context-specific dosing/acquisition linear, squared, and cross-product residualization. Exactness is conditional on rejection acceptance and applies only to the global Fisher sharp null of no effect on any execution under the declared uniform assignment model, not to a weak average-effect null. The uniform pre-outcome draw, consistency, no interference/carryover/cross-well contamination, fixed-manifest/no-exclusion, and arm-blinded ascertainment assumptions are not software-attested. Lineage/exposure classification disagreement fails closed. Current unattested entropy permits only a conditional synthetic pass; non-synthetic inference requires authenticated randomization and otherwise holds. The fixed-seed synthetic relabel helper rejects non-synthetic data and does not authorize seed searching or observed/real resealing. This is conditional randomization and metadata consistency, not proof of entropy honesty, physical sample identity, biological rescue, or efficacy. See `reports/TRACK2_RANDOMIZATION_CONTRACT.md`.
- Assay power: a locked plan must declare at least three edit events, two clones, and 24 opportunities, with a treatment error rate below the vehicle rate, a detection rate at or above the predeclared floor, and locked completion rates inside the pediatric band. Unlocked, underpowered, shrunk-after-the-lock, or pediatric-crash tables cannot pass. A locked plan is not a completed study.
- Nested clone-safety and concordance objects must carry the same lineage source fingerprint. A hand-authored pass table from a different lineage cannot advance.
- Count rows that cannot exist fail closed: first-attempt outcomes must partition opportunities, positive and negative divisions must sum to detected divisions when both are present, and followed error daughters cannot exceed two per error division.
- Advance only when nested clone-safety, exposure, computed concordance, hypomorph, hypothesis, confirmation, phase, and transcript objects passed, both declared and computed endpoints agree, the replicating site is different from the originating site, `checkpoint_ready` is true, and nested child-claim strength is `conditional_ex_vivo`. Direct API use cannot skip confirmation, phase, or transcript.

## Stop-early pipeline

The checked-in synthetic toolkit holds at confirmation because the public fixture is incomplete. Geometry ranking runs before confirmation and passes; it cannot make checkpoint-ready true. A ranking table marked `used_as_function` stops the pipeline. A FoldX-on-AlphaFold or AlphaMissense ranking holds and cannot open confirmation. An exact-role residue that is not claimed as exact also holds. Inflated pLDDT, RSA outside the unit interval, negative polar contacts, two ranking roles sharing a residue id, or an extra ranking row hold. Phase and every later gate except the next-experiment chooser are skipped. The chooser still runs and ranks confirmation first. That is the intended demo, not a child's outcome.

A separate save-path simulation copies those fixtures, fills complete synthetic lab objects, and checks that the pipeline can advance when every nested contract is constructed to pass. The current replay opens **1/1 constructed synthetic true path** and blocks **126/126 synthetic false paths from advancing: 100 final stops, 22 final holds, 4 contract-violation errors**. Current v2 records use `blocked_from_advancing`; only historical v1 receipts used `killed` for any non-advance, and no hold is a falsification. The suite blocks cis linkage, missing linkage, computational or RNA-seq phase, unmatched phase specimen, analog-as-function, analog correction as reversal of an exact missense, computational missense, computational correction, cell-free correction, imposed-stress correction, ectopic WT correction, unmatched correction specimen, failed reciprocal recreation, imposed extrinsic stress, cell-free biophysical labeled wet, ectopic transgene or cDNA expression as a cellular hypomorph, unmatched assay specimen as a cellular hypomorph, heat-shock PD, competing risk, organ-size overclaim, incomplete confirmation, RNA confirmation, unmatched confirmation specimen, computational transcript, unmatched transcript specimen, predicted-stability-as-function, probe-before-identity, digest-only k-mer confirmation, dropout-compatible phase, strand imbalance, unlocked or underpowered imaging plans, missing controls, failed controls, observed stability without an assay, pediatric-crash power plans, observed endpoints on unlocked counts, an exposure table collaged with attractive functional results from a different execution, and context-specific quadratic or dosing-by-acquisition order surfaces that mimic treatment. It also blocks blinded-count/lineage-table disagreement, a single discordant clone inside a pooled-significant table, declared-but-unticked confirmation floors, transcript status and specimen-binding violations, nominal-only exposure, missing vehicle baseline, nonpositive or mis-timed exposure windows, under-followed clone-safety daughters, censored daughter fates diluting the resolved-fate denominators, multipolar daughter slots declared but under-followed or not co-declared with a multipolar-division count, a dominant-interfering carrier phenotype, an unmeasured or unmatched carrier-dose comparator, a stabilization claim advanced without function measured at abundance, undeclared or uncleared selection-agent carryover including culture-batch taint, blinded and lineage tables disagreeing on first-attempt competing-risk counts, missing linkage or two-guide phase requirements, replication declared discordant or same-site, composite multi-file tampering, and malformed payloads that raise rather than stop. Geometry ranking can prioritize a wet assay; it cannot make checkpoint-ready true.

Identity gates come first. Declaring a probe, imaging, or replication spend while confirmation, phase, or transcript is still open is a stop (`probe_before_identity`). An unrecognized declared gate cannot stand in (`unknown_next_gate`). Declaring an already-passed earlier gate cannot stand in (`stale_declared_gate`; hold). The shared generation gate names assay power or concordance according to the recommended spend.
