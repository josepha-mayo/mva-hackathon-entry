# Candidate-ledger release contract

Status: future attempt-2 quarantine contract only. The frozen v3 release manifest is unchanged. This document does not authorize publication, a freeze, or a submission.

Candidate-ledger schema `mva.track2-candidate-ledger/v7` requires a replicated functional phenocopy and an active lead to bind same-lane sample-stewardship held-out and replication completion receipts, and to match the same-lane context-qualification mitotic class and transformation state. Historical v2/v3/v4/v5/v6 ledgers remain historical bytes and are not silently reinterpreted as v7.

## Problem closed by this contract

A narrative can name a candidate while a stale receipt silently reflects an older ledger. Digesting the two files independently is insufficient unless the release gate also proves that the receipt is the exact deterministic output of the released ledger.

The future attempt-2 quarantine therefore adds two fixed artifact roles:

- `release/track2-candidate-ledger.json`
- `release/track2-candidate-ranking.json`

Both roles must be `planned` or both must be `released`. They cannot have mixed status. A planned artifact must be absent and use a null digest. A released artifact must exist at its fixed path and match its lowercase SHA-256.

## Semantic binding

The released ledger must:

- be strict UTF-8 JSON with no BOM, duplicate keys, non-finite values, surplus fields, or controlled privacy class;
- pass every candidate-ledger schema and causal-distance invariant;
- encode the manually declared experiment queue separately from biological lead status;
- require every row to declare one functional-hit state: `not_tested`, `no_hit`, `full_phenocopy_pending_replication`, `graded_partial_hit`, `mechanism_discordant_hit`, `mixed_or_unsafe`, or `replicated_full_phenocopy`;
- declare `screen_context` as renewable nonparticipant A0, minimal participant A1, or participant-lineage Tier B, without embedding participant identifiers;
- declare `no_hit_basis` separately from the hit state. A0 screen failure, insufficient material, failed controls, or unmeasured exposure cannot be encoded as a participant-branch `no_hit`; a valid no-hit basis must match its A0 or participant context;
- declare `discovery_lane` as targeted-hypothesis or correction-trained phenotypic, and `translation_state` as mechanism-only, context-qualified, or preclinical-replication-ready. The discovery lane is immutable provenance, not an eligibility shortcut or veto: either lane may advance only through the same correction-defined evidence gates, and validation or ranking must not relabel it;
- represent therapeutic context, architecture-lineage state, mitotic-context class, and transformation state as enums plus a dedicated gate. Renewable 2-D surrogate evidence is mechanism-only. Immortalized, reprogrammed, or unknown cultures cannot satisfy postnatal nontransformed context. Context-qualified evidence requires a participant context in a primary-finite postnatal proliferative lineage: epithelial claims require architecture-preserving conditions, while lineage-intrinsic mitotic claims (for example nontransformed blood mitoses) require a matching lineage-intrinsic architecture state rather than a fake organoid; preclinical-replication-ready evidence additionally requires participant-lineage Tier B;
- carry an opaque `replication_design` with different discovery and replication sites, nonempty nonoverlapping edit-event-family sets, frozen protocol, exposure, endpoint, margin, and analysis identity ids, a nullable lower-case SHA-256 analytic-transfer result fingerprint, and explicit site-2 blinding state plus nullable evidence fingerprint before any meaningful functional-hit state in either discovery lane is admitted; and
- expose every correction-defined rescue gate, including measured-exposure validity, analysis lock plus multiplicity control, discovery/confirmation separation, matched-control plus retained-domain safety, separate analytic-transfer and biological-replication pass states, blinded replication execution, and replication provenance. An analytic-transfer pass or failure is valid only with its result fingerprint and complete frozen design. A blinded-execution pass requires analytic transfer to pass, site-2 state `blinded`, and a distinct blinding-evidence fingerprint; `unblinded` maps only to failure. Biological-replication and independent/provenance pass states require the receipt-bound analytic and blinding evidence plus different sites and nonoverlapping edit-event families. No active lead exists unless those receipt-bound conditions, every other gate, independent replication, `replicated_full_phenocopy`, same-lane sample-stewardship held-out and replication completion receipts, and a matching same-lane context-qualification mitotic class and transformation state all pass;
- contain only public or synthetic evidence rows; and
- equal the one normalized, sorted-key, newline-terminated canonical rendering byte for byte.

A `public_software` source cannot mint positive exact-allele, checkpoint, direct-target, or human-PD evidence, cannot occupy lead or conditional-hold advancement, and cannot occupy a pharmacologic ranking role. A software catalog, ranking engine, or predictor object is not a wet assay, is not a conditional probe, and is not a screened pharmacologic comparator. A `public_database` or `public_ontology` source cannot occupy those same pharmacologic roles or lead-advancement states; a database or ontology record is not a screened medicine. Causal axes may still be recorded on mechanistic-control or no-go rows. A `derived_evidence` row must name a non-derived `parent_source_class` and inherits that parent's occupancy and mint bans; literature-derived or manual-review parents remain eligible. Relabeling a catalog, label, database, or ontology row as derived evidence is not a screened medicine. An `official_label` source cannot mint those same positive causal axes or occupy those same advancement states. A labeled indication or labeled PK estimate is not exact-allele function. Official-label comparator and mechanistic-control rows remain eligible. Decision-effect `promote` requires `active_lead` or `conditional_hold`; a parked comparator-only row cannot claim promotion. Lead or conditional-hold advancement cannot carry `demote` or `exclude`. Conditional hold and active lead require a pharmacologic ranking role; a mechanistic-control or no-go row cannot occupy the probe queue. Active lead requires role `lead`; a challenger or comparator cannot hide as the active medicine. A ledger may have at most one role `lead`. A `synthetic_fixture` row cannot be labeled public or controlled. A public catalog, label, database, ontology, or literature row cannot be labeled synthetic or controlled. Oncology-stop, unassessed-oncology, unassessed-exposure, expired-eligibility, or nontranslational-high rows cannot occupy conditional hold or active lead. A `mixed_or_unsafe` functional hit cannot occupy those same advancement states. Role `no_go` and decision-effect `exclude` require advancement `rejected`. Absent or unassessed pediatric information cannot enter the screened displacement set and cannot occupy `active_lead`; conditional hold may still carry those pediatric labels. Immortalized or reprogrammed cultures cannot enter the screened displacement set; conditional hold may still carry those labels as mechanism-only probes. Renewable isogenic A0 material cannot enter the screened displacement set; conditional hold may still carry that label as a mechanism-only probe. Public-literature proof wording cannot count as exact-allele function; literature occupancy remains eligible when causal axes are independently evidenced. Software proof wording cannot count as exact-allele function. Cell-free, ectopic, unmatched, or imposed-stress proof wording cannot count as exact-allele function. Ranking or predictor proof wording cannot count as exact-allele function. Unlabeled, transgene, or overexpression proof wording cannot count as exact-allele function. A mixed-or-unsafe functional hit cannot enter the screened displacement set; a parked comparator-only row may still carry that label. Computational or cDNA proof wording cannot count as exact-allele function. Transient or biophysical proof wording cannot count as exact-allele function. Thermal-shift or purified-protein proof wording cannot count as exact-allele function. Protein-surrogate proof wording cannot count as exact-allele function. Unmatched-line or RNA-seq proof wording cannot count as exact-allele function. Computational-haplotype proof wording cannot count as exact-allele function. A valid no-hit cannot enter the screened displacement set; a parked comparator-only row may still carry that valid negative. A rejected pharmacologic row cannot enter the screened displacement set; comparator-only remaining references stay ranking-eligible. A not-assessable pharmacologic row cannot enter the screened displacement set; comparator-only remaining references stay ranking-eligible.

The released ranking must equal the canonical result recomputed from those exact ledger bytes. It must expose all exposure/regulatory-screened pharmacologic rows, conditional probes, equal causal-score groups, the manual experimental queue, every candidate's immutable discovery lane and functional-hit state, separate `analytic_transfer_pass`, `biological_replication_pass`, and `blinded_replication_execution` states, the three receipt fields from `replication_design`, and per-candidate screening/evidence/rescue and held-out-confirmation blockers. `held_out_confirmation_ids` is restricted to `graded_partial_hit` and `mechanism_discordant_hit` rows in either discovery lane with positive exact-allele and checkpoint evidence, an advancing decision, and an exposure-or-later program gate. They must first pass exact correction rescue and reciprocal recreation, then pass measured-exposure validity, symmetric exposure/regulatory/oncology screening, the pre-locked correction-like functional branch, both native first-division fidelity estimands represented by `native_fidelity`, completion equivalence, clone safety, analysis lock and multiplicity control, discovery/confirmation separation, and matched-control/retained-domain no-harm gates. A graded partial hit requires proximal engagement to pass; a mechanism-discordant hit requires the proposed proximal chain to fail or remain unresolved. A renewable 2-D surrogate hit may enter only this held-out mechanism-only lane; it cannot become a full-phenocopy or active-lead state. Both held-out states require analytic transfer, biological replication, blinded replication execution, independent replication, and replication provenance to remain unattempted, with null result/blinding fingerprints and site-2 state `not_attempted`; a known failed or overlapping replication attempt cannot re-enter this queue. `full_phenocopy_pending_replication` requires preclinical-replication-ready context, every pre-replication gate to pass, and every replication state and receipt to remain unattempted or null. `replicated_full_phenocopy` requires preclinical-replication-ready context, analytic transfer, biological replication, and blinded replication execution separately to pass, every other gate to pass, top-level independent replication to be `replicated`, different sites, held-out nonoverlapping edit-event families, the five frozen identity references, a valid analytic-transfer result SHA-256, site-2 state `blinded`, a distinct valid site-2 blinding-evidence SHA-256, same-lane sample-stewardship held-out and replication completion receipts whose frozen exposure, endpoint, and margin identities match the ledger replication design, and a same-lane completed context-qualification ancestor whose mitotic class and transformation state match the candidate. An active lead additionally requires the exact correction/recreation path in a justified participant-lineage Tier B, postnatal nontransformed proliferative, lineage-matched mitotic context; either discovery lane is eligible and its original provenance label must remain unchanged. Missing, malformed, reused, unblinded, overlapping, or label-inconsistent replication evidence fails closed. Held-out status authorizes only blinded confirmation and mechanism deconvolution, never child-translational status. A role label cannot choose the queue or manufacture an active lead; adverse, excluded, unsafe, early-stage, selection-biased, multiplicity-uncontrolled, discovery-leaking, exposure-invalid, unblinded, replication-inconsistent, surrogate-only, sample-receipt-unbound, context-mismatched, or incompletely gated rows fail closed. Hand editing, whitespace drift, stale candidate ids, changed queue/rescue/exposure/regulatory/oncology/functional-hit fields, or a different decision invalidates the entire manifest. When the manifest is invalid, neither file receives the narrow biology-publication exemption.

When an attempt-2 Track 2 report and the candidate pair are all `released`, the report must carry exactly one of each canonical binding line:

```text
**Candidate evidence ledger SHA-256:** `<exact lowercase ledger SHA-256>`
**Candidate ranking receipt SHA-256:** `<exact lowercase ranking SHA-256>`
```

The privacy gate cross-checks those values against the digest-validated pair. A missing, repeated, or stale line invalidates the whole manifest. A released report may not declare those bindings while the pair remains planned.

The exemption is bound to the exact fixed paths and digests. The scanner rehashes the bytes it actually inspects, so a file changed after manifest validation loses its exception. Pair recomputation uses the same digest-checked snapshot. Copying identical bytes elsewhere remains subject to the ordinary identifier scanner. Secret, controlled-payload, phenotype-bundle, unsafe-path, size, and opaque-file checks are never suppressed.

## Private staging command

Run this only into a new private directory outside the public checkout:

```powershell
python scripts/render_candidate_release_bundle.py `
  --input <reviewed-private-ledger.json> `
  --output-dir <new-private-staging-directory>
```

The stager validates public-only evidence, writes the canonical fixed filenames, verifies the pair, prints content digests, and refuses an existing output directory. It does not edit the release manifest, copy files into `release/`, commit, push, freeze, or upload.

## Future freeze sequence

1. Re-run the bounded evidence search and review every named row and source.
2. Stage the canonical pair privately and review the exact bytes.
3. Put the complete candidate interpretation and limitations in the judged report, including the two exact canonical digest-binding lines.
4. Only during an authorized versioned freeze, copy the reviewed pair to the two fixed release paths and create the attempt-2 release manifest with their exact digests.
5. Bind the intended report, pitch, code, tests, candidate pair, and other receipts to one committed reproducible checkout.
6. Run the complete tests, privacy gate, and new attempt-2 reproducibility verification against those exact bytes.
7. Require separate attempt-specific authorization before any upload.

The current v3 manifest and frozen attempt-1 packet remain authoritative until that sequence is deliberately completed.
