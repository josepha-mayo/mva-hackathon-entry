That is a false-rescue pattern: a bulk or organ-level improvement can be coping, selection, or death-masking rather than fewer new segregation errors. Press language that organ size was restored does not mean the checkpoint was restored, and it does not restore an aneuploid stem-cell count. It is **not** a probe-to-target match for the Track 2 lead, and it does not authorize any administration, dose, or supplement.
